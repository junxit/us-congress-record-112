# Text of Senate Amendment 1770

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
  SA 1770. Mr. SCHUMER (for himself and Mrs. Gillibrand) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill S. 1813, to 
reauthorize Federal-aid highway and highway safety construction 
programs, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       In section 5340(d)(5) of title 49, United States Code, as 
     amended by section 20030, strike the second and third 
     sentences and insert the following: ``Amounts apportioned to 
     each urbanized area shall be added to amounts apportioned to 
     that urbanized area under section 5336 and shall be made 
     available for grants under section 5307.''.
```
