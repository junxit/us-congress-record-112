# Text of Senate Amendment 1767

**Speaking:** Lautenberg, Frank R. (L000123) [D-NJ]

```
  SA 1767. Mr. LAUTENBERG (for himself, Mr. Durbin, Mrs. Gillibrand, 
and Mr. Sanders) submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by him 
to the bill S. 1813, to reauthorize Federal-aid highway and highway 
safety construction programs, and for other purposes; which was ordered 
to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 87, between lines 15 and 16, insert the following:
       ``(29) Capital costs for passenger rail projects eligible
```
