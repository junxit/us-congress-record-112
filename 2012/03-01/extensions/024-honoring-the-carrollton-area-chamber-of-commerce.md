# HONORING THE CARROLLTON AREA CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

**Speaking:** Graves, Sam (G000546) [R-MO]

```
            HONORING THE CARROLLTON AREA CHAMBER OF COMMERCE

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SAM GRAVES

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 1, 2012

  Mr. GRAVES of Missouri. Mr. Speaker, I proudly pause to recognize the 
Carrollton Area Chamber of Commerce and to congratulate them on their 
100th anniversary.
   Since its founding in 1912, the Chamber has been an effective 
advocate for Carrollton and Carroll County. Now numbering 160 members, 
the Chamber remains committed to leading the way for economic 
development and promoting small businesses throughout the Carrollton 
region.
   Mr. Speaker, I proudly ask you to join me in commending the 
Carrollton Area Chamber of Commerce for their accomplishments over the 
past 100 years and in looking forward to the years to come.
```
