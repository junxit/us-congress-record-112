  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, over 2 years have passed since I last 
included the names of our troops who have lost their lives serving in 
support of operations in Iraq and Afghanistan. I wish to honor their 
service and sacrifice by including their names in the Congressional 
Record.

2010, the Pentagon announced the deaths of 1,020 troops in Iraq and in 
Operation Enduring Freedom, which includes Afghanistan. They will not 
be forgotten, and today I ask unanimous consent that their names be 
printed in the Record.
