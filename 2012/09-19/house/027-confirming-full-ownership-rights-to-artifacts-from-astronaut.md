# CONFIRMING FULL OWNERSHIP RIGHTS TO ARTIFACTS FROM ASTRONAUTS' SPACE MISSIONS

```
 CONFIRMING FULL OWNERSHIP RIGHTS TO ARTIFACTS FROM ASTRONAUTS' SPACE 
                                MISSIONS

  Mr. HALL. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the ordering of 
the yeas and nays on the motion that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 4158) to confirm full ownership rights for certain 
United States astronauts to artifacts from the astronauts' space 
missions, be vacated, to the end that the Chair put the question de 
novo.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Texas (Mr. Hall) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill, H.R. 4158.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
