# AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET

```
                    AUTHORITY FOR COMMITTEES TO MEET


                     Committee on Foreign Relations

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Foreign Relations be authorized to meet during the session of the

[[Page 15756]]

Senate on November 28, 2012, at 2 p.m., to hold a nominations hearing.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.


   Subcommittee on International Development and Foreign Assistance, 
      Economic Affairs and International Environmental Protection

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Committee 
on Foreign Relations be authorized to meet during the session of the 
Senate on November 28, 2012, at 10 a.m., to hold an International 
Development and Foreign Assistance, Economic Affairs and International 
Environmental Protection subcommittee hearing entitled, ``Evaluating 
Current U.S. Global Food Security Efforts and Determining Future U.S. 
Leadership Opportunities.''
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
