# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 3001, H.R. 4155

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment bills of the House of the 
following titles:

       H.R. 3001. An act to award a Congressional Gold Medal to 
     Raoul Wallenberg, in recognition of his achievements and 
     heroic actions during the Holocaust.

  H.R. 4155. An act to direct the head of each Federal department and 
agency to treat relevant military training as sufficient to satisfy 
training or certification requirements for Federal licenses.

                          ____________________
```
