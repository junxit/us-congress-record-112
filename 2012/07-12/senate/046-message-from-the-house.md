# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S. 2061, H.R. 6079

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 9:59 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 6079. An act to repeal the Patient Protection and 
     Affordable Care Act and health care-related provisions in the 
     Health Care and Education Reconciliation Act of 2010.


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  The President pro tempore [Mr. Inouye] reported that he had signed 
the following enrolled bill, which was previously signed by the Speaker 
of the House:

       S. 2061. An act to provide for an exchange of land between 
     the Department of Homeland Security and the South Carolina 
     State Ports Authority.
```
