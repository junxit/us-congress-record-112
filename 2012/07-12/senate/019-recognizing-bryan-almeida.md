# RECOGNIZING BRYAN ALMEIDA

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                       RECOGNIZING BRYAN ALMEIDA

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Bryan Almeida, a 
spring press intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff and the people of the State of 
Florida.
  Bryan is a graduate of Belen Jesuit Preparatory School in Miami, FL. 
Currently, he is a sophomore at The George Washington University 
majoring in political communications. He is a dedicated and diligent 
worker who has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Bryan 
for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
