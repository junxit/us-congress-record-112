# RECOGNIZING CHARLES C. DAVIS III

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
                    RECOGNIZING CHARLES C. DAVIS III

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Charles Carlton 
Davis III, a summer intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the 
hard work he has done for me, my staff and the people of the State of 
Florida.
  Chad is a graduate of Jesuit High School in Tampa, FL. Currently he 
is a junior at the University of Florida majoring in political science 
and minoring in history and religion. He is a dedicated and diligent 
worker who has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Charles 
for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●
```
