  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate consider 
the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 261, 338, 339, 340, 665, 678, 
679, 680, 681, 682, 706, 707, 708, 710, 711, 712, 713, 715, 716, 717, 
725, 727 through 757, and 758; and all nominations placed on the 
Secretary's desk in the Air Force, Army, Foreign Service, Marine Corps,

intervening action or debate, and no further motions be in order to any 
of the nominations; that any related statements be printed in the

action and the Senate then resume legislative session.
