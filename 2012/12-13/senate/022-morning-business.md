# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business until 1:45 p.m. with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; further that Senator 
Snowe be recognized at 1 p.m. for up to 45 minutes; finally, at 1:45 
p.m. the Senate proceed to executive session as provided under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
