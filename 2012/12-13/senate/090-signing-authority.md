# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that from Thursday, 
December 13, through Monday, December 17, the majority leader be 
authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
