# TRIBUTE TO JASON SIMMONS

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO JASON SIMMONS

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I wish to recognize Jason 
Simmons, an intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over 
the past several weeks.
  Jason is a graduate of New Underwood High School in New Underwood, 
SD. Currently he is attending the University of South Dakota, where he 
is earning his master of business administration in health services 
administration. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting 
the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Jason 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
