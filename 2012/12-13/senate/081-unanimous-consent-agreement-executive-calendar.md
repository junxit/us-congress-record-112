# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Monday, 
December 17, at 5 p.m., the Senate proceed to executive session to 
consider the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 833 and 875; that 
there be 30 minutes for debate equally divided in the usual form; that 
upon the use or yielding back of that time, the Senate proceed, with no 
intervening action or debate, to vote on Calendar Nos. 833 and 875, in 
that order; that the motions to reconsider be considered made and laid 
upon the table, with no intervening action or debate; that no further 
motions be in order; that any related statements be printed in the 
Record; and that President Obama be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action, and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
