# TRIBUTE TO TYLER STENBERG

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                       TRIBUTE TO TYLER STENBERG

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Tyler Stenberg, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota.
  Tyler is a graduate of Mobridge-Pollock High School in Mobridge, SD. 
Currently, he is attending The University

[[Page S8041]]

of South Dakota, where he is majoring in political science and criminal 
justice. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most 
out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Tyler for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
