# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6664

**Measures:** H.R. 6664

```
           By Mr. CALVERT:
       H.R. 6664.

       The constitutional authority of Congress to enact this

     States Constitution, specifically clause 1 and clause 18, and
```
