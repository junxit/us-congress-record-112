# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 17, 2012

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 17, 2012

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, May 17; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and that the majority leader be recognized.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
