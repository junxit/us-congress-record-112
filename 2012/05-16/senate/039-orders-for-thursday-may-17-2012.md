# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 17, 2012

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 17, 2012

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, May 17; that following the prayer and the

[[Page S3242]]

hour be deemed to have expired, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day, and that the majority leader 
be recognized.
```
