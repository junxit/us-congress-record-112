# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       44. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the City of 
     Lauderdale Lakes, Florida, relative to Resolution No. 2012-38 
     expressing condolences to the family of Trayvon Martin; to 
     the Committee on the Judiciary.
       45. Also, a petition of the Town of New Shoreham, Rhode 
     Island, relative to Resolution condemning in no uncertain 
     terms Section 1021 of the 2012 NDAA; jointly to the 
     Committees on Armed Services and Foreign Affairs.
```
