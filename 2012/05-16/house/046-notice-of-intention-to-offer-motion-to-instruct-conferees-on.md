# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4348, SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012, PART II

```
NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4348, 
         SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012, PART II

  Mr. RAHALL. Madam Speaker, pursuant to rule XXII, clause 7(c), I 
hereby announce my attention to offer a motion to instruct on H.R. 
4348.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mr. Rahall moves that the managers on the part of the House 
     at the conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses 
     on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 4348 be instructed 
     to agree to sections 1528, 20017 (to the extent that such 
     section amends section 5323 of title 49, United States Code, 
     to provide subsection (k) relating to Buy America), 33007, 
     33008, and 35210 of the Senate amendment.

                          ____________________
```
