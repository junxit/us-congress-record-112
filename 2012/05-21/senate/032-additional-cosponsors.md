# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 491

  At the request of Mr. Pryor, the names of the Senator from Iowa (Mr. 
Harkin) and the Senator from Minnesota (Mr. Franken) were added as 
cosponsors of S. 491, a bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
recognize the service in the reserve components of the Armed Forces of 
certain persons by honoring them with status as veterans under law, and 
for other purposes.
```
