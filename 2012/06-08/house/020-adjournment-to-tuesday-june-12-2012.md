# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, JUNE 12, 2012

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

```
                 ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, JUNE 12, 2012

  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that when the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. on 
Tuesday, June 12, 2012; when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn 
to meet at 10 a.m. on Friday, June 15, 2012; and when the House 
adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday, June 18, 
2012.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
