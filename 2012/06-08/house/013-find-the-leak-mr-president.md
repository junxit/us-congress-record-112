# FIND THE LEAK, MR. PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Brooks, Mo (B001274) [R-AL]

```
                              {time}  0910
                      FIND THE LEAK, MR. PRESIDENT

  (Mr. BROOKS asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. BROOKS. Mr. Speaker, today I call on the White House to get off 
the campaign trail, show leadership, do the President's job, and 
aggressively pursue the leakers of America's state secrets. These leaks 
have ranged from implicating the United States in Stuxnet, a computer 
virus that targeted nuclear centrifuges in Iran, to revealing a 
detailed ``kill list'' for terrorists targeted for assassination. Not 
only do these leaks compromise America's efforts to preserve our 
national security, they teach our allies not to trust us. Look at the 
doctor who helped the United States gather DNA evidence to locate Osama 
bin Laden. His reward? His identity was revealed, and he faces 33 years 
in a Pakistan jail.
  Mr. Speaker, where is the outrage from the White House about these 
leaks? It's time for the President to plug the holes and protect 
America's national security.

                          ____________________
```
