# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3830

**Measures:** H.R. 3830

```
            By Mr. ROHRABACHER:
        H.R. 3830.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill follows the Constitutional prerogatives of 
     Congress under Article I, Section 8, pertaining to the 
     clauses to ``to regulate commerce with foreign nations.''
```
