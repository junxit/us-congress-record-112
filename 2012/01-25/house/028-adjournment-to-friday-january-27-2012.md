# ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JANUARY 27, 2012

**Speaking:** McHenry, Patrick T. (M001156) [R-NC]

```
                              {time}  1100
                ADJOURNMENT TO FRIDAY, JANUARY 27, 2012

  Mr. McHENRY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 11 a.m. on Friday, January 27, 
2012, and further when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to 
meet at noon on Tuesday, January 31, 2012, for morning-hour debate and 
2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
```
