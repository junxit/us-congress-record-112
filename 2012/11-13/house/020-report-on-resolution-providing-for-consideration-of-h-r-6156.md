# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6156, RUSSIA AND MOLDOVA JACKSON-VANIK REPEAL AND SERGEI MAGNITSKY RULE OF LAW ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2012

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 6156, RUSSIA 
   AND MOLDOVA JACKSON-VANIK REPEAL AND SERGEI MAGNITSKY RULE OF LAW 
                       ACCOUNTABILITY ACT OF 2012

  Mr. DREIER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 112-693) on the resolution (H. Res. 808) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 6156) to authorize the extension of 
nondiscriminatory treatment (normal trade relations treatment) to 
products of the Russian Federation and Moldova and to require reports 
on the compliance of the Russian Federation with its obligations as a 
member of the World Trade Organization, and for other purposes, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
