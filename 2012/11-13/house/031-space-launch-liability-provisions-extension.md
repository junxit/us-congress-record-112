# SPACE LAUNCH LIABILITY PROVISIONS EXTENSION

```
                              {time}  1920
              SPACE LAUNCH LIABILITY PROVISIONS EXTENSION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Womack). The unfinished business is the 
question on suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 6586) to 
extend the application of certain space launch liability provisions 
through 2014.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Mississippi (Mr. Palazzo) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
