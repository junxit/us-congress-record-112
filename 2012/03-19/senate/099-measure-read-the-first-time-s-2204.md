# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2204

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2204

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2204

  Mr. REID. Madam President, S. 2204 is at the desk and due for its 
first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2204) to eliminate unnecessary tax subsidies and
```
