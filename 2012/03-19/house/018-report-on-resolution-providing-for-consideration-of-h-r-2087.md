# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2087, REMOVING RESTRICTIONS FOR ACCOMACK COUNTY LAND PARCEL

**Measures:** H.Res. 587, H.R. 2087

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2087, REMOVING 
              RESTRICTIONS FOR ACCOMACK COUNTY LAND PARCEL

  Mr. BISHOP of Utah, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 112 415) on the resolution (H. Res. 587) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2087) to remove 
restrictions from a parcel of land situated in the Atlantic District, 
Accomack County, Virginia, which was referred to the House Calendar and
```
