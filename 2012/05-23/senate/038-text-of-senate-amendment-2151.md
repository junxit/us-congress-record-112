# Text of Senate Amendment 2151

**Speaking:** Manchin, Joe, III (M001183) [D-WV]

```
  SA 2151. Mr. MANCHIN (for himself, Mr. Kirk, Mrs. Gillibrand, Mr. 
Schumer, and Mr. Rockefeller) submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by him to the bill S. 3187, to amend the Federal Food, Drug, 
and Cosmetic Act to revise and extend the user-fee programs for 
prescription drugs and medical devices, to establish user-fee programs 
for generic drugs and biosimilars, and for other purposes; as follows:

       At the end of subtitle C of title XI, add the following:

     SEC. 1132. HYDROCODONE AMENDMENT.

       Schedule III(d) in section 202(c) of the Controlled 
     Substances Act (21 U.S.C. 812(c)) is amended by--
       (1) striking paragraphs (3) and (4); and
       (2) redesignating paragraphs (5), (6), (7), and (8) as 
     paragraphs (3), (4), (5), and (6), respectively.
                                 ______
```
