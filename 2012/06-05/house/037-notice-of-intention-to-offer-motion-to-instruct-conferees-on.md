# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4348, SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012, PART II

```
                              {time}  1920
NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4348, 
         SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012, PART II

  Mr. DOGGETT. Mr. Speaker, under rule XXII, clause 7(c), I hereby 
announce my intention to offer a motion to instruct on H.R. 4348, the 
transportation conference report.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mr. Doggett moves that the managers on the part of the 
     House at the conference on the disagreeing votes of the two 
     Houses on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 4348 be 
     instructed to recede from disagreement with the provisions 
     contained in section 100201 of the Senate amendment (relating 
     to stop tax haven abuse--authorizing special measures against 
     foreign jurisdictions, financial institutions, and others 
     that significantly impede United States tax enforcement).

                          ____________________
```
