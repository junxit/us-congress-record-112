# Text of House Amendment 19

**Speaking:** Lummis, Cynthia M. (L000571) [R-WY]

**Measures:** H.R. 5325

```
H.R. 5325

                        Offered By: Mrs. Lummis

       Amendment No. 19: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used to plan or undertake sales, trades, barters, or 
     transfers of uranium from the Department of Energy in total 
     amounts that in fiscal year 2013 exceed 1,917 metric tons of 
     uranium as uranium hexafluoride equivalent.
```
