# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 26, 2012

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI]

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 26, 2012

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, July 26; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that the majority leader be recognized, and that the first hour be 
equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the majority controlling the first half and the 
Republicans controlling the final half.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
