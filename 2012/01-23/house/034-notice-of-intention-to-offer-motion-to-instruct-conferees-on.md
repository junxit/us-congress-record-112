# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 3630, TEMPORARY PAYROLL TAX CUT CONTINUATION ACT OF 2011

```
NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 3630, 
           TEMPORARY PAYROLL TAX CUT CONTINUATION ACT OF 2011

  Mrs. CAPPS. Mr. Speaker, under rule XXII, clause 7(c), I hereby 
announce my intention to offer a motion to instruct on H.R. 3630, the 
conference report to extend payroll tax, unemployment insurance, and 
sustainable growth rate payments for doctors.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mrs. Capps moves that the managers on the part of the House 
     at the conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses 
     on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 3630 be instructed 
     to file a conference report not later than February 17, 2012.

                          ____________________
```
