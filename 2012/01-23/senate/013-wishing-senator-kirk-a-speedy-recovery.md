# WISHING SENATOR KIRK A SPEEDY RECOVERY

```
                 WISHING SENATOR KIRK A SPEEDY RECOVERY

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, let me start on a sort of sober note by 
saying we are all thinking of our colleague Mark Kirk. It is at moments 
such as these that we are all reminded of how fragile life is, and that 
there are far more important things in life than politics. So we send 
Mark and his family our prayers and our wishes for a speedy recovery.

                          ____________________
```
