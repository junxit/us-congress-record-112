# PROVIDING FOR A JOINT SESSION OF CONGRESS TO RECEIVE A MESSAGE FROM THE PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 96

```
PROVIDING FOR A JOINT SESSION OF CONGRESS TO RECEIVE A MESSAGE FROM THE 
                               PRESIDENT

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of H. Con. Res. 96, which was received 
from the House and is at the desk; that the concurrent resolution be 
agreed to and the motion to reconsider be laid upon the table, with no 
intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 96) was agreed to.

                          ____________________
```
