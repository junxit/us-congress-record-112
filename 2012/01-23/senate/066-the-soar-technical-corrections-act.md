# THE SOAR TECHNICAL CORRECTIONS ACT

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 3237

```
                   THE SOAR TECHNICAL CORRECTIONS ACT

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Health, 
Education, Labor, and Pensions Committee be discharged from further 
consideration of H.R. 3237 and the Senate proceed to its immediate 
consideration.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3237) to amend the SOAR Act by clarifying the 
     scope of coverage of the Act.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read a third time and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, with no intervening action or debate, and any statements related 
to the bill be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 3237) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
