# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 3 p.m. today.
  Following morning business, we will begin consideration of H.R. 1, 
the legislative vehicle for the supplemental appropriations bill. That 
bill will be managed by Senator Pat Leahy.
  At 5 p.m. the Senate will proceed to executive session to consider 
the Olguin and Durkin nominations, both to be U.S. district judges of 
Illinois and California, respectively.
  At 5:30 p.m., the Senate will vote on confirmation of the 
nominations. It is expected there will be a rollcall vote on the 
confirmation of the Durkin nomination and a voice vote on the 
confirmation of the Olguin nomination.

                          ____________________
```
