# SENATE RESOLUTION 620--TO AUTHORIZE HARRY REID, A SENATOR FROM THE STATE OF NEVADA, TO ADMINISTER THE OATH OF OFFICE OF PRESIDENT OF THE SENATE PRO TEMPORE TO PATRICK J. LEAHY, A SENATOR FROM THE STATE OF VERMONT

```
  SENATE RESOLUTION 620--TO AUTHORIZE HARRY REID, A SENATOR FROM THE 
 STATE OF NEVADA, TO ADMINISTER THE OATH OF OFFICE OF PRESIDENT OF THE 
  SENATE PRO TEMPORE TO PATRICK J. LEAHY, A SENATOR FROM THE STATE OF 
                                VERMONT

  Mr. McCONNELL (for himself and Mr. Durbin) submitted the following 
resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 620

       Resolved, That Harry Reid, a Senator from the State of 
     Nevada, is hereby authorized to administer the oath of office 
     of President of the Senate Pro Tempore to Patrick J. Leahy, a 
     Senator from the State of Vermont.
```
