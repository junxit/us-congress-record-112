# HONORING SAM CRANE

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
                           HONORING SAM CRANE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 29, 2012

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Sam Crane of the Rock Bridge High School 
Bruins Wrestling team for winning the Class 4 Missouri State Wrestling 
Championship in the 132-pound weight class.
  Mr. Crane and his coaches should be commended for all their hard work 
throughout the regular season and for bringing home the state title in 
the Class 4 132-pound weight class to his school and community. His win 
represents the school's first state champion in Class 4 since 1997.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing Mr. Crane for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
