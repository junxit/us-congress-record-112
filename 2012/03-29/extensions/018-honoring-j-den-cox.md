# HONORING J'DEN COX

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
                           HONORING J'DEN COX

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 29, 2012

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating J'den Cox of the Hickman High School 
Wrestling team for winning the Class 4 Missouri State Wrestling 
Championship in the 220-pound weight class.
  Mr. Cox and his coaches should be commended for all their hard work 
throughout the regular season and for bringing home the state title in 
the 220-pound weight class to his school and community. This is his 
third state championship. He is the Tribune Wrestler of the Year with 
his unofficially compiled 526 takedowns.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing Mr. Cox for a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
