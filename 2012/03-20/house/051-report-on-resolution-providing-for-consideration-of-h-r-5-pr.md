# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5, PROTECTING ACCESS TO HEALTHCARE ACT

**Measures:** H.R. 5, H.Res. 591

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5, PROTECTING 
                        ACCESS TO HEALTHCARE ACT

  Mr. Nugent, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 112 416) on the resolution (H. Res. 591) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 5) to improve patient access to 
health care services and provide improved medical care by reducing the 
excessive burden the liability system places on the health care 
delivery system, which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered 
to be printed.
```
