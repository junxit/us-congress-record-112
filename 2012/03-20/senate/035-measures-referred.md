# MEASURES REFERRED

**Measures:** H.R. 3992, H.R. 4086

```
                           MEASURES REFERRED

  The following bills were read the first and the second times by 
unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

       H.R. 3992. An act to allow otherwise eligible Israeli 
     nationals to receive E-2 nonimmigrant visas if similarly 
     situated United States nationals are eligible for similar 
     nonimmigrant status in Israel; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
       H.R. 4086. An act to amend chapter 97 of title 28, United 
     States Code, to clarify the exception to foreign sovereign 
     immunity set forth in section 1605(a)(3) of such title; to 
     the Committee on the Judiciary.

                          ____________________
```
