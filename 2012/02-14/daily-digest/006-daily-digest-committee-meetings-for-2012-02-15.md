                   COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR WEDNESDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry: Business 
meeting to consider the nominations of Michael T. Scuse, of 
Delaware, to be Under Secretary for Farm and Foreign Agricultural 
Services, and to be a Member of the Board of Directors of the 
Commodity Credit Corporation, Department of Agriculture, and Chester

large financial institutions, 2 p.m., SD-538.

President's proposed budget request for fiscal year 2013 for the 
Department of Transportation, 10 a.m., SD-608.
  Committee on Finance: To continue hearings to examine the 
President's proposed budget request for fiscal year 2013, 10 a.m., 
SD-215.
  Committee on the Judiciary: To hold hearings to examine the

United States District Judge for the District of South Carolina, and 
Timothy S. Hillman, to be United States District Judge for the 
District of Massachusetts, 2:30 p.m., SD-226.
