# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 18, 2012

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, JUNE 18, 2012

  Mr. FRANKEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 3 p.m., Monday, 
June 18; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that the majority leader be recognized, and that at 5 
p.m. the Senate proceed to executive session under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
