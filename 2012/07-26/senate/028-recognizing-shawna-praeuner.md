# RECOGNIZING SHAWNA PRAEUNER

```
                      RECOGNIZING SHAWNA PRAEUNER

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Shawna Praeuner for her hard 
work as an intern in my Cheyenne office. I recognize her efforts and 
contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Shawna is a native of Newcastle, WY and a graduate of Newcastle High 
School. She recently graduated from the University of Wyoming, where 
she is majored in Agricultural Communications and Marketing. She has 
demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made her an invaluable 
asset to our office. The quality of her work is reflected in her great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I thank Shawna for the dedication she has shown while working for me 
and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of our team. I know 
she will have continued success with all of her future endeavors. I 
wish her all my best on her next journey.●
```
