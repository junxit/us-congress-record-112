# CONGRATULATORY REMARKS FOR OBTAINING THE RANK OF EAGLE SCOUT

```
      CONGRATULATORY REMARKS FOR OBTAINING THE RANK OF EAGLE SCOUT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SANDY ADAMS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 26, 2012

  Mrs. ADAMS. Mr. Speaker, I would like to congratulate Matthew 
Winchester for achieving the rank of Eagle Scout.
  For his Eagle Scout project, Matthew provided several tetherball sets 
to benefit a local summer youth camp. Throughout the history of the Boy 
Scouts of America, the rank of Eagle Scout has only been attained 
through dedication to concepts such as honor, duty, country and 
charity. By applying these concepts to daily life, Matthew has proven 
his true and complete understanding of their meanings, and thereby 
deserves this honor.
  I offer my congratulations on a job well done and best wishes for the 
future.

                          ____________________
```
