# MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR THE 1972 ISRAELI OLYMPIANS

```
            MOMENT OF SILENCE FOR THE 1972 ISRAELI OLYMPIANS

  (Mrs. LOWEY asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)

silence for the Munich 11 who lost their lives at the 1972 Olympic
```
