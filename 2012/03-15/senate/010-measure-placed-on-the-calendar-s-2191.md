# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2191

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2191

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 2191

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I am told that S. 2191 is due for a second 
reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2191) to amend the Americans with Disabilities 
     Act of 1990 to prohibit the attorney general from 
     administering or enforcing certain accessibility regulations 
     relating to pools at public accommodations or provided by 
     public entities.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I would object to further proceedings on 
this matter at this time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The bill will be placed on the 
calendar.

                          ____________________
```
