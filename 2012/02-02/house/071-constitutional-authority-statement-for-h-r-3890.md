# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3890

**Measures:** H.R. 3890

```
           By Mr. DANIEL E. LUNGREN of California:
       H.R. 3890.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Emergency Judicial Relief Act of 2012 is authorized by 
     Article 1 Section 8 to constitute Tribunals inferior to the 
     Supreme Court.
```
