# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** H.R. 4281

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

following enrolled bill was signed by Speaker pro tempore Thornberry on
```
