# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6357

**Measures:** H.R. 6357

```
           By Mr. KUCINICH:
       H.R. 6357.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:

[[Page H5717]]

       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     Article XIV of the Constitution, which enumerates that no 
     United States Citizen can be ``deprived of life, liberty, 
     property, without due process of law.'' The bill will assert 
     Congress' responsibility to defend the rights included in the 
     Bill of Rights of the Constitution.
```
