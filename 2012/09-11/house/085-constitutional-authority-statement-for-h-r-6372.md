# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6372

**Measures:** H.R. 6372

```
            By Mr. COFFMAN of Colorado:
       H.R. 6372.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 12, 13, and 14 of the United 
     States Constitution reserves to Congress the power to raise 
     and support Armies, to provide and maintain a Navy, and to 
     make Rules for the Government and Regulation of the land and 
     naval Forces.
```
