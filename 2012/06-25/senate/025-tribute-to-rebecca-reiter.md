# TRIBUTE TO REBECCA REITER

```
                       TRIBUTE TO REBECCA REITER

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Rebecca Reiter, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Rebecca is a graduate of Watertown High School in Watertown, SD. 
Currently, she is attending the University of South Dakota, where she 
is majoring in political science and criminal justice. She is a hard 
worker who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Rebecca for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
