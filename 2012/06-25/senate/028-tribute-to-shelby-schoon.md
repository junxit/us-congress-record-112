# TRIBUTE TO SHELBY SCHOON

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO SHELBY SCHOON

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Shelby Schoon, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Shelby was home schooled and is a native of Brandon, SD. Currently, 
she is a graduate of Northwestern College where she majored in business 
administration and biology health professionals. She is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Shelby for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
