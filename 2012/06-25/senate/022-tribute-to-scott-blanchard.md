# TRIBUTE TO SCOTT BLANCHARD

```
                       TRIBUTE TO SCOTT BLANCHARD

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Scott Blanchard, 
an intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Scott is a graduate of Aberdeen Central High School in Aberdeen, SD. 
Currently, he is attending Northern State University, where he is 
majoring in political science. He is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Scott for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
