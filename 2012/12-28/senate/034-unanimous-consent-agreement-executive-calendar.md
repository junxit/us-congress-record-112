# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following 
leader remarks on Sunday, December 30, the Senate proceed to executive 
session to consider Calendar No. 518 under the previous order; further, 
that after the use or yielding back of the time, we proceed to 
consideration of Calendar No. 909 and proceed to vote on Calendar No. 
909 and Calendar No. 518; that there be 2 minutes for debate equally 
divided in the usual form prior to each vote; that the motions to 
reconsider be considered made and laid on the table; that there being 
no intervening action or debate and no further motions be in order to 
the nomination; that any statements related to the nominations be 
printed in the Record, President Obama be immediately notified of the 
Senate's action, and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
