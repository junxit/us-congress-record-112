# ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, DECEMBER 30, 2012

```
                  ORDERS FOR SUNDAY, DECEMBER 30, 2012

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 1 p.m. on Sunday, 
December 30, 2012; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive 
session under the previous order; and that following disposition of the 
Galante nomination, the Senate recess for 1 hour to allow for caucus 
meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
