# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4093

**Measures:** H.R. 4093

```
           By Mr. CHAFFETZ:
       H.R. 4093.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This law is enacted pursuant to Article I Section 8 Clause 
     18 of the United States Constitution which states: The 
     Congress shall have Power To make all Laws which shall be 
     necessary and proper for carrying into Execution the 
     foregoing Powers, and all other Powers vested by the 
     Constitution in the Government of the United States, or in 
     any Department or Officer thereof.
```
