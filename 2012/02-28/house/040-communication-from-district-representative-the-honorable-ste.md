# COMMUNICATION FROM DISTRICT REPRESENTATIVE, THE HONORABLE STEVE KING, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

```
 COMMUNICATION FROM DISTRICT REPRESENTATIVE, THE HONORABLE STEVE KING, 
                           MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Sandra Hanlon, District Representative, the 
Honorable Steve King, Member of Congress:

                                                February 24, 2012.
     Hon. John A. Boehner,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker, this is to notify you formally, pursuant 
     to rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I have been served with a trial subpoena ad 
     testificandum issued by the United States District Court for 
     the Northern District of Iowa.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     will determine whether compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,

                                                Sandra Hanlon,

                                          District Representative,
     Congressman Steve King.

                          ____________________
```
