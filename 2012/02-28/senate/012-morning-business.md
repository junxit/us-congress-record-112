# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business until the hour of 12:30 p.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, 
with the time equally divided and controlled by the leaders or their 
designees, with the majority controlling the first hour and the 
Republicans the second hour.
  The Senator from Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
