# Text of House Amendment 3

**Speaking:** Quayle, Benjamin (Q000024) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 5854

```
H.R. 5854

                         Offered By: Mr. Quayle

       Amendment No. 3: Page 66, after line 10, add the following 
     new section:
       Sec. 519.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to implement, administer, or enforce the final rule 
     published by the National Labor Relations Board in the 
     Federal Register on August 30, 2011 (76 Fed. Reg. 54006).
```
