# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Poe, Ted (P000592) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 5854

```
H.R. 5854

                      Offered By: Mr. Poe of Texas

       Amendment No. 5: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to censor or otherwise limit the speech of a veterans 
     service organization participating in the funeral or memorial 
     service of a veteran.
```
