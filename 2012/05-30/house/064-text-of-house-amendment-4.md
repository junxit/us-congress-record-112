# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Poe, Ted (P000592) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 5854

```
H.R. 5854

                      Offered By: Mr. Poe of Texas

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used for a director of a national cemetery who, on or 
     after the date that is 180 days after the date of the 
     enactment of this Act, is not a veteran.
```
