# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, MAY 22, 2012

**Speaking:** Bishop, Rob (B001250) [R-UT]

```
                  ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, MAY 22, 2012

  Mr. BISHOP of Utah. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. on Tuesday, May 
22, 2012; when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 10 
a.m. on Friday, May 25, 2012; when the House adjourns on that day, it 
adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Tuesday, May 29, 2012; and when the House 
adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Wednesday, May 
30, 2012.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Utah?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
