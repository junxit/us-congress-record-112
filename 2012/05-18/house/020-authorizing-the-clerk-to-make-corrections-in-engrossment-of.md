**Speaking:** McKeon, Howard P. "Buck" (M000508) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4310

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 4310, 
        NATIONAL DEFENSE AUTHORIZATION ACT FOR FISCAL YEAR 2013

  Mr. McKEON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that in the 
engrossment of the bill, H.R. 4310, the Clerk be authorized to correct

bill.
```
