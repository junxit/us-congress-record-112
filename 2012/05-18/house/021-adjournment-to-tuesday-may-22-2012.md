# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, MAY 22, 2012

                  ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, MAY 22, 2012

  Mr. BISHOP of Utah. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when

22, 2012; when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 10

adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Wednesday, May 
30, 2012.

gentleman from Utah?
  There was no objection.
