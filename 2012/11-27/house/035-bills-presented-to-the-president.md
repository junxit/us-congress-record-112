# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on November 16, 
2012, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval the following bills.

       H.R. 2606. To authorize the Secretary of the Interior to 
     allow the construction and operation of natural gas pipeline 
     facilities in the Gateway National Recreation Area, and for 
     other purposes.
       H.R. 4114. To increase, effective as of December 1, 2012,

     disabilities and the rates of dependency and indemnity 
     compensation for the survivors of certain disabled veterans,
```
