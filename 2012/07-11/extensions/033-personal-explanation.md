# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Perlmutter, Ed (P000593) [D-CO]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ED PERLMUTTER

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 11, 2012

  Mr. PERLMUTTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to correct an error I made in 
voting recently.
  Rollcall vote 430, the twelfth Broun amendment, proposed to reduce 
the Public Housing Operating fund by $56 million. Because that 
particular series of votes included six successive amendments by 
Representative Broun of Georgia, and these votes were shortened to two 
minutes in length, I mistakenly lost track of the order of amendments 
and voted ``yes'' on rollcall 430. I ask that the Record reflect I 
intended to vote ``no'' on rollcall 430.

                          ____________________
```
