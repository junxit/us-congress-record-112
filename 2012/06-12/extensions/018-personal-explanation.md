# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Denham, Jeff (D000612) [R-CA]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JEFF DENHAM

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 12, 2012

  Mr. DENHAM. Mr. Speaker, on rollcall No. 371, I was unable to cast a 
vote on the Gosar amendment to the Legislative Branch Appropriations 
bill for fiscal year 2013 (H.R. 5882) because I was unavoidably 
detained at a memorial for a fallen peace officer.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``aye'' on rollcall No. 371, 
to reduce funding for the U.S. Botanical Gardens by $1.235 million.
```
