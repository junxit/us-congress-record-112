# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 13, 2012

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 13, 2012

  Mr. BENNET. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, June 13; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that the majority leader be recognized; and that following any 
leader remarks the first hour be equally divided and controlled between 
the two leaders or their designees with the Republicans controlling the 
first half and the majority controlling the final half.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
