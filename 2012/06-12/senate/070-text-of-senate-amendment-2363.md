# Text of Senate Amendment 2363

**Speaking:** Vitter, David (V000127) [R-LA]

```
  SA 2363. Mr. VITTER submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 3240, to reauthorize agricultural programs through 
2017, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       On page 1009, after line 11, add the following:

     SEC. 12207. ANIMAL WELFARE.

       Section 2(h) of the Animal Welfare Act (7 U.S.C. 2132(h)) 
     is amended by adding ``an

[[Page S3992]]

     owner of a common, domesticated household pet who derives 
     less than a substantial portion of income from a nonprimary 
     source (as determined by the Secretary) for exhibiting an 
     animal that exclusively resides at the residence of the pet 
     owner,'' after ``stores,''.
                                 ______
```
