# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 2947, H.R. 3992, H.R. 4097

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D579)
  H.R. 2947, to provide for the release of the reversionary interest 
held by the United States in certain land conveyed by the United States 
in 1950 for the establishment of an airport in Cook County, Minnesota.

are eligible for similar nonimmigrant status in Israel. Signed on June 
8, 2012. (Public Law 112-130)
  H.R. 4097, to amend the John F. Kennedy Center Act to authorize 
appropriations for the John F. Kennedy Center for the Performing Arts. 
Signed on June 8, 2012. (Public Law 112-131)
```
