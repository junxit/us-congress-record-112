# Text of House Amendment 12

**Speaking:** Tipton, Scott  R. (T000470) [R-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 5325

```
H.R. 5325

                         Offered By: Mr. Tipton

       Amendment No. 12: At the end of the bill (before the short
```
