# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Harris, Andy (H001052) [R-MD]

**Measures:** H.R. 5325

```
H.R. 5325

                         Offered by: Mr. Harris

       Amendment No. 18: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used to fund any portion of the International program 
     activities at the Office of the Department of Energy with the 
     exception of the activities authorized in section 917 of the 
     Energy Independence and Security Act of 2007 (42 U.S.C. 
     17337).
```
