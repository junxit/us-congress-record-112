  Mr. CONNOLLY of Virginia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
BEACON for Adult Literacy Program Volunteers.
  BEACON for Adult Literacy is an award-winning non-profit proudly 
serving the adult literacy needs of the Northern Virginia community for 
20 years. In its first year BEACON served 30 learners; today BEACON 
serves over 400 adults a year. Over 3,500 low-income non-native 
Americans have gained English-language literacy skills over the past 
two decades.
