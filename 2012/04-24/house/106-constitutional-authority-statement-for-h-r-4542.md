# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4542

**Measures:** H.R. 4542

```
            By Mr. JONES:
        H.R. 4542.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution, which gives 
     Congress the ``Power to lay and collect Taxes, Duties, 
     Imposts and Excises,'' and to ``regulate Commerce with 
     foreign nations''.
```
