# Text of Senate Amendment 3384

**Speaking:** McCain, John (M000303) [R-AZ]

```
  SA 3384. Mr. McCAIN (for himself and Mr. Coburn) submitted an 
amendment intended to be proposed by him to the bill H.R. 1, making 
appropriations for the Department of Defense and the other departments 
and agencies of the Government for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
2011, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as 
follows:

       On page 82, lines 21 and 22, strike ``to remain available 
     until expended: Provided,'' and insert ``to remain available 
     until the earlier of the date on which such funds are 
     expended or the date that is 2 years after the date of the 
     enactment of this Act: Provided, That any funding provided 
     under this heading that remains available for obligation or 
     has been obligated but not yet spent as of the date that is 2 
     years after the date of the enactment of this Act shall be 
     rescinded and returned to the Treasury for deficit reduction: 
     Provided further, That none of the funds provided under this 
     heading may be distributed until the National Railroad 
     Passenger Corporation submits a detailed plan to Congress 
     pertaining to each project or program that describes how such 
     funds will be expended: Provided further, That none of the 
     funds provided under this heading may be used for capital 
     improvements or other expenses that are not directly 
     associated with Hurricane Sandy or Tropical Storm Sandy: 
     Provided further,''.
                                 ______
```
