                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the President pro 
tempore, pursuant to Public Law 106-398, as amended by Public Law 108-
7, and upon the recommendation of the Republican leader, in 
consultation with the Ranking Members of the Senate Committee on Armed 
Services and the Senate Committee on Finance, appoints the following

31, 2014, Dennis C. Shea of Virginia for a term expiring December 31, 
2014, and James M. Talent of Missouri, for a term expiring December 31, 
2013.

Law 106-398, as amended by Public Law 108-7, and upon the 
recommendation of the Majority Leader, in consultation with the 
Chairmen of the Senate Committee on Armed Services and the Senate 
Committee on Finance, appoints the following individual to the United 
States-China Economic Security Review Commission: Katherine Tobin of 
Virginia for a term beginning January 1, 2013 and expiring December 31, 
2014.
