# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 20, 2012

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 20, 2012

  Mr. MERKLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 11 a.m. on 
Thursday, December 20, 2012; that following the prayer and the pledge, 
the Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the

[[Page 17922]]

morning hour be deemed expired, and the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day; that following any leader 
remarks, the Senate resume consideration of H.R. 1, the legislative 
vehicle for the emergency supplemental appropriations bill.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
