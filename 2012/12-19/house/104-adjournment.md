# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Sessions, Pete (S000250) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.Res. 839

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 10 o'clock and 21 minutes 
p.m.), under its previous order and pursuant to House Resolution 839, 
the House adjourned until tomorrow, Thursday, December 20, 2012, at 
noon, as a further mark of respect to the memory of the late Honorable 
Daniel K. Inouye.
```
