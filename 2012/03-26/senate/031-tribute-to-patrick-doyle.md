# TRIBUTE TO PATRICK DOYLE

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         ADDITIONAL STATEMENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                        TRIBUTE TO PATRICK DOYLE

● Mr. THUNE. Madam President, today I recognize Patrick Doyle, 
an intern in my Rapid City, SD, office for all of the

[[Page S2030]]

hard work he has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota 
over the past few months.
  Patrick is a graduate of Stevens High School in Rapid City, SD. 
Currently, he is attending the University of South Dakota, where he is 
majoring in political science and history. He is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Patrick 
for all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
