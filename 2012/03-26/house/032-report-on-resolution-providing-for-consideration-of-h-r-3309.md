# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3309, FEDERAL COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION PROCESS REFORM ACT OF 2012

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3309, FEDERAL 
          COMMUNICATIONS COMMISSION PROCESS REFORM ACT OF 2012

  Mr. WEBSTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 112-422) on the resolution (H. Res. 595) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3309) to amend the Communications 
Act of 1934 to provide for greater transparency and efficiency in the 
procedures followed by the Federal Communications Commission, which was 
referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
