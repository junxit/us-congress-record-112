# CONGRATULATORY REMARKS FOR OBTAINING THE RANK OF EAGLE SCOUT--ADAM HAWKES

```
  CONGRATULATORY REMARKS FOR OBTAINING THE RANK OF EAGLE SCOUT--ADAM 
                                 HAWKES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SANDY ADAMS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, September 10, 2012

  Mrs. ADAMS. Mr. Speaker, I would like to congratulate Adam Hawkes for 
achieving the rank of Eagle Scout.
  For his Eagle Scout project, Adam constructed picnic tables for a 
local soup kitchen. Throughout the history of the Boy Scouts of 
America, the rank of Eagle Scout has only been attained through 
dedication to concepts such as honor, duty, country and charity. By 
applying these concepts to daily life, Adam has proven his true and 
complete understanding of their meanings, and thereby deserves this 
honor.
  I offer my congratulations on a job well done and best wishes for the 
future.

                          ____________________
```
