              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. MENENDEZ (for himself, Mrs. Boxer, Mrs.

           By Mr. SCHUMER (for himself, Mr. Hatch, Mrs.

     extend protection to fashion design, and for other purposes;

     purposes; from the Committee on Finance; placed on the 
     calendar.
           By Mr. TESTER:
       S. 3525. A bill to protect and enhance opportunities for 
     recreational hunting, fishing, and shooting, and for other
