# MEASURE READ THE 1ST TIME--S. 2191

```
                   MEASURE READ THE 1ST TIME--S. 2191

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I am told there is a bill at the desk due 
for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will read the bill by title for the 
first time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2191) to amend the Americans with Disabilities 
     Act of 1990 to prohibit the Attorney General from 
     administering or enforcing certain accessibility regulations 
     relating to pools at public accommodations or provided by 
     public entities.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now ask for a second reading and, for 
purposes of placing the bill on the calendar under the provisions of 
rule XIV, I object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The bill will be read for 
a second time on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
