# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS

```
           UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE NOMINATIONS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Thursday, 
March 15, 2012, at 1:45 p.m., the Senate proceed to executive session 
to consider the following nominations: Calendar Nos. 408 and 461; that 
there be 15 minutes for debate equally divided in the usual form; that 
upon the use or yielding back of the time, the Senate proceed to vote, 
without intervening action or debate, on Calendar No. 408 and No. 461, 
in that order; that the motions to reconsider be considered made and 
laid upon the table, with no intervening action or debate; that no 
further motions be in order; that any related statements be printed in 
the Record; that the President be immediately notified of the Senate's 
action and the Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
