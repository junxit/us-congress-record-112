# COMPLIMENTING SENATOR LANDRIEU

**Speaking:** Schumer, Charles E. (S000148) [D-NY]

```
                     COMPLIMENTING SENATOR LANDRIEU

  Mr. SCHUMER. Mr. President, first, let me compliment my colleague 
from Louisiana for her diligence, her hard work. I don't think anybody 
effectively delivers more for her State in this Chamber than the 
Senator from Louisiana. I can assure you, knowing her, now that she has 
done this, she will have another proposal and she will be talking to us

charm and many other good qualities, she never wears out her welcome, 
at least with the Senator from New York.
```
