# Text of House Amendment 26

**Speaking:** LoBiondo, Frank A. (L000554) [R-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 5856

```
H.R. 5856

                        Offered By: Mr. LoBiondo

       Amendment No. 26: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
       Sec. ___.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to operate an unmanned aircraft system except in 
     accordance with the Fourth Amendment of the Constitution.
```
