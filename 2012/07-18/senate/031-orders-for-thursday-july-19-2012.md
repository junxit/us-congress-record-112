# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 19, 2012

```
                   ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, JULY 19, 2012

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Thursday, 
July 19; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that the majority leader be recognized and the first 
hour be equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half and the 
majority controlling the final half; further, that the cloture vote on 
the motion to proceed to S. 3364, the Bring Jobs Home Act, be at 2:15 
p.m. tomorrow.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Begich). Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
