                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 3401

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I understand S. 3401 is due for its first 
reading.

first time.
  The assistant bill clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 3401) to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986

     and 2003, to
