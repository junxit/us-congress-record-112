# Text of Senate Amendment 2557

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
  SA 2557. Mrs. HUTCHISON submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by her to the bill S. 3364, to provide an incentive for 
businesses to bring jobs back to America; which was ordered to lie on 
the table; as follows:

       At the end, add the following:

     SEC. _. REPEAL OF SUNSET ON MARRIAGE PENALTY RELIEF.

       Title IX of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief 
     Reconciliation Act of 2001 (relating to sunset of provisions 
     of such Act) shall not apply to sections 301, 302, and 303(a) 
     of such Act (relating to marriage penalty relief).
                                 ______
```
