# SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

```
            SUBMISSION OF CONCURRENT AND SENATE RESOLUTIONS

  The following concurrent resolutions and Senate resolutions were

           By Mrs. MURRAY (for herself and Mr. Burr):

             Isakson, Mr. Toomey, Mr. Blunt, Mr. Cochran, Mr. 
             Cornyn, Ms. Klobuchar, Mr. Schumer, Mr. Burr, Mr. 
             Franken, Mr. Kyl, Mr. Johnson of South Dakota, Mr. 
             Barrasso, Mr. Blumenthal, Mr. Heller, Mr. Brown of 
             Ohio, Mr. Johanns, Mr. Manchin, Mrs. Hagan, Mr.
```
