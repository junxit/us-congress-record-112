# ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

**Measures:** H.R. 2606, H.R. 4114

```
                         ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly enrolled 
bills of the House of the following titles, which were thereupon signed 
by the Speaker:

       H.R. 2606. An act to authorize the Secretary of the 
     Interior to allow the construction and operation of natural 
     gas pipeline facilities in the Gateway National Recreation 
     Area, and for other purposes.
       H.R. 4114. An act to increase, effective as of December 1, 
     2012, the rates of compensation for veterans with service-
     connected disabilities and the rates of dependency and 
     indemnity compensation for the survivors of certain disabled 
     veterans, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
