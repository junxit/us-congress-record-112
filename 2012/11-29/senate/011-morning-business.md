# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business for 1 hour, with 
Senators permitted to speak for 10 minutes each, with the time equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with 
the Republicans controlling the first half.
```
