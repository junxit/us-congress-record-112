# Text of Senate Amendment 2568

**Speaking:** Hutchison, Kay Bailey (H001016) [R-TX]

```
  SA 2568. Mrs. HUTCHISON submitted an amendment intended to be 
proposed by her to the bill S. 3412, to amend the Internal Revenue Code 
of 1986 to provide tax relief to middle-class families; which was 
ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end, add the following:

                     TITLE IV--PERMANENT TAX RELIEF

     SEC. 401. REPEAL OF SUNSET ON MARRIAGE PENALTY RELIEF.

       Title IX of the Economic Growth and Tax Relief 
     Reconciliation Act of 2001 (relating to sunset of provisions 
     of such Act) shall not apply to sections 301, 302, and 303(a) 
     of such Act (relating to marriage penalty relief).
                                 ______
```
