# SENATE BILL REFERRED

**Measures:** S. 2170

```
                          SENATE BILL REFERRED

  Bill of the Senate of the following title was taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 2170. An act to amend the provisions of title 5, United 
     States Code, which are commonly referred to as the ``Hatch 
     Act'', to scale back the provision forbidding certain State 
     and local employees from seeking elective office, clarify the 
     application of certain provisions to the District of 
     Columbia, and modify the penalties which may be imposed for 
     certain violations under subchapter III of chapter 73 of that 
     title; to the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.

                          ____________________
```
