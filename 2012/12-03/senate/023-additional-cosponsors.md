# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 823

  At the request of Mr. Schumer, the name of the Senator from New 
Hampshire (Mrs. Shaheen) was added as a cosponsor of S. 823, a bill to 
permit aliens who lawfully enter the United States on valid visas as 
nonimmigrant elementary and secondary school students to attend public 
schools in the United States for longer than 1 year if such aliens 
reimburse the local educational agency that administers the school for 
the full, unsubsidized per capita cost of providing education at such 
school for the period of the alien's attendance.
