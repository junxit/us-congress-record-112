# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 1813

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 11 a.m. The majority will control 
the first half of the time and the Republicans will control the final 
half. Following morning business, the Senate will resume consideration 
of S. 1813. At 2 p.m., there will be a rollcall vote on the motion to 
invoke cloture on the motion to proceed to S. 1813.
  We have been in consultation with the Republican leader and his 
staff, and we may have another vote this afternoon. We are probably 
going to have more than one vote this afternoon.
```
