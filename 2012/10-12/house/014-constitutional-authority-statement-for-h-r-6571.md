# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6571

**Measures:** H.R. 6571

```
           By Mr. ANDREWS:
       H.R. 6571.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 5, which states ``The Congress 
     shall have power . . . to coin money, regulate the value 
     thereof, and of foreign coin, and fix the standard of weights 
     and measures.''
```
