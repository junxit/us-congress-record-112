# Text of House Amendment 8

**Speaking:** Duncan, Jeff (D000615) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 5326

```
H.R. 5326

                Offered By: Mr. Duncan of South Carolina

       Amendment No. 8: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to implement the National Ocean Policy developed 
     under Executive Order 13547 (75 Fed. Reg. 43023, relating to 
     the stewardship of oceans, coasts, and the Great Lakes).
```
