```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 5326, 
 COMMERCE, JUSTICE, SCIENCE, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 
 2013; WAIVING REQUIREMENT OF CLAUSE 6(a) OF RULE XIII WITH RESPECT TO 
      CONSIDERATION OF CERTAIN RESOLUTIONS; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

report (Rept. No. 112-464) on the resolution (H. Res. 643) providing

Departments of Commerce and Justice, Science, and Related Agencies for 
the fiscal year ending September 30, 2013, and for other purposes; 
waiving a requirement of clause 6(a) of rule XIII with respect to 
consideration of certain resolutions reported from the Committee on 
Rules; and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar 
and ordered to be printed.
```
