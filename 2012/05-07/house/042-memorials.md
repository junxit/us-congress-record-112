# MEMORIALS

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 03, H.J.Res. 08

```
                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 4 of rule XXII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       195. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Wyoming, relative to House 
     Joint Resolution No. 08 expressing support for designation by 
     the Congress of the fourth Saturday in July a National Day of 
     the Cowboy; to the Committee on Oversight and Government 
     Reform.
       196. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Wyoming, relative to Joint Resolution No. 01 
     urging the Congress to pass legislation to open the section 
     1002 study of the coastal plain of the Arctic National 
     Wildlife Refuge; to the Committee on Natural Resources.
       197. Also, a memorial of the House of Representatives of 
     the State of Wyoming, relative to House Joint Resolution No. 
     03 urging Congress to propose the Parental Rights Amendment 
     to the states for ratification; to the Committee on the 
     Judiciary.
```
