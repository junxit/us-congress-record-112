# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.R. 104

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
Clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       41. The SPEAKER presented a petition of The Borough Of 
     Oakland, New Jersey, relative to Resolution 12-46 requesting 
     the assistance of the appropriate Federal State, and County 
     agencies in assist the Borough in addressing the flood 
     control measures needed to prevent or reduce flooding along 
     the Ramapo River and Allerman Brook; to the Committee on 
     Transportation and Infrastructure.
       42. Also, a petition of the Council of St. Charles Parish, 
     Louisiana, relative to Resolution No. 5896 requesting the 
     Congress to support H.R. 104 Ramp Act; jointly to the 
     Committees on Transportation and Infrastructure and Rules.

                          ____________________
```
