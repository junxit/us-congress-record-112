# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5510

**Measures:** H.R. 5510

```
            By Mr. STIVERS:
        H.R. 5510. Congress has the power to enact this 
     legislation pursuant to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the Constitution of the 
     United States grants Congress the explicit power to lay and 
     collect taxes, duties, imports and excises.
```
