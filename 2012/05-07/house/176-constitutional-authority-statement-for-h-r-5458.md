# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5458

**Measures:** H.R. 5458

```
            By Mr. NUNNELEE:
        H.R. 5458.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the United States 
     Constitution, the Taxing, and Spending Clause: ``The Congress 
     shall have Power to lay and collect Taxes, Duties, Imposts 
     and Excises, to pay the Debts and provide for the

[[Page H2326]]

     common Defence and general Welfare of the United States; but 
     all Duties, Imposts and Excises shall be uniform throughout 
     the United States . . .''
```
