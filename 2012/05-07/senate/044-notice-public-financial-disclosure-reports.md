# NOTICE: PUBLIC FINANCIAL DISCLOSURE REPORTS

```
              NOTICE: PUBLIC FINANCIAL DISCLOSURE REPORTS

  The filing date for the 2011 Public Financial Disclosure reports is 
Tuesday, May 15, 2012. Senators, political fund designees and staff 
members whose salaries exceed 120% of the GS 15 pay scale must file 
reports.
  Public Financial Disclosure reports should be submitted to the Senate 
Office of Public Records, 232 Hart Building, Washington, DC. 20510.
  The Public Records office will be open from 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on 
the filing date to accept these filings. For further information, 
please contact the Public Records office at (202) 224 0322.
```
