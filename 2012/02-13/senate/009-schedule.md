# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 4:30 p.m. today. Following 
morning business, the Senate will go to executive session to consider 
the nomination of Adalberto Jordan to be a circuit judge for the 
Eleventh Circuit. At 5:30 p.m., there will be a cloture vote on the 
Jordan nomination. We hope to be able to yield back postcloture time 
and confirm this nomination this evening.
```
