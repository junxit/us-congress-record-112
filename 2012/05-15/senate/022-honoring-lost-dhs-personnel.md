                      HONORING LOST DHS PERSONNEL

  Mr. LIEBERMAN. Mr. President, the mission of the Department of 
Homeland Security, DHS, is broad and diverse. The men and women of DHS 
protect our borders and modes of transportation; they guard our 
waterways; they protect U.S. and foreign leaders; they

[[Page 6741]]

prepare for and respond to disasters; they manage our immigration 
process; and, they defend us against cyber attack. DHS employees 
provide selfless service to their nation and they do so with honor and 
distinction under an ever-present threat. With National Police Week 
2012 commencing, I would like to pay tribute to the Department of 
Homeland Security's agents, officers, and military personnel who lost 
their lives in the service of our Nation. Fifty-five courageous men and 
women of DHS have died in the line of duty since the Department's 
inception in 2003. We owe them more than a tribute on this day, but our 
gratitude begins with that.
