# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Walsh, Joe (W000811) [R-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 5855

```
H.R. 5855

                   Offered By: Mr. Walsh of Illinois

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under title I 
     may be used by the Chief Financial Officer of the Department 
     of Homeland Security to purchase any new software licenses 
     for applications that have been identified as exceeding the 
     number of existing and unused software licenses held by the 
     Department.
```
