# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 5, S. 2061

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks announced 
that the Senate has passed a bill and agreed to a concurrent resolution 
of the following titles in which the concurrence of the House is 
requested:

       S. 2061. An act to provide for an exchange of land between 
     the Department of Homeland Security and the South Carolina 
     State Ports Authority.
       S. Con. Res. 5. Concurrent resolution authorizing the use 
     of Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center for an 
     event to award the Congressional Gold Medal, collectively, to 
     the Montford Point Marines.
```
