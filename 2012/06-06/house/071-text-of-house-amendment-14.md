# Text of House Amendment 14

**Speaking:** Murphy, Tim (M001151) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 5855

```
H.R. 5855

                 Offered By: Mr. Murphy of Pennsylvania

       Amendment No. 14: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to close the Federal Air Marshal Service office 
     located at Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, or to relocate air 
     marshals stationed at that office.
```
