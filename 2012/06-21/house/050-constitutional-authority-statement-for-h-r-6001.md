# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6001

**Measures:** H.R. 6001

```
           By Mr. BURGESS:
       H.R. 6001.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 4 of the Constitution of the 
     United States:
       [The Congress shall have Power] To establish an uniform 
     Rule of Naturalization.
```
