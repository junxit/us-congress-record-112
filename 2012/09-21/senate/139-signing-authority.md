# SIGNING AUTHORITY

**Speaking:** Pryor, Mark L. (P000590) [D-AR]

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. PRYOR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that from Saturday, 
September 22, through Tuesday, November 13, the majority leader and

resolutions.
```
