# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JANUARY 1, 2013

```
                          ORDERS FOR TUESDAY,
                            JANUARY 1, 2013

  Mr. PRYOR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on 
Tuesday, January 1, 2013; that following the prayer and the pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that following any leader remarks the Senate proceed 
to a period of morning business until 3:30 p.m. for debate only, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
