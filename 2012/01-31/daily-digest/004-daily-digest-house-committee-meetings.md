# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

**Measures:** H.R. 3521

```
Committee Meetings
MISCELLANEOUS MEASURES
Committee on Rules: Full Committee held a markup of H.R. 3521, the 
``Expedited Legislative Line-Item Veto and Rescissions Act of 2011.'' 
The bill was ordered reported, as amended.

Joint Meetings
MOLDOVA DEMOCRACY
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe. Commission concluded 
a briefing on Moldova, focusing on democracy, after receiving testimony 
from H. E. Igor Munteanu, Ambassador of Moldova to the United States, 
Canada, Mexico, and Brazil, Chisinau, Moldova; and William H. Hill, 
National War College, and Matthew Rojansky, Carnegie Endowment for 
International Peace Russia and Eurasia Program, both of Washington, 
D.C.
```
