# Daily Digest/NEW PUBLIC LAWS

**Measures:** H.R. 2415, H.R. 3220, H.R. 3413, H.R. 4119, H.R. 4849

```
                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D548)
  H.R. 2415, to designate the facility of the United States Postal 
Service located at 11 Dock Street in Pittston, Pennsylvania, as the 
``Trooper Joshua D. Miller Post Office Building''. Signed on June 5, 
2012. (Public Law 112-124)
  H.R. 3220, to designate the facility of the United States Postal 
Service located at 170 Evergreen Square SW in Pine City, Minnesota, as 
the ``Master Sergeant Daniel L. Fedder Post Office''. Signed on June 5, 
2012. (Public Law 112-125)
  H.R. 3413, to designate the facility of the United States Postal 
Service located at 1449 West Avenue in Bronx, New York, as the 
``Private Isaac T. Cortes Post Office''. Signed on June 5, 2012. 
(Public Law 112-126)
  H.R. 4119, to reduce the trafficking of drugs and to prevent human 
smuggling across the Southwest Border by deterring the construction and 
use of border tunnels. Signed on June 5, 2012. (Public Law 112-127)
  H.R. 4849, to direct the Secretary of the Interior to issue 
commercial use authorizations to commercial stock operators for 
operations in designated wilderness within the Sequoia and Kings Canyon 
National Parks. Signed on June 5, 2012. (Public Law 112-128)
```
