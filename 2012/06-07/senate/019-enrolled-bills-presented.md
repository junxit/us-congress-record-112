# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 292, S. 363

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate announced that on today, June 7, 2012, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 292. An act to resolve the claims of the Bering Straits 
     Native Corporation and the State of Alaska to land adjacent 
     to Salmon Lake in the State of Alaska and to provide for the 
     conveyance to the Bering Straits Native Corporation of 
     certain other public land in partial satisfaction of the land 
     entitlement of the Corporation under the Alaska Native Claims 
     Settlement Act.
       S. 363. An act to authorize the Secretary of Commerce to 
     convey property of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric 
     Administration to the City of Pascagoula, Mississippi, and 
     for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
