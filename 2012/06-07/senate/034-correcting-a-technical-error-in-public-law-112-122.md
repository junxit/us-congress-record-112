# CORRECTING A TECHNICAL ERROR IN PUBLIC LAW 112-122

```
           CORRECTING A TECHNICAL ERROR IN PUBLIC LAW 112-122

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to the consideration of H.R. 5890.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 5890) to correct a technical error in Public 
     Law 112-122.

  There being no objection, the Senate proceeded to consider the bill.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the bill be 
read three times and passed, the motion to reconsider be laid upon the 
table, there be no intervening action or debate, and any statements 
related to this matter be printed in the Record.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The bill (H.R. 5890) was ordered to a third reading, was read the 
third time, and passed.
```
