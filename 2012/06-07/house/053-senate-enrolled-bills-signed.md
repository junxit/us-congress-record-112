# SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

```
                      SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The Speaker announced his signature to enrolled bills of the Senate 
of the following titles:

       S. 292. An act to resolve the claims of the Bering Straits 
     Native Corporation and the State of the Alaska to land 
     adjacent to Salmon Lake in the State of Alaska and to provide 
     for the conveyance to the Bering Straits Native Corporation 
     of certain other public land in partial satisfaction of the 
     land entitlement of the Corporation under the Alaska Native 
     Claims Settlement Act.
       S. 363. An act to authorize the Secretary of Commerce to 
     convey property of the National Oceanic and Atmospheric 
     Administration to the City of Pascagoula, Mississippi, and 
     for other purposes.
```
