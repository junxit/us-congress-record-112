# AMENDMENTS

**Measures:** H.R. 5855

```
                               AMENDMENTS

  Under clause 8 of rule XVIII, proposed amendments were submitted as 
follows:

                               H.R. 5855

                        Offered By: Mr. Crowley

       Amendment No. 17: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  It is the sense of Congress that the Department 
     of Homeland Security should increase coordination with India 
     on efforts to prevent terrorist attacks in the United States 
     and India.

                               H.R. 5855

                        Offered By: Mr. Barletta

       Amendment No. 18: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. ___.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used in contravention of section 642(a) of the Illegal 
     Immigration Reform and Immigrant Responsibility Act of 1996 
     (8 U.S.C. 1373(a)).
```
