# SENATE RESOLUTION 622--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

**Measures:** S.Res. 622

```
                         SUBMITTED RESOLUTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

 SENATE RESOLUTION 622--NOTIFYING THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES OF THE 
                  ELECTION OF A PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  Mr. REID of Nevada (for himself and Mr. McConnell) submitted the 
following resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                              S. Res. 622
```
