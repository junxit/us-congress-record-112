# MOMENT OF SILENCE

```
                           MOMENT OF SILENCE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, it is tradition in this body to have flowers 
on the desk of the departed. We have flying in from Hawaii now a lei 
that will be more Hawaiian than these roses.
  I ask unanimous consent that we now have a moment of silence in honor 
of our departed friend, Dan Inouye.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senate will observe a moment of 
silence.
  (Moment of silence)

                          ____________________
```
