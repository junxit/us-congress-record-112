# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Measures:** S.Res. 624

```
                  ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 9:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate stands adjourned until 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, December 19, 2012, and does so under the provisions of S. 
Res. 624 as a further mark of respect to the late Senator Daniel K. 
Inouye of Hawaii.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 7:51 p.m., adjourned until Wednesday, 
December 19, 2012, at 9:30 a.m.
```
