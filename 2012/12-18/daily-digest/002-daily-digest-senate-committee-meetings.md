COMPUTERIZED TRADING VENUES
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Subcommittee on

and Daniel Mathisson, Credit Suisse, both of Rye, New York; Eric Noll, 
NASDAQ OMX, Haverford, Pennsylvania; and Robert C. Gasser, ITG, 
Madison, New Jersey.
