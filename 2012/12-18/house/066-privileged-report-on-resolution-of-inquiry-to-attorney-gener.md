# PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO ATTORNEY GENERAL

**Measures:** H.Res. 819

```
     PRIVILEGED REPORT ON RESOLUTION OF INQUIRY TO ATTORNEY GENERAL

  Mr. SMITH of Texas, from the Committee on the Judiciary, submitted an 
adverse privileged report (Rept. No. 112-704) on the resolution (H. 
Res. 819) directing the Attorney General of the United States to 
transmit to the House of Representatives, not later than 14 days after 
the date of the adoption of this resolution, any documents and legal 
memoranda in the Attorney General's possession relating to the practice 
of targeted killing of United States citizens and targets abroad, which 
was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
