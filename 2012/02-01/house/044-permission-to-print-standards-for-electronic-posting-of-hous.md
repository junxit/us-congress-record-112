# PERMISSION TO PRINT STANDARDS FOR ELECTRONIC POSTING OF HOUSE AND COMMITTEE DOCUMENTS AND DATA

**Speaking:** Lungren, Daniel E. (L000517) [R-CA]

```
   PERMISSION TO PRINT STANDARDS FOR ELECTRONIC POSTING OF HOUSE AND 
                      COMMITTEE DOCUMENTS AND DATA

  Mr. DANIEL E. LUNGREN of California. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous 
consent that the Standards for the Electronic Posting of House and 
Committee Documents and Data, which were adopted by the Committee on 
House Administration on December 16, 2011, be printed in the 
Congressional Record.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.


                             General Leave

  Mr. DANIEL E. LUNGREN of California. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous 
consent that all Members have 5

[[Page H315]]

legislative days in which to revise and extend their remarks and 
include extraneous materials on the Standards for the Electronic 
Posting of House and Committee Documents and Data.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
