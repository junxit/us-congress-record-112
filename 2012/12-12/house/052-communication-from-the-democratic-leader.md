# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, Democratic Leader:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, December 12, 2012.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker of the House,
     U.S. Capitol, Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Boehner: Pursuant to section 3(b) of the 
     Public Safety Officer Medal of Valor Act of 2001 (42 U.S.C. 
     15202), I am pleased to appoint Joanne Hayes-White of San 
     Francisco, CA, to the Medal of Valor Review Board.
       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     House Democratic Leader.
```
