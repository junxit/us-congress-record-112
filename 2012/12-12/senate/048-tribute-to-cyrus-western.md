# TRIBUTE TO CYRUS WESTERN

```
                        TRIBUTE TO CYRUS WESTERN

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Cyrus Western for his hard 
work as an intern in the U.S. Senate Committee on Indian Affairs. I 
recognize his efforts and contributions to my office as well as to the 
State of Wyoming.
  Cyrus is a native of Sheridan, WY. He graduated from Washington and 
Jefferson College with a bachelor of arts in environmental science. He 
has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has made him an invaluable 
asset to our office. The quality of his work is reflected in his great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Cyrus for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
