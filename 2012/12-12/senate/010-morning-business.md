# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under previous order, the Senate 
will be in a period of morning business until 2 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the first 
hour equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first half.
  Mr. BROWN of Massachusetts. Madam President, I commend the minority 
leader for his nice tribute to Senator Snowe.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Massachusetts.
```
