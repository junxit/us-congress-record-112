# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI], Begich, Mark (B001265) [D-AK]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Senate continue in morning business until 7 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. WHITEHOUSE. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BEGICH. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
