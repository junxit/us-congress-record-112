# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 4310

```
                 UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--H.R. 4310

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate receives the papers with respect to H.R. 4310, the Senate's 
passage of H.R. 4310, as amended, be vitiated; that adoption of the 
Senate amendment be vitiated; that the amendment, the text of S. 3254, 
as amended by the Senate, be modified with the changes that are at the 
desk; that no other amendments be in order, and the Senate proceed to 
vote in relation to the amendment, as modified; that if the substitute 
amendment, as modified, is agreed to, H.R. 4310, as amended, be read a 
third time and passed; finally, that the previous request with respect 
to the Senate's request for conference, including the appointment of 
conferees, be agreed to; with all of the above occurring with no 
intervening action or debate.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
