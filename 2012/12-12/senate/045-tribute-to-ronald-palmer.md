# TRIBUTE TO RONALD PALMER

```
                        TRIBUTE TO RONALD PALMER

● Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Ronald Palmer for his hard 
work as an intern in my Casper office. I recognize his efforts and 
contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Ronald is a native of Rawlins, WY and a graduate of Rawlins High 
School. He currently attends Casper College where he is majoring in 
theatre performance. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic, which has 
made him an invaluable asset to our office. The quality of his work is 
reflected in his great efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Ronald for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
