# TRIBUTE TO THE CENTRALIA LADY PANTHERS SOFTBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
          TRIBUTE TO THE CENTRALIA LADY PANTHERS SOFTBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 9, 2012

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Centralia Lady Panthers softball team of 
Centralia High School for winning the Class 3 Missouri State Softball 
Championship.
  The Centralia Lady Panthers and their coaches should be commended for 
all of their hard work throughout the regular season and for bringing 
home the state title in Class 3 to their school and their community. 
This is their second straight state title.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing the Centralia Lady Panthers 
softball team for a job well done!

                          ____________________
```
