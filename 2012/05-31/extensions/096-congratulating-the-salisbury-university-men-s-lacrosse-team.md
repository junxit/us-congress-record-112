# CONGRATULATING THE SALISBURY UNIVERSITY MEN'S LACROSSE TEAM ON WINNING THEIR 10TH NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP IN THE 2012 NCAA TOURNAMENT FINAL ON MAY 27TH, 2011

```
CONGRATULATING THE SALISBURY UNIVERSITY MEN'S LACROSSE TEAM ON WINNING 
 THEIR 10TH NATIONAL CHAMPIONSHIP IN THE 2012 NCAA TOURNAMENT FINAL ON 
                             MAY 27TH, 2011

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 31, 2012

  Mr. HARRIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Salisbury 
University Men's Lacrosse team on winning the 2012 division three 
lacrosse national championship with a 14 to 10 victory in the 2012 NCAA 
tournament final over Cortland University, on May 27th, 2012.
  The Salisbury Sea Gulls join an elite group of Division III programs 
in NCAA history in collecting at least 10 national championships.
  They became the seventh squad under head coach Jim Berkman to 
complete a perfect season with 23 wins and no losses. Also, senior 
midfielder Sam Bradman was named the Most Outstanding Player in the 
NCAA Tournament for the second consecutive season.
  Salisbury University is located in the heart of the Eastern Shore of 
Maryland and is a Maryland University of National Distinction. I am 
honored and proud to represent them in Maryland's 1st Congressional 
District.

                          ____________________
```
