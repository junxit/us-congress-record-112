# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2117, PROTECTING ACADEMIC FREEDOM IN HIGHER EDUCATION ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 563, H.R. 2117

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2117, 
          PROTECTING ACADEMIC FREEDOM IN HIGHER EDUCATION ACT
```
