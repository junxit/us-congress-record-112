# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following the address, the Senate will be in 
morning business until 4:30 p.m. today.
  At 4:30 p.m., the Senate will proceed to executive session to 
consider the nomination of Margo Brodie to be United States District 
Judge for the Eastern District of New York. At 5:30 p.m., the Senate 
will vote on confirmation of the Brodie nomination.
  I ask unanimous consent that following the vote and resumption of 
legislative session, the Senate be in a period of morning business for 
up to 1 hour, with the time equally divided and controlled between 
Senators Pryor and Alexander or their designees.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
