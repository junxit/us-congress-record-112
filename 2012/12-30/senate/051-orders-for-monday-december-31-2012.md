# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 31, 2012

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                  ORDERS FOR MONDAY, DECEMBER 31, 2012

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it recess until 11 a.m. Monday, 
December 31, 2012; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for their use later in the day; that following any leader 
remarks, the Senate proceed to a period of morning business until 12 
p.m. for debate only, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up 
to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
