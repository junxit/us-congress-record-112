# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 4310, H.R. 5949

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on December 30, 
2012, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bills:

       H.R. 5949. To extend the FISA Amendments Act of 2008 for 
     five years.
       H.R. 4310. To authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2013 
     for military activities of the Department of Defense, for 
     military construction, and for defense activities of the 
     Department of Energy, to prescribe military personnel 
     strengths for such fiscal year, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
