# NOTICE OF REASSEMBLY

**Measures:** H.Res. 479

```
                          NOTICE OF REASSEMBLY

  The SPEAKER laid before the House the text of the formal notification 
sent to Members on Thursday, December 27, 2012, of the reassembly of 
the House.

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, December 27, 2012.
       Dear Colleague: Pursuant to section 2 of House Resolution 
     479, and after consultation with the Minority Leader of the 
     House, I have determined that the public interest requires 
     that the House reassemble at 2:00 PM on Sunday, December 30, 
     2012. Further announcements will be provided by the Majority 
     Leader's office.
       Thank you for your attention to this urgent matter.
           Sincerely,
     John A. Boehner.

                          ____________________
```
