# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                Washington, DC, December 30, 2012.
     Hon. John A. Boehner,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the U.S. House of Representatives, 
     the Clerk received the following message from the Secretary 
     of the Senate on December 30, 2012 at 1:00 p.m.
       That the Senate passed S. 3454.
       That the Senate passed with amendments H.R. 1.
       That the Senate passed with amendments H.R. 1464.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 6014.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 6620.
       That the Senate passed with amendment H.R. 6621.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
     Karen L. Haas.
```
