# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until 12 noon, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each and with 
the time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first 30 minutes 
and the majority controlling the second 30 minutes.
```
