# Cloture Motion (Executive Session)

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                             Cloture Motion

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I have a cloture motion which is at the 
desk.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The cloture motion having been presented under 
rule XXII, the Chair directs the clerk to read the motion.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

                             Cloture Motion

       We, the undersigned Senators, in accordance with the 
     provisions of rule XXII of the Standing Rules of the Senate, 
     hereby move to bring to a close debate on the nomination of 
     Jesse M. Furman, of New York, to be United States District 
     Judge for the Southern District of New York:
         Harry Reid, Patrick J. Leahy, Robert P. Casey Jr., 
           Richard J. Durbin, Richard Blumenthal, Jeff Bingaman, 
           Christopher A. Coons, Sheldon Whitehouse, Al Franken, 
           Herb Kohl, Dianne Feinstein, Tom Udall, Mark Begich, 
           Kent Conrad, Amy Klobuchar, Charles E. Schumer, Kirsten 
           E. Gillibrand, Joseph I. Lieberman.

                          ____________________
```
