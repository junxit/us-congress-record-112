# Text of Senate Amendment 1629

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
  SA 1629. Mr. RUBIO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1813, to reauthorize Federal-aid highway and highway 
safety construction programs, and for other purposes; which was ordered 
to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the appropriate place, insert the following:

     SEC. ___. PROHIBITION ON USE OF AMOUNTS.

       None of the amounts appropriated or otherwise made 
     available under this Act or an amendment made by this Act may 
     be used to erect physical signage indicating that a project 
     is funded under this Act.
                                 ______
```
