# Text of Senate Amendment 1644

**Speaking:** Snowe, Olympia J. (S000663) [R-ME]

```
  SA 1644. Ms. SNOWE (for herself, Mr. Whitehouse, Ms. Collins, Mr. 
Sanders, Mr. Reed, and Mrs. Shaheen) submitted an amendment intended to 
be proposed by her to the bill S. 1813, to reauthorize Federal-aid 
highway and highway safety construction programs, and for other 
purposes; which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 143, between lines 9 and 10, insert the following:
       ``(3) Use of funds for other activities.--Notwithstanding 
     paragraph (1) and subsection (c), the Secretary may permit a 
     State to use amounts apportioned to the State for the 
     congestion mitigation and air quality improvement program 
     under section 104(b)(4) to carry out any activity on a system 
     that was eligible for funding under that program as in effect 
     on December 31, 2010.
                                 ______
```
