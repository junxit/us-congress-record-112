# Text of Senate Amendment 1628

**Speaking:** Rubio, Marco (R000595) [R-FL]

```
  SA 1628. Mr. RUBIO submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 1813, to reauthorize Federal-aid highway and highway 
safety construction programs, and for other purposes; which was ordered 
to lie on the table; as follows:

       At the end of subtitle E of title I, add the following:

     SEC. ___. EVACUATION ROUTES.

       Each State shall give adequate consideration to the needs 
     of evacuation routes in the State when allocating funds 
     apportioned to the State under title 23, Unites States Code, 
     for the construction of Federal-aid highways.
                                 ______
```
