# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

**Measures:** H.R. 3902, H.R. 4348

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on July 2, 2012, she 
presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, the 
following bill.

       H.R. 4348. To provide an extension of Federal-aid highway, 
     highway safety, motor carrier safety, transit, and other 
     programs funded out of the Highway Trust Fund pending 
     enactment of a multiyear law reauthorizing such programs, and 
     for other purposes.

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, further reported that on July 16, 
2012, she presented to the President of the United States, for his 
approval, the following bill.

       H.R. 3902. To amend the District of Columbia Home Rule Act 
     to revise the timing of special elections for local office in 
     the District of Columbia.

                          ____________________
```
