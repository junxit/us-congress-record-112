# AMENDMENT REGARDING FORMERLY USED DEFENSE SITES H.R. 5856 DEPARTMENT OF DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT

```
AMENDMENT REGARDING FORMERLY USED DEFENSE SITES H.R. 5856 DEPARTMENT OF 
                       DEFENSE APPROPRIATIONS ACT

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. EARL BLUMENAUER

                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 17, 2012

  Mr. BLUMENAUER. Mr. Speaker, tonight I offer an amendment to H.R. 
5856 that would reduce spending on ``Operation and Maintenance, Defense 
Wide'' account by $88,952,000 and increase spending on the 
``Environmental Restoration, Formerly Used Defense Sites'' account by 
an equal amount.
```
