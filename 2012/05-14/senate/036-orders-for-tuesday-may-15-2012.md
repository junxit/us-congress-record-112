# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 15, 2012

**Speaking:** Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 15, 2012

  Mr. BROWN of Ohio. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that when 
the Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Tuesday, May 15, 2012; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; and that the majority leader be recognized; further, 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 until 2:15 to allow for the weekly 
caucus meetings.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
