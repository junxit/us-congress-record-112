# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House, reported that on March 29, 2012,

the following bill.

       H.R. 4281. To provide an extension of Federal-aid highway, 
     highway safety, motor carrier safety, transit, and other 
     programs funded out of the Highway Trust Fund pending
```
