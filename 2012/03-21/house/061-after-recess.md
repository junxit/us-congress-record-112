# AFTER RECESS

**Measures:** H.Res. 591, H.R. 886

```
                              {time}  1415
                              AFTER RECESS

  The recess having expired, the House was called to order by the 
Speaker pro tempore (Mr. Hastings of Washington) at 2 o'clock and 15 
minutes p.m.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on questions previously postponed.
  Votes will be taken in the following order:
  Ordering the previous question on H. Res. 591;
  Adopting H. Res. 591, if ordered;
  Suspending the rules and concurring in the Senate amendment to H.R. 
886; and
  Agreeing to the Speaker's approval of the Journal, if ordered.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.

                          ____________________
```
