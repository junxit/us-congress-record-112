# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4229

**Measures:** H.R. 4229

```
            By Mr. BERMAN:
        H.R. 4229.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:

     the authority delineated in Article I section I, which 
     includes an implied power for the Congress to regulate the 
     conduct of the United States with respect to foreign affairs.
```
