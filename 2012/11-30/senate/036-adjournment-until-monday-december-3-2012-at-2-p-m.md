# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, DECEMBER 3, 2012, AT 2 P.M.

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, DECEMBER 3, 2012, AT 2 P.M.

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that it adjourn under the 
provisions of S. Res. 607 as a further mark of respect to the memory of 
former Senator George McGovern of South Dakota.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 4:38 p.m., adjourned until 
Monday, December 3, 2012, at 2 p.m.
```
