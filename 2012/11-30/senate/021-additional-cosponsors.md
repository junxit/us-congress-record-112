                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 998

  At the request of Mr. Akaka, the name of the Senator from Maryland 
(Ms. Mikulski) was added as a cosponsor of S. 998, a bill to amend 
title IV of the Employee Retirement Income Security Act of 1974 to 
require the Pension Benefit Guaranty Corporation, in the case of 
airline pilots who are required by regulation to retire at age 60, to 
compute the actuarial value of monthly benefits in the form of a life 
annuity commencing at age 60.
