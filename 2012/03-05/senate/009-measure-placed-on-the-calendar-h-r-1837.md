# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 1837

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 1837

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 1837

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, H.R. 1837 is at the desk and due for a 
second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will report the bill by 
title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1837) to address certain water-related

  Mr. REID. I object to any further proceedings on the legislation at 
this time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bill will 
be placed on the calendar.
```
