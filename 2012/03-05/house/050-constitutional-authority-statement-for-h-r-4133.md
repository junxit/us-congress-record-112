# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4133

**Measures:** H.R. 4133

```
            By Mr. CANTOR:
        H.R. 4133.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this legislation 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8, clause 3 of the U.S. 
     Constitution, the power to ``regulate Commerce with foreign 
     Nations'' and pursuant to Article I, Section 8, clause 1, the 
     power to ``provide for the common Defence.''
```
