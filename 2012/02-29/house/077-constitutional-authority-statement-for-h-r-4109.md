# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4109

**Measures:** H.R. 4109

```
           By Mr. GALLEGLY:
       H.R. 4109.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Under Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2 of the United States 
     Constitution, the power of Congress to make all needful Rules 
     and Regulations respecting the Territory or other Property 
     belonging to the United States. As well as Article I, Section 
     8, Clause 18, relating to the power to make all laws 
     necessary and proper for carrying out the powers vested in 
     Congress.
```
