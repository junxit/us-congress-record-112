# TRIBUTE TO GREYSON BUCKINGHAM

```
                     TRIBUTE TO GREYSON BUCKINGHAM

● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Greyson Buckingham for his 
hard work as an intern for the U.S. Senate Republican Policy Committee. 
I recognize his continued efforts and contributions to my office.
  Greyson is a native of Kelly, WY, and a graduate of Jackson Hole High 
School. He is a student at Georgetown University, where he is majoring 
in history and government. He has demonstrated a strong work ethic 
which has made him an invaluable asset to the U.S. Senate Republican 
Policy Committee. The quality of his work is reflected in his great 
efforts over the last several months.
  I want to thank Greyson for the dedication he has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have him as part of our team. 
I know he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. 
I wish him all my best on his next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
