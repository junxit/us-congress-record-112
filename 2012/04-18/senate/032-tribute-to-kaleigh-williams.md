# TRIBUTE TO KALEIGH WILLIAMS

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO KALEIGH WILLIAMS

● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Kaleigh Williams for her hard 
work as an intern--for a second term--in my Cheyenne office. I 
recognize her efforts and contributions to my office as well as to the 
State of Wyoming.
  Kaleigh is a native of Cheyenne and a graduate of Cheyenne East High 
School. She graduated from the University of Wyoming in 2011 with a 
degree in political science. She has once again demonstrated a strong 
work ethic, which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of her work is reflected in her great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Kaleigh for the dedication she has shown while 
working for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of 
our team. I know she will have continued success with

[[Page S2493]]

all of her future endeavors. I wish her all my best on her next 
journey.●

                          ____________________
```
