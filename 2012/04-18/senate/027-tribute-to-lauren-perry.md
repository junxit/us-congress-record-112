# TRIBUTE TO LAUREN PERRY

```
                        TRIBUTE TO LAUREN PERRY

● Mr. BARRASSO. Madam President, I would like to take the 
opportunity to express my appreciation to Lauren Perry for her hard 
work as an intern in my Washington, DC office. I recognize her efforts 
and contributions to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Lauren is a native of Buffalo, WY, and graduated from Paint Branch 
High School in Burtonsville, MD. She recently earned a master of arts 
in English at the University of Wyoming. She has demonstrated a strong 
work ethic which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of her work is reflected in her great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I want to thank Lauren for the dedication she has shown while working 
for me and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of our team. 
I know she will have continued success with all of her future 
endeavors. I wish her all my best on her next journey.●

                          ____________________
```
