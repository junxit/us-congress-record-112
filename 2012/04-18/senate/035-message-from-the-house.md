# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1815, H.R. 4089

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:33 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1815. An act to posthumously award a Congressional 
     Gold Medal to Lena Horne in recognition of her achievements 
     and contributions to American culture and the civil rights 
     movement.
       H.R. 4089. An act to protect and enhance opportunities for 
     recreational hunting, fishing and shooting.
```
