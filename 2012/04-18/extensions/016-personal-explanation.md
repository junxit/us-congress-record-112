# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JOHN SULLIVAN

                              of oklahoma

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, April 18, 2012

  Mr. SULLIVAN. Mr. Speaker, I intended to vote ``no'' on rollcall vote 
159 to H.R. 4089 taken on April 17, 2012. The Congressional Record 
currently lists me as not voting on this amendment. I firmly believe 
that this amendment, under the guise of permitting hunting, fishing and 
recreational shooting, in effect guts the bill, and is a vote against 
sportsmen nationwide.
```
