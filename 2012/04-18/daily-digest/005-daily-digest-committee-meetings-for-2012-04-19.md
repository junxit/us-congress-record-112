                    COMMITTEE MEETINGS FOR THURSDAY,

        (Committee meetings are open unless otherwise indicated)


                                 Senate

  Committee on Appropriations: business meeting to consider adoption 
of the fiscal year 2013 302(b) allocations and to markup proposed 
budget estimates for fiscal year 2013 for Commerce, Justice, Science

Development and related agencies, 10:30 a.m., SD-192.

hearings to examine Navy shipbuilding programs in review of the 
Defense Authorization request for fiscal year 2013 and the Future 
Years Defense Program, 9:30 a.m., SR-232A.

examine the impacts of sea level rise on domestic energy and water 
infrastructure, 9:30 a.m., SD-366.

focusing on United States policy options, 10 a.m., SD-419.
  Committee on Health, Education, Labor, and Pensions: to hold 
hearings to examine delays in OSHA's standard-setting process and

District of Iowa, Michael P. Shea, of Connecticut, to be United

Curiel, of California, to be United States District Judge for the 
Southern District of California, and Robert J. Shelby, of Utah, to

SD-226.
