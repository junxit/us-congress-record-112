# TRIBUTE TO CODY HORTON

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO CODY HORTON

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Cody Horton, an 
intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Cody is a graduate of Mitchell High School in Mitchell, SD. 
Currently, he is attending the University of South Dakota, where he is 
majoring in political science and history. He is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.

[[Page S3040]]

  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Cody for 
all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
