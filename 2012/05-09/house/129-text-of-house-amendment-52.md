# Text of House Amendment 52

**Speaking:** Schweikert, David (S001183) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 5326

```
H.R. 5326

                       Offered By: Mr. Schweikert

       Amendment No. 52: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used by the Department of Justice to bring any action 
     against any State for implemention of a State law requiring 
     voter identification.
```
