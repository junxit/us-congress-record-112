# RECOGNIZING LT. COL. DAVID CARROLL FOR HIS TWENTY-FIVE YEARS OF SERVICE IN THE U.S. AIR FORCE.

**Speaking:** Schweikert, David (S001183) [R-AZ]

```
RECOGNIZING LT. COL. DAVID CARROLL FOR HIS TWENTY-FIVE YEARS OF SERVICE 
                         IN THE U.S. AIR FORCE.

                                 ______

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives
```
