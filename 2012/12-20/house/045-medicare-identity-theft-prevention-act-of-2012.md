# MEDICARE IDENTITY THEFT PREVENTION ACT OF 2012

**Measures:** H.R. 1509

```
             MEDICARE IDENTITY THEFT PREVENTION ACT OF 2012

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 1509) to amend title II 
of the Social Security Act to prohibit the inclusion of Social Security 
account numbers on Medicare cards, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Texas (Mr. Sam Johnson) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table. H.R. 1509.
```
