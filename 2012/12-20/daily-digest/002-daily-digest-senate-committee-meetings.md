# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
SUPERSTORM SANDY RECOVERY
Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs: Subcommittee on 
Housing, Transportation and Community Development concluded a hearing 
to examine recovering from superstorm Sandy, focusing on rebuilding our 
infrastructure, after receiving testimony from Peter Rogoff, Federal 
Transit Administrator, Department of Transportation; Yolanda Chavez, 
Deputy Assistant Secretary for Grant Programs, Office of Community 
Planning and Development,

[[Page D1059]]

Department of Housing and Urban Development; James Weinstein, NJ 
TRANSIT Corporation, Morristown, New Jersey; Thomas F. Prendergast, MTA 
New York City Transit, Brewster, New York; and Patrick J. Foye, The 
Port Authority of New York and New Jersey, Sands Point, New York.
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Finance: Committee concluded a hearing to examine the 
nominations of William B. Shultz, of the District of Columbia, to be 
General Counsel of the Department of Health and Human Services, and 
Christopher J. Meade, of New York, to be General Counsel for the 
Department of the Treasury, who was introduced by Senator Schumer, 
after the nominees testified and answered questions in their own 
behalf.
BENGHAZI
Committee on Foreign Relations: Committee concluded a hearing to 
examine Benghazi, focusing on the attacks and the lessons learned, 
after receiving testimony from William J. Burns and Thomas R. Nides, 
both a Deputy Secretary of State.
```
