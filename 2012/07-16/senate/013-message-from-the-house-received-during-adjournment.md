# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

```
           MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  Under the order of the Senate of January 5, 2011, the Secretary of 
the Senate, on July 13, 2012, during the adjournment of the Senate, 
received a message from the House of Representatives announcing the 
Speaker had signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 3902. An act to amend the District of Columbia Home 
     Rule Act to revise the timing of special elections for local 
     office in the District of Columbia.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed during the session of the 
Senate by the Acting President pro tempore [Mr. Coons].

                          ____________________
```
