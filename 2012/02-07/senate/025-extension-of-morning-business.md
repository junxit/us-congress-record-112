# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Casey, Robert P., Jr. (C001070) [D-PA], Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the period for 
morning business be extended until 7 p.m, with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. CASEY. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. BROWN of Ohio. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the 
order for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
