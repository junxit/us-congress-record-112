# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. WILLIAM L. OWENS

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 7, 2012

  Mr. OWENS. Mr. Speaker, on February 6, 2012, I missed a series of 
votes. If I had been present, I would have voted as follows:
  On rollcall 34, On Agreeing to the Resolution providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1734) to decrease the deficit by 
realigning, consolidating, selling, disposing, and improving the 
efficiency of Federal buildings and other civilian real property, and 
for other purposes, I would have voted ``nay.''
  On rollcall 35, On Motion to Suspend the Rules and Pass, as amended, 
a bill to provide the Quileute Indian Tribe tsunami and flood 
protection, I would have voted ``yea.''
```
