# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 3630, TEMPORARY PAYROLL TAX CUT CONTINUATION ACT OF 2011

**Speaking:** Bishop, Timothy H. (B001242) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.R. 3630

```
NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 3630, 
           TEMPORARY PAYROLL TAX CUT CONTINUATION ACT OF 2011

  Mr. BISHOP of New York. Mr. Speaker, under rule XXII, clause 7(c), I 
hereby announce my intention to offer a motion to instruct on H.R. 
3630, the conference report to extend the payroll tax, unemployment 
insurance, and SGR payments for doctors.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mr. Bishop of New York moves that the managers on the part 
     of the House at the conference on the disagreeing votes of 
     the two Houses on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 3630 
     be instructed to file a conference report not later than 
     February 17, 2012.

                          ____________________
```
