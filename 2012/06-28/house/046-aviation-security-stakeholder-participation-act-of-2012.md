# AVIATION SECURITY STAKEHOLDER PARTICIPATION ACT OF 2012

**Measures:** H.R. 1447

```
        AVIATION SECURITY STAKEHOLDER PARTICIPATION ACT OF 2012

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 1447) to amend title 
49, United States Code, to direct the Assistant Secretary of Homeland 
Security (Transportation Security Administration) to establish an 
Aviation Security Advisory Committee, and for other purposes, as 
amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from New York (Mr. King) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill, as amended, was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
