# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 29, 2012

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, JUNE 29, 2012

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on Friday, June 
29; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date; that the morning hour be deemed to 
have expired and the time for two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that the majority leader may be recognized, and that 
Senators be permitted to speak up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
