# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 3187

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, June 28, 2012, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bill:

       S. 3187. An act to amend the Federal Food, Drug, and 
     Cosmetic Act to revise and extend the user-fee programs for 
     prescription drugs and medical devices, to establish user-fee 
     programs for generic drugs and biosimilars, and for other 
     purposes.
```
