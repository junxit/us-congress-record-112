# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive Message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June 
28, 2012 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nomination:
       TIMOTHY M. BROAS, OF MARYLAND, TO BE AMBASSADOR 
     EXTRAORDINARY AND PLENIPOTENTIARY OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
     AMERICA TO THE KINGDOM OF THE NETHERLANDS, WHICH WAS SENT TO 
     THE SENATE ON APRIL 26, 2012.
```
