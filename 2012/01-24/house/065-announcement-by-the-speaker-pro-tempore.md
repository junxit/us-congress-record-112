# ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

```
                ANNOUNCEMENT BY THE SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, proceedings 
will resume on questions previously postponed. Votes will be taken in 
the following order:
  The motion to suspend the rules and adopt House Resolution 516, by 
the yeas and nays;
  The motion to suspend the rules and pass H.R. 2070, by the yeas and 
nays; and
  The motion to instruct on H.R. 3630, by the yeas and nays.
  The first electronic vote will be conducted as a 15-minute vote. 
Remaining electronic votes will be conducted as 5-minute votes.
```
