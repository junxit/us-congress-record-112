# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until 4 p.m. today, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the first 30 minutes controlled by the majority leader 
or his designee and the second 30 minutes controlled by the Republican 
leader or his designee.

[[Page 220]]

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
