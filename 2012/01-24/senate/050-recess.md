# RECESS

```

  Mr. REID. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I ask that it recess under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands in 
recess until 8:30 p.m.
  Thereupon, at 4:41 p.m., the Senate recessed until 8:30 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Udall of 
Colorado).
```
