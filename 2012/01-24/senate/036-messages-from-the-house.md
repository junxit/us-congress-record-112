# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 1141, H.R. 3117

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:54 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1141. An act to authorize the Secretary of the 
     Interior to study the suitability and feasibility of 
     designating prehistoric, historic, and limestone forest sites 
     on Rota, Commonwealth of the Northern Mariana Islands, as a 
     unit of the National Park System.
       H.R. 3117. An act to grant the Secretary of the Interior 
     permanent authority to authorize States to issue electronic 
     duck stamps, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
