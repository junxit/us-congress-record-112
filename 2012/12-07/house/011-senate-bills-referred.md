# SENATE BILLS REFERRED

```
                         SENATE BILLS REFERRED

  Bills of the Senate of the following titles were taken from the 
Speaker's table and, under the rule, referred as follows:

       S. 1947. An act to prohibit attendance of an animal 
     fighting venture, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Agriculture; in addition to the Committee on the Judiciary 
     for a period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in 
     each case for consideration of such provisions as fall within 
     the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.

[[Page 16592]]


       S. 3331. An act to provide for universal intercountry 
     adoption accreditation standards, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
       S. 3666. An act to amend the Animal Welfare Act to modify 
     the definition of ``exhibitor''; to the Committee on 
     Agriculture.
```
