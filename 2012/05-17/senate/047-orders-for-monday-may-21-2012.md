# ORDERS FOR MONDAY MAY 21, 2012

```
                     ORDERS FOR MONDAY MAY 21, 2012

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, May 
21, 2012; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed to have 
expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later 
in the day, and that the majority leader be recognized; further, that 
at 4:30 p.m. the Senate proceed to executive session to consider 
Calendar No. 552, Paul J. Watford, of California, to be U.S. Circuit 
Judge for the Ninth Circuit, with 1 hour of debate equally divided and 
controlled in the usual form; that upon the use or yielding back of the 
time, the Senate proceed to vote on the motion to invoke cloture on the 
nomination; and that if cloture is not invoked, the Senate resume 
legislative session and proceed to vote on the motion to invoke cloture 
on the motion to proceed to S. 3187, the FDA user fees legislation.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
