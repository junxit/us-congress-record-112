# TRIBUTE TO HEATHER JELEN

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO HEATHER JELEN

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Heather Jelen, a 
legal intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all the hard work she 
has done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
year.
  Heather is a graduate of Bethel University in Saint Paul, MN. 
Currently, she is attending George Washington University Law School in 
Washington, DC. She is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting 
the most out of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Heather 
for all the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
