# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4967

**Measures:** H.R. 4967

```
           By Mr. SMITH of Texas:
       H.R. 4967.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article III, section 1, of the Constitution empowers 
     Congress to create ``such inferior courts as the Congress may 
     from time to time ordain and establish.'' Additionally, 
     Article I, section 8, gives Congress plenary power over the 
     subject of bankruptcy.
```
