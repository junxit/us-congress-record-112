# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3523, CYBER INTELLIGENCE SHARING AND PROTECTION ACT; PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES; PROVIDING FOR...

**Measures:** H.Res. 631, H.R. 3523, H.R. 4628

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 3523, CYBER 
INTELLIGENCE SHARING AND PROTECTION ACT; PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF 
MOTIONS TO SUSPEND THE RULES; PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4628, 
          INTEREST RATE REDUCTION ACT; AND FOR OTHER PURPOSES

  Mr. NUGENT, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 112 454) on the resolution (H. Res. 631) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 3523) to provide for the sharing of 
certain cyber threat intelligence and cyber threat information between 
the intelligence community and cybersecurity entities, and for other 
purposes; providing for consideration of motions to suspend the rules; 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4628) to extend student 
loan interest rates for undergraduate Federal Direct Stafford Loans; 
and for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
