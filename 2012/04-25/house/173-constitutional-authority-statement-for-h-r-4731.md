# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4731

**Measures:** H.R. 4731

```
           By Mr. HUIZENGA of Michigan:
       H.R. 4731.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to the power granted to Congress under Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause 3 of the United States Constitution, which 
     states the Congress shall have the power ``To regulate 
     Commerce with foreign Nations.''
```
