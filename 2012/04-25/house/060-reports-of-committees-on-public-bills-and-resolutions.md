the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as

     suspend the rules; providing for consideration of the bill 
     (H.R. 4628) to extend student loan interest rates for 
     undergraduate Federal Direct Stafford Loans; and for other 
     purposes (Rept. 112 454). Referred to the House Calendar.
