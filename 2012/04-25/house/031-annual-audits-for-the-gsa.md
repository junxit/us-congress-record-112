# ANNUAL AUDITS FOR THE GSA

**Speaking:** Denham, Jeff (D000612) [R-CA]

```
                       ANNUAL AUDITS FOR THE GSA

  (Mr. DENHAM asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. DENHAM. Madam Speaker, I rise to talk about the corruption, the 
fraud, the waste within GSA, an agency that has nearly a $10 billion 
slush fund that they hide from the American taxpayers every single 
year.
  Today I'm going to be introducing a bill that will request 
transparency on an annual basis, show an annual audit so the American 
taxpayers can see exactly where this waste is going and hold this 
agency accountable.
  We're going to hold another hearing on the issue to make sure that 
the waste stops, and that we actually start selling off some of the 
buildings that are sitting vacant right now today, an opportunity for 
Republicans and Democrats to actually come together, just getting rid 
of waste, and at the same time that we sell the properties and 
redevelop the things that we aren't using, put people back to work.

                          ____________________
```
