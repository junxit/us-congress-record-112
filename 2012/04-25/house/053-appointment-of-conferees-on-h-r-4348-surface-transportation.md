32710, 32713, 40101, and 40301 of the Senate amendment, and 
modifications committed to conference: Messrs. Upton, Whitfield, and

  From the Committee on Ways and Means, for consideration of secs. 141 
and 142 of the House bill, and secs. 1801, 40101, 40102, 40201, 40202,
