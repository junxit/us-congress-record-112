# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1925

```
                  UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--S. 1925

  Mr. LEAHY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that following the 
adoption of the motion to proceed to S. 1925, the Senate be in a period 
of debate only on the bill for the remainder of today's session; that 
when the Senate resumes consideration of the bill on Thursday, April 
26, it be for debate only until 11:30 a.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
