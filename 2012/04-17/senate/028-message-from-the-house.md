# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 12:33 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 3001. An act to award a Congressional Gold Medal to 
     Raoul Wallenberg, in recognition of his achievements and 
     heroic actions during the Holocaust.
       H.R. 4040. An act to provide for the award of a gold medal 
     on behalf of Congress to Jack Nicklaus in recognition of his 
     service to the Nation in promoting excellence and good 
     sportsmanship in golf.
```
