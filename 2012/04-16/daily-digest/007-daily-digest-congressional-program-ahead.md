# Daily Digest/CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

**Measures:** S. 1789

```
                      CONGRESSIONAL PROGRAM AHEAD

                Week of April 17 through April 20, 2012

                             Senate Chamber

On Tuesday, at approximately 11 a.m., Senate will resume consideration 
of the motion to proceed to consideration of S. 1789, 21st Century 
Postal Service Act, and after agreeing to the motion to reconsider the 
vote by which cloture was not invoked, vote on the motion to invoke 
cloture on the motion to proceed to consideration of the bill, upon 
reconsideration, at approximately 11:10 a.m.
  During the balance of the week, Senate may consider any cleared 
legislative and executive business.
```
