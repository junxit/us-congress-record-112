         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

     a member of the World Trade Organization, and for other 
     purposes (Rept. 112-632). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.

     current law, and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept.

     state of the Union.

     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. ISSA: Committee on Oversight and Government Reform.

       Mr. SMITH of Texas: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 6063.

       Mr. SMITH of Texas: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 4362.

       Mr. SCOTT of South Carolina: Committee on Rules. House
