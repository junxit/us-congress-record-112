**Speaking:** Speier, Jackie (S001175) [D-CA]

                                 ______

  Ms. SPEIER. Mr. Speaker, I would like to state for the record that my 
vote against the Holt amendment, Roll No. 504, to H.R. was made in 
error. I support this amendment, which would strike a provision that
