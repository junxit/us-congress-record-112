# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 9, 2012

```
                   ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 9, 2012

  Ms. STABENOW. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until Wednesday, May 9, 
at 9:30 a.m.; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and that the majority leader be recognized.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Ms. STABENOW. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Ms. STABENOW. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
