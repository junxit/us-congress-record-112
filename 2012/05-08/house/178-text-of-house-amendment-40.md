# Text of House Amendment 40

**Speaking:** Scalise, Steve (S001176) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 5326

```
H.R. 5326

                        Offered By: Mr. Scalise

       Amendment No. 40: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. 542.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used may be used to implement, administer, or enforce the 
     Equal Employment Opportunity Commission (EEOC) Enforcement 
     Guidance Number 915.002 concerning ``Consideration of arrest 
     and conviction records in employment decisions''.
```
