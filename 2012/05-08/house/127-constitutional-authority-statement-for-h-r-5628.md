# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 5628

**Measures:** H.R. 5628

```
            By Mr. PAUL:
        H.R. 5628.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Constitutional Justification: A Bill To extend the 
     temporary suspension of duty on mixtures or coprecipitates of 
     yttrium oxide and europium oxide.
       Justification: This is authorized by Article I, Section 8 
     of the US Constitution.
```
