# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4406

**Measures:** H.R. 4406

```
           By Mr. CAMP:
       H.R. 4406.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       According to Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the 
     Constitution: The Congress shall have the power to enact this 
     legislation to regulate commerce with foreign Nations, among 
     the several States, and with Indian tribes;
```
