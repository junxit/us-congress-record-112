# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4407

**Measures:** H.R. 4407

```
           By Mr. YOUNG of Indiana:
       H.R. 4407.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 in which Congress has the 
     explicit authority to provide for the common Defense and 
     general Welfare of the United States and Article I, Section 
     8, Clause 14 to make Rules for the Government and Regulation 
     of land and naval forces.
```
