# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4400

**Measures:** H.R. 4400

```
           By Mr. MARKEY:
       H.R. 4400.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, section 3 of the United States Constitution, 
     which states in relevant part that, ``The Congress shall have 
     Power to dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory or other Property 
     belonging to the United States,'' grants Congress the 
     authority to enact this legislation.
```
