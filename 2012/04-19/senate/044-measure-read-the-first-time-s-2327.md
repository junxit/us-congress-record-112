# MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2327

```
                  MEASURE READ THE FIRST TIME--S. 2327

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, there is a bill at the desk due for its 
first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 2327) to prohibit direct foreign assistance to 
     the Government of Egypt until the President makes certain 
     certifications related to treatment of nongovernmental 
     organization workers, and for other purposes.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I now ask for a second reading and, in order 
to place the bill on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard.
  The bill will be read for the second time on the next legislative 
day.

                          ____________________
```
