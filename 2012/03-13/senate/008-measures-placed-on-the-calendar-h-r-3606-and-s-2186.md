# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3606 AND S. 2186

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 2186, H.R. 3606

```
         MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 3606 AND S. 2186

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I am told there are two bills at the desk 
due for a second reading.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bills by 
title for a second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 3606) to increase American job creation and 
     economic growth by improving access to the public capital 
     markets for emerging growth companies.
       A bill (S. 2186) to amend the Americans with Disabilities 
     Act of 1990 to prohibit the Attorney General from 
     administering or enforcing certain accessibility regulations 
     relating to pools at public accommodations or provided by 
     public entities.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I object to further proceedings regarding 
these two bills.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The objection is heard. The bills 
will be placed on the calendar.

                          ____________________
```
