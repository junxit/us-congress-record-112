# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Hagan, Kay R. (H001049) [D-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 4310

```
                                SCHEDULE

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from North Carolina.
  Mrs. HAGAN. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
begin consideration of the conference report to accompany H.R. 4310, 
the National Defense Authorization Act. The filing deadline for second-
degree amendments to the emergency supplemental bill is 1:30 p.m. 
today.
  At approximately 2 p.m., there will be a rollcall vote on adoption of 
the national defense conference report. We will work on an agreement 
for amendments in order to complete action on the supplemental as well 
as an agreement on FISA.
```
