# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Barber, Ron (B001279) [D-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 4606

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. RON BARBER

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 20, 2012

  Mr. BARBER. Mr. Speaker, due to my attending a congressional vigil 
for the victims of the shooting at Sandy Hook Elementary, I missed one 
recorded vote on Monday, December 17. I would like the Record to 
indicate at this point how I would have voted had I been present for 
this vote.
  On rollcall No. 627, H.R. 4606, I would have voted ``yea'' to 
authorize the Secretary of the Interior to issue right-of-way permits 
for natural gas pipelines within the boundary of Glacier National Park 
in Montana.
```
