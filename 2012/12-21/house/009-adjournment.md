# ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 146

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER. Without objection, the House stands adjourned pursuant 
to the previous order.
  There was no objection.

order of the House of today, the House adjourned until noon, Monday,
```
