# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 27, 2012

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 27, 2012

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, June 27; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use until later 
in the day; that the majority leader be recognized; and that the first 
hour of debate be equally divided and controlled between the two 
leaders or their designees, with the majority controlling the first 
half and the Republicans controlling the final half.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
