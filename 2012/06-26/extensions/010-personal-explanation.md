# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Miller, George (M000725) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 4480

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GEORGE MILLER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 26, 2012

  Mr. GEORGE MILLER of California. Mr. Speaker, on June 21, 2012, I was 
unavoidably absent for rollcall votes 408, 409, 410, and 411.
  Had I been present, I would have voted ``yea'' on rollcall no. 408, 
the amendment to fully fund the Commodity Futures Trading Commission to 
limit speculation in energy markets.
  I oppose the irresponsible H.R. 4480. I would have voted ``yea'' on 
rollcall no. 409, and I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall no. 410.
  Finally, had I been present, I would have voted ``no'' on rollcall 
no. 411, the motion to instruct transportation conferees to supersede 
the EPA's authority to permit coal waste disposal sites.
```
