# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
HURRICANE SANDY
Committee on Appropriations: Subcommittee on Department of Homeland 
Security concluded a hearing to examine Hurricane Sandy, focusing on 
response and recovery and progress and challenges, after receiving 
testimony from Senators Schumer, Mikulski, Lieberman, Reed, Carper, 
Cardin, Gillibrand, and Blumenthal; Shaun Donovan, Secretary of Housing 
and Urban Development; and W. Craig Fugate, Administrator, Federal 
Emergency Management Agency, Department of Homeland Security.
MALI
Committee on Foreign Relations: Subcommittee on African Affairs 
concluded a hearing to examine assessing developments in Mali, focusing 
on restoring democracy and reclaiming the north, after receiving 
testimony from Johnnie Carson, Assistant Secretary of State, Bureau of 
African Affairs; Earl Gast, Assistant Administrator, Bureau for Africa, 
United States Agency for International Development; Amanda Dory, Deputy 
Assistant Secretary of Defense for African Affairs, Office of the 
Secretary of Defense; Christopher Fomunyoh, National Democratic 
Institute, Corinne Dufka, Human Rights Watch, and Nii Akuetteh, all of 
Washington, D.C.; and Mohamed Ould Mahmoud, Lobbying Network for Peace, 
Security, and Development for Northern Mali, Bamako.
```
