# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 6, 2012

```
                 ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, DECEMBER 6, 2012

  Mr. CARDIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Thursday, December 6, 2012; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that following the leader remarks, the Senate be in a 
period of morning business until 11:45 a.m., with Senators permitted to 
speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the previous order 
regarding retirement speeches remaining in effect; and that following 
morning business, the Senate proceed to executive session under the 
previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
