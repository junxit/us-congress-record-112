# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that on Monday, 
February 27, 2012, at 4:30 p.m., the Senate proceed to executive 
session to consider the following nomination: Calendar No. 409; that 
there be 60 minutes for debate equally divided in the usual form; that 
upon the use or yielding back of that time, the Senate proceed to vote 
without intervening action or debate on that nomination, the motion to 
reconsider be considered made and laid upon the table, with no 
intervening action or debate, and that there be no further motions in 
order; that any related statements be printed in the Record; that the 
President be immediately notified of the Senate's action, and the 
Senate then resume legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
