# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following any leader remarks, the Senate 
will be in a period of morning business until 11:00 a.m., with the 
Republicans controlling the first half and the majority controlling the 
final half. We will not have the full half hour on each side. It will 
be until 11 a.m. today. My intent is to have the vote on the two 
pending matters; that is, the Furman nomination and the cloture vote on 
the surface transportation bill, beginning at 11 o'clock.
  I ask unanimous consent that the second vote in order of that 
sequence be 10 minutes in duration and that--well, I don't need 
consent; the vote starts at 11 o'clock.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Is there objection? Without 
objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. Following morning business, we will resume consideration of 
the matters that will be before the Senate. At a time to be determined, 
there will be at least two rollcall votes.
  I am sorry, I will rephrase that. We will have other votes, or vote, 
to dispense with the conference report. We have to find out what the 
House does on that matter first.

                          ____________________
```
