# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 21 THROUGH MONDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2012

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 21 THROUGH MONDAY, FEBRUARY 27, 2012

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes its 
business for the day, it adjourn until Tuesday, February 21, at 12 
p.m., and convene for a pro forma session only with no business 
conducted and that following the pro forma session the Senate adjourn 
until Friday, February 24, at 11 a.m. and convene for a pro forma 
session only with no business conducted, and that following the pro 
forma session, the Senate adjourn until 2 p.m., on Monday, February 27; 
that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, and the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day; that 
Senator Shaheen be recognized to deliver Washington's Farewell Address; 
further, that upon the conclusion of the reading, the Senate be in 
morning business until 4:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for 
up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
