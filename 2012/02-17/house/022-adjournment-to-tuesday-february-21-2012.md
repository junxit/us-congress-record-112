# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 21, 2012

**Speaking:** Dreier, David (D000492) [R-CA]

```
                              {time}  1150
               ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 21, 2012

  Mr. DREIER. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House

day, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday, February 27, 2012.

gentleman from California?
  There was no objection.
```
