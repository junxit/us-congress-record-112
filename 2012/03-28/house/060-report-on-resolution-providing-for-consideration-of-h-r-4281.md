# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4281, SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 4281, SURFACE 
                  TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012

  Mr. WEBSTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 112-424) on the resolution (H. Res. 600) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 4281) to provide an extension of 
Federal-aid highway, highway safety, motor carrier safety, transit, and 
other programs funded out of the Highway Trust Fund pending enactment 
of a multiyear law reauthorizing such programs, which was referred to 
the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
