# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 2038

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate announced that on today, March 28, 2012, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bill:

       S. 2038. An act to prohibit Members of Congress and 
     employees of Congress from using nonpublic information 
     derived from their official positions for personal benefit,
```
