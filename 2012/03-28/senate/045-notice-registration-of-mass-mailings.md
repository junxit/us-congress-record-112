# NOTICE: REGISTRATION OF MASS MAILINGS

```
                 NOTICE: REGISTRATION OF MASS MAILINGS

  The filing date for the 2012 first quarter Mass Mailing report is 
Wednesday, April 25, 2012. If your office did no mass mailings during 
this period, please submit a form that states ``none.''
  Mass mailing registrations, or negative reports, should be submitted 
to the Senate Office of Public Records, 232 Hart Building, Washington, 
D.C. 20510-7116.
  The Senate Office of Public Records will be open from 9 a.m. to 6 
p.m. on the filing date to accept these filings. For further 
information, please contact the Senate Office of Public Records at 
(202) 224-0322.

                          ____________________
```
