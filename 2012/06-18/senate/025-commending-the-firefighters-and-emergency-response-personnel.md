# COMMENDING THE FIREFIGHTERS AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE PERSONNEL--USS ``MIAMI'' FIRE

```
   COMMENDING THE FIREFIGHTERS AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE PERSONNEL--USS 
                             ``MIAMI'' FIRE

  Ms. STABENOW. Mr. President, notwithstanding the adoption of S. Res. 
488 and the preamble thereto, I ask unanimous consent that a Snowe 
amendment to the preamble that is at the desk be agreed to.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The amendment (No. 2458) was agreed to, as follows:

       In the fourth whereas clause of the preamble, strike 
     paragraph (18) and insert the following:
       ``(18) Newington Fire Department, New Hampshire;''.
```
