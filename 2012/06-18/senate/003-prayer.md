# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, in Your faithfulness guide our Senators today. As they 
trust Your leadership, may they experience Your faithful love. Lord, 
lead them from the path of disunity, as You teach them Your will. As 
they experience the constancy of Your presence, guide them to Your 
higher wisdom and fill their hearts with Your peace. Watch over them 
with Your gracious protection.
  We pray in Your strong Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
