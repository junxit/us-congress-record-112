# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4348, SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012, PART II

**Speaking:** Walz, Timothy J. (W000799) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 4348

```
NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4348, 
         SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012, PART II

  Mr. WALZ of Minnesota. Madam Speaker, under rule XXII, clause 7(c), I 
hereby announce my intention to offer a motion to instruct on H.R. 
4348, the transportation conference report.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mr. Walz of Minnesota moves that the managers on the part 
     of the House at the conference on the disagreeing votes of 
     the two Houses on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 4348 
     be instructed to resolve all issues and file a conference 
     report not later than June 22, 2012.
```
