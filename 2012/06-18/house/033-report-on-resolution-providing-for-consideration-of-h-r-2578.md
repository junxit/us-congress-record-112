# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2578, CONSERVATION AND ECONOMIC GROWTH ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 688, H.R. 2578

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2578, 
                  CONSERVATION AND ECONOMIC GROWTH ACT

privileged report (Rept. No. 112 539) on the resolution (H. Res. 688) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2578) to amend the Wild

California, and for other purposes, which was referred to the House
```
