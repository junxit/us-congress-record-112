                            NEW PUBLIC LAWS

      (For last listing of Public Laws, see Daily Digest, p. D493)

States. Signed on May 30, 2012. (Public Law 112-122)
  H.R. 5740, to extend the National Flood Insurance Program. Signed on 
May 31, 2012. (Public Law 112-123)
