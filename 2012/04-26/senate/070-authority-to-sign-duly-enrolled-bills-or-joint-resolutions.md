# AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
       AUTHORITY TO SIGN DULY ENROLLED BILLS OR JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that from Thursday, 
April 26, through Monday, May 7, the majority leader be authorized to 
sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
