                              WITHDRAWALS

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on April 
26, 2012 withdrawing from further Senate consideration the following 
nominations:
       THOMAS M. BECK, OF VIRGINIA, TO BE A MEMBER OF THE NATIONAL 
     MEDIATION BOARD FOR A TERM EXPIRING JULY 1, 2013, VICE 
     ELIZABETH DOUGHERTY, TERM EXPIRED, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE 
     SENATE ON JANUARY 5, 2011.

     SENIOR FOREIGN SERVICE, CLASS OF COUNSELOR, TO BE AMBASSADOR 
     EXTRAORDINARY AND PLENIPOTENTIARY OF THE UNITED STATES OF 
     AMERICA TO THE REPUBLIC OF AZERBAIJAN, TO WHICH POSITION HE 
     WAS APPOINTED DURING THE RECESS OF THE SENATE FROM DECEMBER 
     22, 2010, TO JANUARY 5, 2011, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON 
     JANUARY 26, 2011.
