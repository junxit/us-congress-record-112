# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4913

**Measures:** H.R. 4913

```
            By Mr. COFFMAN of Colorado:
        H.R. 4913.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authorities on which this bill rests 
     are:
       The power of Congress ``to make rules for the government 
     and regulation of the land and naval forces'' in Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause 14 of the United States Constitution.
```
