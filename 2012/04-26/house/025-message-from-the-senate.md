# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S. 1789

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       S. 1789. An act to improve, sustain, and transform the 
     United States Postal Service.

  The message also announced that pursuant to section 5 of title I of 
division H of Public Law 110 161, the Chair, on behalf of the Vice 
President, appoints the following Senator as Vice Chairman of the U.S.-
Japan Interparliamentary Group conference for the One Hundred Twelfth 
Congress:
  The Senator from Alaska (Ms. Murkowski).

                          ____________________
```
