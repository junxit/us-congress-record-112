# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen Rossetti, Associate Professor, The Catholic 
University of America, Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, it is Your spirit that guides us into all 
truth and leads us on the straight path. In these challenging days, may 
we be open to being led by this spirit. May we be so docile to Your 
divine guidance that all of us will work together with one heart for 
the betterment of all.
  Finally, we know that one day Your spirit will lead us safely home. 
Buoyed up with this saving knowledge and guided by this same spirit, we 
now step forward into the future with confidence and hope. We make this 
prayer in Your holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
