# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the 
House of the following title:

       H.R. 5986. An act to amend the African Growth and 
     Opportunity Act to extend the third-country fabric program 
     and to add South Sudan to the list of countries eligible for 
     designation under that Act, to make technical corrections to 
     the Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States relating 
     to the textile and apparel rules of origin for the Dominican 
     Republic-Central America-United States Free Trade Agreement, 
     to approve the renewal of import restrictions contained in 
     the Burmese Freedom and Democracy Act of 2003, and for other 
     purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill and 
concurrent resolution of the following titles in which the concurrence 
of the House is requested:

       S. 3510. An act to prevent harm to the national security or 
     endangering the military officers and civilian employees to 
     whom internet publication of certain information applies, and 
     for other purposes.
       S. Con. Res. 56. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     conditional adjournment or recess of the Senate and an 
     adjournment of the House of Representatives.

                          ____________________
```
