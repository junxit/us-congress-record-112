# SENATE BILL AND A JOINT RESOLUTION REFERRED

```
              SENATE BILL AND A JOINT RESOLUTION REFERRED

  A bill and a joint resolution of the Senate of the following titles

follows:

     Government Reform.

     Board of Regents of the Smithsonian Institution; to the
```
