# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10:15 A.M. TOMORROW

```
                 ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10:15 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. If there is no further business to come before the Senate, 
I ask unanimous consent it adjourn under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands 
adjourned until 10:15 a.m. tomorrow, unless the Senate receives a

2012, under the provisions of S. Con. Res. 59.

3, 2012, at 10:15 a.m.
```
