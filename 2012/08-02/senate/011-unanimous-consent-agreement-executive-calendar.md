# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that at 12:50 p.m. 
today, the Senate proceed to executive session to consider Calendar No. 
651; that there be an hour of debate equally divided in the usual form; 
that upon the use or yielding back of that time, the Senate proceed to 
vote with no intervening action or debate on Calendar No. 651, Judge 
Drain of Michigan, at least a judge-to-be in Michigan.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
