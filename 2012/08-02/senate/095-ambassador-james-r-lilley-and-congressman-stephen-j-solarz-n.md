             KOREA HUMAN RIGHTS REAUTHORIZATION ACT OF 2012

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to Calendar No. 458.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the bill by title.

       A bill, (H.R. 4240) to reauthorize the North Korean Human

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask that the bill be read a third time and

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The question is on passage of the bill.
  The bill (H.R. 4240) was passed.
