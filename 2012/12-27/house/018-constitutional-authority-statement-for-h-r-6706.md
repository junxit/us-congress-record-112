# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 6706

**Measures:** H.R. 6706

```
           By Mr. JONES:
       H.R. 6706.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8 of the U.S. Constitution, which gives 
     Congress the power ``to regulate Commerce with foreign 
     Nations, and among the several states''.
```
