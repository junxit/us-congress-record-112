# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1734, CIVILIAN PROPERTY REALIGNMENT ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1734, CIVILIAN 
                        PROPERTY REALIGNMENT ACT

  Mr. WEBSTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 112-385) on the resolution (H. Res. 537) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1734) to decrease the deficit by 
realigning, consolidating, selling, disposing, and improving the 
efficiency of Federal buildings and other civilian real property, and 
for other purposes, which was referred to the House Calendar and 
ordered to be printed.
```
