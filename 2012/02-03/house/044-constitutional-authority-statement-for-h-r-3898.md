# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3898

**Measures:** H.R. 3898

```
            By Mr. KING of Iowa:
        H.R. 3898.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clauses 1 and 2 of Article 1, Section 5, collectively grant 
     Congress the authority to determine the rules of its 
     proceedings and the requirements it chooses to place upon its 
     Members.
```
