# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT ON JUNE 29, 2012

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 51

```
  MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT ON JUNE 29, 2012

  A message from the House of Representatives, delivered by one of its 
reading clerks on June 29, 2012, announced that the House agreed to the 
following concurrent resolution, without amendment:

       S. Con. Res. 51. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     conditional adjournment or recess of the Senate and an
```
