# JUMPSTART OUR BUSINESS STARTUPS

**Speaking:** Schweikert, David (S001183) [R-AZ]

```
                    JUMPSTART OUR BUSINESS STARTUPS

  (Mr. SCHWEIKERT asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SCHWEIKERT. Madam Speaker, this is going to be one of those weeks 
where I believe we can be proud here in the House. We're going to be 
moving forward with a jobs bill we've monikered Jumpstart Our Business 
Startups. I just had in my office a coalition from high-tech companies 
from Arizona, and they unanimously had a story to tell, and that was a 
story of the difficulty in finding capital for moving small companies, 
small organizations, these organizations that are creating jobs.
  I'm particularly blessed this week to have multiple bills in the 
package. One is the Small Company Capital Formation bill, a Private 
Company Flexibility and Growth Act of the six bills that are coming.
  I'm proud of the House. I look forward to these bills moving forward.

                          ____________________
```
