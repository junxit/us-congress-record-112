# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 4059

**Measures:** H.R. 4059

```
           By Mrs. BONO MACK:
       H.R. 4059.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The authority for enactment of this Bill flows from Article 
     I, Section 8, clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution. Congress may 
     prescribe by statute the procedures which are reasonably 
     necessary to effectuate its constitutional purpose of 
     regulating commerce among the several states.
```
