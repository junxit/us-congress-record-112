# NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4348, SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012, PART II

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD]

**Measures:** H.R. 4348

```
NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 4348, 
         SURFACE TRANSPORTATION EXTENSION ACT OF 2012, PART II

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 7(c) of rule XXII, I 
hereby give notice of my intention to offer a motion to instruct 
conferees on H.R. 4348.
  The form of the motion is as follows:

       Mr. Hoyer moves that the managers on the part of the House 
     at the conference on the disagreeing votes of the two Houses 
     on the Senate amendment to the bill H.R. 4348 be instructed 
     to recede from disagreement to the amendment of the Senate.
```
