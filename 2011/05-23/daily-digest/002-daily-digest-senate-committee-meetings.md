# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

**Measures:** S. 413

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
PROTECTING CYBERSPACE
Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs: Committee 
concluded a hearing to examine protecting cyberspace, focusing on 
assessing the White House proposal, including S. 413, to amend the 
Homeland Security Act of 2002 and other laws to enhance the security 
and resiliency of the cyber and communications infrastructure of the 
United States, after receiving testimony from Philip Reitinger, Deputy 
Under Secretary of Homeland Security for National Protection and 
Programs Directorate; Robert J. Butler, Deputy Assistant Secretary of 
Defense for Cyber Policy; Ari Schwartz, Senior Internet Policy Advisor, 
National Institute of Standards and Technology, Department of Commerce; 
and Jason Chipman, Senior Counsel to the Deputy Attorney General, 
Department of Justice.
```
