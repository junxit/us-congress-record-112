# Text of House Amendment 55

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 55: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act 
     under the heading ``Defense Health Program'' may be used in 
     contravention of chapter 55 of title 10, United States Code.
```
