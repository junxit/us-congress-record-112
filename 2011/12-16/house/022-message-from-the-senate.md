# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.Con.Res. 93, S. 1612, H.R. 2845, H.R. 3421

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill and a concurrent 
resolution of the House of the following tiles:

       H.R. 3421. An act to award Congressional Gold Medals in 
     honor of the men and women who perished as a result of the 
     terrorist attacks on the United States on September 11, 2001.
       H. con. Res. 93. Concurrent resolution providing for a 
     correction to the enrollment of the bill H.R. 2845.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested.

       S. 1612. An act to provide the Department of Justice with 
     additional tools to target extraterritorial drug trafficking 
     activity.

                          ____________________
```
