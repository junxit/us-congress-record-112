# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 1249

```
               MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--H.R. 1249

  Mr. REID. Madam President, H.R. 1249 is due for a second reading. I 
ask the clerk to report.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will read the bill by 
title for the second time.
  The legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (H.R. 1249) to amend title 35, United States Code, 
     to provide for patent reform.

  Mr. REID. I now object to any further proceedings on this bill at 
this time.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Objection is heard. The bill will 
be placed on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV.

                          ____________________
```
