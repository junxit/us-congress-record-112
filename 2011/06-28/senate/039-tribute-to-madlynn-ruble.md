# TRIBUTE TO MADLYNN RUBLE

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO MADLYNN RUBLE

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Madlynn Ruble, an 
intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Madlynn is a graduate of Albert Lea Senior High School in Albert Lea, 
MN. Currently she is attending the University of South Dakota where she 
is majoring in Spanish and communications. She is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Madlynn for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
