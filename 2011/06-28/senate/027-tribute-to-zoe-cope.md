# TRIBUTE TO ZOE COPE

```
                          TRIBUTE TO ZOE COPE

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Zoe Cope, an 
intern in my Aberdeen, SD, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Zoe is a native of Aberdeen and a graduate of Roncalli High School. 
Currently, she is attending the University of Nebraska-Lincoln where 
she is pursuing a degree in architecture. She is a very hard worker who 
has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Zoe for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
