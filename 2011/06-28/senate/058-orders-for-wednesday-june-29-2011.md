# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 29, 2011

```
                  ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, JUNE 29, 2011

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Wednesday, 
June 29; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed to a period 
of morning business for up to 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak 
for up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and controlled 
between the two leaders or their designees, with the Republicans 
controlling the first half and the majority controlling the final half; 
that following morning business, the Senate resume consideration of S. 
679, the Presidential Appointment Efficiency and Streamlining Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
