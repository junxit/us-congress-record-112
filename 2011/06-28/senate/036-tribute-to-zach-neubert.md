# TRIBUTE TO ZACH NEUBERT

```
                        TRIBUTE TO ZACH NEUBERT

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Zach Neubert, an 
intern in my Aberdeen, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has done 
for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past several 
months.
  Zach is a native of Aberdeen and a graduate of Central High School. 
Currently, he is attending the Northern State University where he is 
studying history and political science. He is a very hard worker who 
has been dedicated to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Zach for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●
```
