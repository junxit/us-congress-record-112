# TRIBUTE TO RACHEL SKEA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO RACHEL SKEA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Rachel Skea, an 
intern in my Rapid City, SD, office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
couple months.
  Rachel is a graduate of Rapid City Christian High School in Rapid 
City, SD. Currently, she is attending Wheaton College in Wheaton, IL, 
where she is majoring in communications and media. She is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Rachel for all of the 
fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
