# TRIBUTE TO KYLE HANSON

```
                         TRIBUTE TO KYLE HANSON

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Kyle Hanson, an 
intern in my Washington, DC, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several months.
  Kyle graduated from South Dakota State University with a major in 
communication studies. In the fall, he will be attending law school at 
the University of South Dakota. He is a hard worker who has been 
dedicated to getting the most out of his internship experience.
  I extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Kyle for all of the 
fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the years to 
come.●

                          ____________________
```
