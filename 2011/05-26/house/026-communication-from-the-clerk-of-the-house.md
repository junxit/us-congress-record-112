# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 13, S. 1082

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                                     May 26, 2011.
     Hon. John A. Boehner,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives, Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in Clause 
     2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of

       That the Senate passed S. 1082.
       That the Senate agreed to S. Con. Res. 13.

       Advisory Committee on the Records of Congress.

     Karen L. Haas.
```
