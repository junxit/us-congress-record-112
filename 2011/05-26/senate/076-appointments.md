# APPOINTMENTS

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Republican leader, 
after consultation with the Chairman of the Select Committee on 
Intelligence of the Senate, and pursuant to the provisions of Public 
Law 107-306, as amended by Public Law 111-259, announces the 
appointment of the following individual to serve as a member of the 
National Commission for the Review of the Research and Development 
Programs of the United States Intelligence Community: John H. Young, of 
Virginia.
  The Chair, on behalf of the President of the Senate, and after 
consultation with the Republican leader, pursuant to Public Law 106-
286, appoints the following Members to serve on the Congressional-
Executive Commission on the People's Republic of China: the Honorable 
Susan Collins of Maine, and the Honorable James E. Risch of Idaho.
```
