# SIGNING AUTHORITY

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that from 
Thursday, May 26, through Friday, June 3, the majority leader, Senator 
Klobuchar, and Senator Webb be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills 
or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Ms. KLOBUCHAR. Mr. President, I note the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
```
