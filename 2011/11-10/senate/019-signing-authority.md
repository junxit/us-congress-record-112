# SIGNING AUTHORITY

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mrs. BOXER. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that from 
Thursday, November 10, through Monday, November 14, the majority leader 
be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
