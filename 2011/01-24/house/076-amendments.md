# AMENDMENTS

**Measures:** H.Res. 38, H.R. 359

```
                               AMENDMENTS

  Under clause 8 of rule XVIII, proposed amendments were submitted as 
follows:

                                H.R. 359

                         Offered By: Mr. Peters

       Amendment No. 1: Page 2, line 23, strike ``Treasury.'' and 
     insert ``Treasury, to be used only for reducing the 
     deficit.''.

                               H. Res. 38

                         Offered By: Mr. Jordan

       Amendment No. 1: Strike: ``for the remainder of fiscal year 
     2011 that assumes non-security spending at fiscal year 2008 
     levels or less.''
       Add: ``that provides a total non-security fiscal year 2011 
     allocation that is at least $100 billion less than provided 
     for in House Report 111-565.''
```
