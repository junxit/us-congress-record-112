# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business for 1 hour, the time 
equally divided, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each. The majority will control the first half and the 
Republicans will control the second half.
  The Senator from Illinois.
```
