# EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF COMMITTEE

```
                     EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF COMMITTEE

  The following executive reports of nominations were submitted:

       By Mr. BAUCUS for the Committee on Finance.
       *Kathleen Kerrigan, of Massachusetts, to be a Judge of the 
     United States Tax Court for the term of fifteen years.
       *Mary John Miller, of Maryland, to be an Under Secretary of 
     the Treasury.
       *Henry J. Aaron, of the District of Columbia, to be a 
     Member of the Social Security Advisory Board for a term 
     expiring September 30, 2014.
       *Alastair M. Fitzpayne, of Maryland, to be a Deputy Under 
     Secretary of the Treasury.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S8775, December 17, 2011, under EXECUTIVE REPORTS OF 
COMMITTEE, the Record reads as follows: *Henry J. Aaron, of the 
District of Columbia, to be a Member of the Social Security 
Advisory Board for a term expiring September 30, 2014.
  
  The online Record has been corrected to reads: *Henry J. Aaron, 
of the District of Columbia, to be a Member of the Social Security 
Advisory Board for a term expiring September 30, 2014. *Alastair 
M. Fitzpayne, of Maryland, to be a Deputy Under Secretary of the 
Treasury.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


  *Nomination was reported with recommendation that it be confirmed 
subject to the nominee's commitment to respond to requests to appear 
and testify before any duly constituted committee of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
