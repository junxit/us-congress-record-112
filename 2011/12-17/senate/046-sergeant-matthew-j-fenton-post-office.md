# SERGEANT MATTHEW J. FENTON POST OFFICE

```
                 SERGEANT MATTHEW J. FENTON POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 789) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 20 Main Street in Little Ferry, New Jersey, 
as the ``Sergeant Matthew J. Fenton Post Office,'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
