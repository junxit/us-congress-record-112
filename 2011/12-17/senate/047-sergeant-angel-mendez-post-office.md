# SERGEANT ANGEL MENDEZ POST OFFICE

```
                   SERGEANT ANGEL MENDEZ POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 2422) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 45 Bay Street, Suite 2, in Staten Island, New 
York, as the ``Sergeant Angel Mendez Post Office,'' was ordered to a 
third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
