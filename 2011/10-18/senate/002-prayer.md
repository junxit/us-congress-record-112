# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal Lord God, our shelter in the time of storm, You have guided 
our Nation through many seasons of danger and duress. In these 
challenging economic times, give our lawmakers the wisdom they need to 
make a positive difference in the lives of our citizens. Help them to 
see that without wise and prompt action, multitudes will face a future 
of privation and uncertainty.
  Lord, use our Senators today to make America all You intend for it to 
be.
  We pray in Your loving Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
