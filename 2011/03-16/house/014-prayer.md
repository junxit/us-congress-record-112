# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:

[[Page H1849]]

  Come and help us, Lord our God. Those wounded returning from war are 
not afraid to submit themselves to physicians. In this humility, they 
live patience and offer You alone the glory. By their being faithful to 
the course outlined for them, they learn that it takes many small steps 
to make full recovery.
  In the same light, O Lord, grant this Nation patience. Give this 
representative government wise discernment and courageous action to 
excise whatever poisons the whole system while preserving each healthy 
member.
  As we pray today, we ask You, Divine Physician, to bless, sustain and 
reward the Navy medical team that cares for Members, staff and guests 
here on Capitol Hill. With them, may we give full measure as we serve 
in Your holy name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
