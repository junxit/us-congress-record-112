# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business until 10:30 a.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders, or their 
designees, with the majority controlling the first half of the time, 
and the Republicans controlling the final half.
  The Senator from Maryland is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
