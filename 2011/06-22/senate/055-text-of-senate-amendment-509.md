# Text of Senate Amendment 509

**Speaking:** Portman, Rob (P000449) [R-OH]

```
  SA 509. Mr. PORTMAN (for himself, Mr. Udall of New Mexico, and Mr. 
Cornyn) submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by him to the 
bill S. 679, to reduce the number of executive positions subject to 
Senate confirmation; as follows:

       On page 76, after line 6, add the following:
       (c) Provisions Not Taking Effect.--Notwithstanding any 
     other provision of this Act, the amendments made by section 
     2(c)(2) through (6), (u), and (ll) shall not take effect.
                                 ______
```
