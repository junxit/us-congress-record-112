# CONGRATULATING MASON DOWDY FOR OBTAINING THE RANK OF EAGLE SCOUT

```
    CONGRATULATING MASON DOWDY FOR OBTAINING THE RANK OF EAGLE SCOUT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SANDY ADAMS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 22, 2011

  Mrs. ADAMS. Mr. Speaker, I would like to congratulate Mason Dowdy for 
achieving the rank of Eagle Scout.
  For his Eagle Scout project, Mason provided trail markers for a youth 
ranch to help hundreds of young adults who re-enact a pioneer 
lifestyle. Throughout the history of the Boy Scouts of America, the 
rank of Eagle Scout has only been attained through dedication to 
concepts such as honor, duty, country and charity. By applying these 
concepts to daily life, Mason has proven his true and complete 
understanding of their meanings, and thereby deserves this honor.
  I offer my congratulations on a job well done and best wishes for the 
future.

                          ____________________
```
