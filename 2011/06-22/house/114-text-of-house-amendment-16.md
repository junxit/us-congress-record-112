# Text of House Amendment 16

**Speaking:** Burton, Dan (B001149) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                   Offered By: Mr. Burton of Indiana

       Amendment No. 16: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to make a contribution to the military budget of the 
     North Atlantic Treaty Organization in excess of $408,100,000.
```
