# Text of House Amendment 11

**Speaking:** Christensen, Donna M. (C000380) [D-VI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                      Offered By: Mrs. Christensen

       Amendment No. 11: Page 124, after line 23, insert the 
     following:
       Sec. __.  The Secretary of Defense, in coordination with 
     the Secretary of Health and Human Services and the Secretary 
     of Veterans Affairs, shall develop a lung cancer mortality 
     reduction program for members of the Armed Forces and 
     veterans whose smoking history and exposure to carcinogens 
     during active duty service has increased their risk for lung 
     cancer and shall implement a program of coordinated care for 
     members of the Armed Forces and veterans diagnosed with lung 
     cancer.
```
