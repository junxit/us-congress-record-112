# Text of House Amendment 30

**Speaking:** Flores, Bill (F000461) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                         Offered By: Mr. Flores

       Amendment No. 30: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to enforce section 526 of the Energy Independence and 
     Security Act of 2007 (Public Law 110-140; 42 U.S.C. 17142).
```
