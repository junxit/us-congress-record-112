# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

  Mr. MILLER of Florida. Mr. Speaker, due to a family commitment, I 
missed rollcall Vote Nos. 411 and 412 on June 3, 2011. If present, I 
would have voted: rollcall Vote No. 411--Declaring that the President 
shall not deploy, establish, or maintain the presence of units and 
members of the United States Armed Forces on the ground in Libya, and 
for other purposes, ``aye;'' rollcall Vote No. 412--Directing the 
President, pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution, to 
remove the United States Armed Forces from Libya, ``aye.''
```
