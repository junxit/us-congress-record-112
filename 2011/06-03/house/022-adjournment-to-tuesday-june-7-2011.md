# ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, JUNE 7, 2011

**Speaking:** Kingston, Jack (K000220) [R-GA]

```
                  ADJOURNMENT TO TUESDAY, JUNE 7, 2011

  Mr. KINGSTON. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. on Tuesday, June 7, 
2011; when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 10:30 
a.m. on Thursday, June 9, 2011; and when the House adjourns on that 
day, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday, June 13, 2011.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Georgia?
  There was no objection.
```
