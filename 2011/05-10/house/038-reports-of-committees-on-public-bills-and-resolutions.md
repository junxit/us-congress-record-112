# REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** H.Res. 257, H.R. 1231

```
         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. REED: Committee on Rules. House Resolution 257. 
     Resolution providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 
     1231) to amend the Outer Continental Shelf Lands Act to 
     require that each 5-year offshore oil and gas leasing program 
     offer leasing in the areas with the most prospective oil and 
     gas resources, to establish a domestic oil and natural gas 
     production goal, and for other purposes (Rept. 112-74). 
     Referred to the House Calendar.
```
