# PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 856

**Speaking:** Heck, Joseph J. (H001055) [R-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 856

```
  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 856

  Mr. HECK. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may hereafter 
be considered as the first sponsor of H.R. 856, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Heller of Nevada, for the purposes of 
adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Nevada?
  There was no objection.
```
