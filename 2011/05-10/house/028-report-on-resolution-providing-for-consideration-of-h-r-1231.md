# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1231, REVERSING PRESIDENT OBAMA'S OFFSHORE MORATORIUM ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 257, H.R. 1231

```
    REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1231, 
          REVERSING PRESIDENT OBAMA'S OFFSHORE MORATORIUM ACT

  Mr. BISHOP of Utah, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a 
privileged report (Rept. No. 112-74) on the resolution (H. Res. 257) 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1231) to amend the Outer 
Continental Shelf Lands Act to require that each 5-year offshore oil 
and gas leasing program offer leasing in the areas with the most 
prospective oil and gas resources, to establish a domestic oil and 
natural gas production goal, and for other purposes, which was referred 
to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
