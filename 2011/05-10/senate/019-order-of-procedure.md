# ORDER OF PROCEDURE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                           ORDER OF PROCEDURE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that at 2:15 today 
the Senate proceed to executive session and begin consideration of 
Executive Calendar No. 61, the nomination of Edward Chen of California 
to be U.S. District Judge for the Northern District of California under 
the previous order.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. REID. Madam President, there will be 3 hours of debate on the 
Chen nomination beginning at 2:15 p.m. today. Senators can expect a 
rollcall vote on the Chen nomination at approximately 5:15 p.m. today.
  I yield the floor.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Arkansas.

                          ____________________
```
