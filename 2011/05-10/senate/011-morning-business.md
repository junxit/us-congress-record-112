# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business for debate only until 5 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each, with the first hour equally divided and controlled between the 
two leaders or their designees, with the majority controlling the first 
30 minutes and the Republicans controlling the next 30 minutes.
  The Senator from Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
