# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MAY 11, 2011

```
                         ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY,
                              MAY 11, 2011

  Mr. BENNET. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on 
Wednesday, May 11; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be

[[Page 6920]]

deemed expired, the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed 
to a period for the transaction of morning business for debate only 
until 2 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each, with the first hour equally divided and controlled by the 
leaders or their designees, with the Republicans controlling the first 
30 minutes and the majority controlling the next 30 minutes; that 
following morning business, the Senate proceed to executive session 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
