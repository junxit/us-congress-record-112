# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the period for 
morning business be extended until 7 p.m., with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak in 
morning business for 25 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
