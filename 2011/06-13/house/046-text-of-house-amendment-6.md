# Text of House Amendment 6

**Speaking:** Flores, Bill (F000461) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2055

```
H.R. 2055

                         Offered By: Mr. Flores

       Amendment No. 6: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. 4__.  None of the funds made available by this Act 
     shall be available to enforce section 526 of the Energy 
     Independence and Security Act of 2007 (Public Law 110-140; 42 
     U.S.C. 17142).
```
