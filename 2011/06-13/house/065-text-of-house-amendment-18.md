# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Nugent, Richard B. (N000185) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2112

```
H.R. 2112

                         Offered By: Mr. Nugent

       Amendment No. 18: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to close or dispose of (or to pay the salaries and 
     expenses of personnel of the Department of Agriculture to 
     close or dispose of) any Agricultural Research Service 
     facility that conducts beef cattle research.
```
