LIBYA AND OPERATION UNIFIED PROTECTOR
Committee on Armed Services: On Friday, June 10, 2011, Committee 
received a closed briefing on the situation in Libya and Operation 
Unified Protector from Michele A. Flournoy, Under Secretary for Policy, 
and Lieutenant General Charles H. Jacoby, Director, Strategic Plans and 
Policy, J5, Joint Staff, both of the Department of Defense; and Major 
General John R. Landry, USA (Ret.), National Intelligence Officer for 
Military Issues, and Alan R. Pino, National Intelligence Officer for 
the Near East, both of the National Intelligence Council.
