# Text of House Amendment 52

**Speaking:** Gosar, Paul A. (G000565) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2354

```
H.R. 2354

                         Offered By: Mr. Gosar

       Amendment No. 52: Insert after section 607 the following 
     new section:
       Sec. 608.  None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be expended to administer or enforce the requirements of 
     subchapter IV of chapter 31 or title 40, United States Code 
     (commonly referred to as the Davis-Bacon Act), except with 
     respect to a contract that exceeds $20,000,000.
       Page 61, line 22, strike ``SEC. 608'' and insert ``SEC. 
     609''.
```
