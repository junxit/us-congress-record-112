# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** H.R. 2608

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, the Senate will be 
in a period of morning business until 4:30 p.m. At that time the Senate 
will resume consideration of the motion to concur in the House message 
to accompany H.R. 2608, the continuing resolution. The filing deadline 
for second-degree amendments to the House message is 5 p.m. At 5:30 
p.m. there will be a cloture vote on the House message with the Reid of 
Nevada amendment.
```
