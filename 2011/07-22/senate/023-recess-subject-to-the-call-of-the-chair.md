# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```

  Mr. HATCH. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that we recess 
subject to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 2:16 p.m., recessed subject 
to the call of the Chair and reassembled at 2:21 p.m. when called to 
order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Manchin).
  Mr. NELSON of Florida. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to 
speak as in morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
