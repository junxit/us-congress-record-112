# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will now be in a period of morning business until 10:30 a.m., 
with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes, with the 
time equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees.
```
