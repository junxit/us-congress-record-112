# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2403

**Measures:** H.R. 2403

```
            By Mr. WELCH:
        H.R. 2403.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 1, the power to lay and 
     collect taxes, duties, imposts and excise, to pay the debts 
     and provide for the common defense and general welfare of the 
     United States.
```
