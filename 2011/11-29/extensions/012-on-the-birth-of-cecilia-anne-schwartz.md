# ON THE BIRTH OF CECILIA ANNE SCHWARTZ

**Speaking:** Wilson, Joe (W000795) [R-SC]

```
                 ON THE BIRTH OF CECILIA ANNE SCHWARTZ

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 29, 2011

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am happy to congratulate 
Lawrence Schwartz and his wife Allison on the birth of their new baby 
girl, Cecilia Anne Schwartz, who was born on Monday, November 28, 2011, 
at 11:26 a.m. in Falls Church, Virginia. Cecilia Anne was 5 pounds, 6 
ounces.
  I am so excited for this new blessing to the Schwartz family and wish 
them all the best. I want to also congratulate Cecilia Anne's 
grandparents Debra and Barry Shulman of Fayetteville, New York, and 
Joanne and Lawrence Schwartz, III, of Anaheim Hills, California, on 
this wonderful new addition to their family.

                          ____________________
```
