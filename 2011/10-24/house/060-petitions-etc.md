# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.R. 2116

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       25. The SPEAKER presented a petition of City and County of 
     Honolulu, Hawaii, relative to Resolution No. 11-231, CD1 
     urging the Congress and the President to support and pass 
     H.R. 2116; to the Committee on the Judiciary.
       26. Also, a petition of the City of Miami, Florida, 
     relative to Resolution No. 11-0334 urging the Congress to 
     support the retention of the Low-Income Housing Credit 
     Program; to the Committee on Ways and Means.
```
