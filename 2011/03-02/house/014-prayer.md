# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God of the Universe, Our Hope in times of testing, Our 
Consolation and Strength always, while this Chamber goes about its work 
to establish security and good order for the Nation, breathe forth a 
new Spirit of creativity and learning in the hearts of Your people and 
guide the course of world events.
  By drawing closer to Your Holy Will and revealed Word, may the hidden 
treasures of lasting freedom empower Your people to seek the truth and 
do what is right in conscience so we may witness to Your presence in 
our midst both now and forever.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
