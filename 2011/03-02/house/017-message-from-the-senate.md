# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.J.Res. 44, S. 388

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has agreed to without amendment a Joint Resolution of 
the House of the following title:

       H.J. Res. 44. Joint resolution making further continuing 
     appropriations for fiscal year 2011, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 388. An act to prohibit Members of Congress and the 
     President from receiving pay during Government shutdowns.

                          ____________________
```
