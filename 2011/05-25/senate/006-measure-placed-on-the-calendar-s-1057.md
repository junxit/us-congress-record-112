# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1057

  Mr. REID. Madam President, S. 1057 is at the desk. It is due for a 
second reading.

the bill for the second time.

       A bill (S. 1057) to repeal the Volumetric Ethanol Excise

  Mr. REID. I would object to any further proceedings with regard to
