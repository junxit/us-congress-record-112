# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10:30 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 14

```
                 ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10:30 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. Madam President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that it adjourn under the 
previous order and under the provisions of S. Res. 14, as a further 
mark of respect for the victims and heroes of the tragedy in Tucson, 
AZ.

[[Page S293]]

Thursday, January 27, 2011, at 10:30 a.m.
```
