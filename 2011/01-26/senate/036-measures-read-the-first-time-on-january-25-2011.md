# MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME ON JANUARY 25, 2011

**Measures:** H.R. 2, S. 162, S. 163

```
            MEASURES READ THE FIRST TIME ON JANUARY 25, 2011

  The following bills were read the first time:

       H.R. 2. An act to repeal the job-killing health care law 
     and health care-related provisions in the Health Care and 
     Education Reconciliation Act of 2010.
       S. 162. A bill to cut $500,000,000,000 in spending in 
     fiscal year 2011.
       S. 163. A bill to require that the Government prioritize 
     all obligations on the debt held by the public in the event 
     that the debt limit is reached.
```
