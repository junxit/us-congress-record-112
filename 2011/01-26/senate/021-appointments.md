# APPOINTMENTS

**Speaking:** Begich, Mark (B001265) [D-AK], Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
                              APPOINTMENTS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The President pro tempore of the Senate and 
the Speaker of the House of Representatives, pursuant to the provisions 
of section 201(a)(2) of the Congressional Budget Act of 1974, have 
appointed Dr. Douglas W. Elmendorf as Director of the Congressional 
Budget Office for the term expiring January 3, 2015.
  The Chair, on behalf of the Republican leader, pursuant to Public Law 
111-25, announces the appointment of the following individual to serve 
as a member of the Ronald Reagan Centennial Commission for the life of 
the commission: The Honorable Orrin Hatch of Utah vice Robert Bennett.
  Mr. BEGICH. Madam President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. INHOFE. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
