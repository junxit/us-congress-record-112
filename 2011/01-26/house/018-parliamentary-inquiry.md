# PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

**Speaking:** Weiner, Anthony D. (W000792) [D-NY]

**Measures:** H.Res. 54

```
                         PARLIAMENTARY INQUIRY

  Mr. WEINER. Mr. Speaker, I rise for a parliamentary inquiry.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The gentleman may inquire.
  Mr. WEINER. Mr. Speaker, on the bill we're going to be considering 
shortly, the Presidential checkoff bill, there's a requirement under 
the rules that the amendments be printed in the Record. Is that Record 
available?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair understands that the printed 
Record is not yet available.
  Mr. WEINER. Further inquiry, does the Speaker have any guidance for 
the House on when that Record might be available so we can read what 
we're going to be considering in a matter of minutes?
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair does not currently have that 
information. Under the terms of House Resolution 54, any issue would 
become ripe when the amendment process begins.
  Mr. WEINER. Thank you, Mr. Speaker.

                          ____________________
```
