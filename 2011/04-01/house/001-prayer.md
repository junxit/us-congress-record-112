# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, to Whom we are all accountable, You are also our source of 
freedom since we are created in Your image.
  We praise You and thank You for the blessing to work here in Congress 
and serve You, Your people, and this Nation.
  During this weekend, help us to enjoy the new life of spring and the 
gifts of family and friends. By drawing closer to beauty and love, mold 
us by Your spirit to be more fully Your free people and so a blessing 
to others both next week and forever.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
