# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing has been 
scheduled before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural Resources. 
The hearing will be held on Thursday, March 31, 2011, at 9:30 a.m., in 
room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building.
  The purpose of the hearing will be to hear testimony on three items:
  S. 629, to improve hydropower, and for other purposes.
  S. 630, to promote marine and hydrokinetic renewable energy research 
and development, and for other purposes.
  Title I, subtitle D of the American Clean Energy Leadership Act of 
2009 (S. 1462 from 111th Congress).
  Because of the limited time available for the hearing, witnesses may 
testify by invitation only. However, those wishing to submit written 
testimony for the hearing record may do so by sending it to the 
Committee on Energy and Natural Resources, United States Senate, 
Washington, D.C. 20510-6150, or by e-mail to Meagan_Gins@energy
.senate.gov.
  For further information, please contact Tanya Trujillo at (202) 224-
5479 or Meagan Gins at (202) 224-0883.

                          ____________________
```
