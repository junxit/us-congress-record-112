# MATTHEW A. PUCINO POST OFFICE

```
                     MATTHEW A. PUCINO POST OFFICE

  The bill (H.R. 2062) to designate the facility of the United States 
Postal Service located at 45 Meetinghouse Lane in Sagamore Beach, 
Massachusetts, as the ``Matthew A. Pucino Post Office,'' which was 
ordered to a third reading, was read the third time, and passed.
```
