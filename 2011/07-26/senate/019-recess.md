# RECESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate stands in 
recess until 2:15 p.m. today.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 12:46 p.m., recessed until 2:15 p.m., and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Webb).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
