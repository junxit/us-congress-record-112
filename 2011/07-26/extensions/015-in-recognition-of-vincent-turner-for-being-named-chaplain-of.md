# IN RECOGNITION OF VINCENT TURNER FOR BEING NAMED CHAPLAIN OF THE YEAR BY THE UNITED STATES CORPS OF CHAPLAINS

**Speaking:** Rogers, Mike D. (R000575) [R-AL]

```
 IN RECOGNITION OF VINCENT TURNER FOR BEING NAMED CHAPLAIN OF THE YEAR 
                BY THE UNITED STATES CORPS OF CHAPLAINS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, July 26, 2011

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I would like to pay tribute to a 
special Chaplain today, Vincent Turner.
  Chaplain (Second Lieutenant) Turner of Alabama was named Chaplain of 
the Year by the United States Corps of Chaplains for 2011.

[[Page E1411]]

  Turner has always been committed to helping others and serving God 
and country both through the United States Corps of Chaplains and the 
American Legion and Disabled Veterans.
  I'm proud to congratulate Chaplain Turner on this great achievement 
and hope he will continue his service to God, to the United States and 
helping people in need. Congratulations, Chaplain Turner.

                          ____________________
```
