# Text of House Amendment 70

**Speaking:** Gohmert, Louie (G000552) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                        Offered By: Mr. Gohmert

       Amendment No. 70: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under title II 
     (relating to the Environmental Protection Agency) may be used 
     for the new construction, purchase, or lease of any facility 
     land, or space except if such construction, purchase, or 
     lease is performed pursuant to a contract entered into before 
     the date of the enactment of this Act.
```
