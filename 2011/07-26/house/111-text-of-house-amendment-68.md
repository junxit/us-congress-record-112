# Text of House Amendment 68

**Speaking:** Holt, Rush (H001032) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                          Offered By: Mr. Holt

       Amendment No. 68: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by reducing the amount made available for ``Bureau of 
     Land Management-Management of Lands and Resources'', and 
     increasing the amount made available for ``United States 
     Geological Survey-Surveys, Investigations, and Research'', by 
     $15,929,000 and $13,929,000, respectively.
```
