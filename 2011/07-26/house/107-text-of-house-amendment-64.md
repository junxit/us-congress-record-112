# Text of House Amendment 64

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                        Offered By: Mr. Lankford

       Amendment No. 64: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used to establish, issue, evaluate, or implement 
     proposed regulations (Existing Facilities Rule) on the 
     location, design, construction, and capacity of water intake 
     structures under section 316(b) of the Federal Water 
     Pollution Control Act.
```
