# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2661

**Measures:** H.R. 2661

```
           By Mr. OWENS:
       H.R. 2661.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Commerce Clause (Art. I, Sec. 8, cl. 3) of the United 
     States Constitution provides that the Congress shall have the 
     power to regulate interstate and foreign commerce.
```
