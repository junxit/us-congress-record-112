# Text of House Amendment 67

**Speaking:** Holt, Rush (H001032) [D-NJ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                          Offered By: Mr. Holt

       Amendment No. 67: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


            funding limitation related to leasing activities

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used to conduct oil or natural gas preleasing, 
     leasing, or related activities in the North and Mid-Atlantic 
     planning areas.
```
