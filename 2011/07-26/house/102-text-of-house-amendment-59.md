# Text of House Amendment 59

**Speaking:** Rigell, E. Scott (R000589) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                         Offered By: Mr. Rigell

       Amendment No. 59: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


           funding limitation related to acquisition of land

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to prepare, install, or manage a transit system for 
     access to Chincoteague National Wildlife Refuge.
```
