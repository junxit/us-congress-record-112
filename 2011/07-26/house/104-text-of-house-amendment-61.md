# Text of House Amendment 61

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                        Offered By: Mr. Lankford

       Amendment No. 61: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to promulgate, implement, administer, or enforce any 
     Federal implementation plan under the Clean Air Act (42 
     U.S.C. 7401 et seq.) that imposes any standard or requirement 
     under subpart P of part 51 of title 40, Code of Federal 
     Regulations.
```
