# Text of House Amendment 62

**Speaking:** Lankford, James (L000575) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                        Offered By: Mr. Lankford

       Amendment No. 62: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


              limitation of funds related to buffer zones

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to create a protective perimeter or buffer zone 
     around an area owned or managed by the National Park Service 
     or the Department of Interior.
```
