# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2658

**Measures:** H.R. 2658

```
           By Mr. DANIEL E. LUNGREN of California:
       H.R. 2658.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The constitutional authority on which this bill rests is 
     the Commerce Clause of Article 1, Section 8 and to provide 
     for the common defense also in Article 1, Section 8.
```
