# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senate will be in a period of 
morning business for debate only until 12 p.m. with Senators permitted 
to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the first hour equally 
divided and controlled between the two leaders or their designees, with 
the majority controlling the first 30 minutes and the Republicans 
controlling the next 30 minutes.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senator from Illinois.

                          ____________________
```
