# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 5, 2011

```
                    ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MAY 5, 2011

  Mr. MERKLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Thursday, May 5; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and that following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed to a 
period of morning business for debate only until 5 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the first 
hour equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or their 
designees, with the Republicans controlling the first 30 minutes and 
the majority controlling the next 30 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
