# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, the filing deadline for all second-
degree amendments to S. 493, the small business jobs bill, is at 11 
a.m. There will be up to two rollcall votes at noon. The first rollcall 
vote will be on the motion to invoke cloture on S. 493, the small 
business jobs bill. If cloture is not invoked on the bill, the Senate 
will immediately proceed to a second vote on the motion to invoke 
cloture on the nomination of John McConnell to be U.S. District Judge 
for the District of Rhode Island.
```
