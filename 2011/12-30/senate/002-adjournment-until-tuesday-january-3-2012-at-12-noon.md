# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, JANUARY 3, 2012, AT 12 NOON

```
         ADJOURNMENT UNTIL TUESDAY, JANUARY 3, 2012, AT 12 NOON

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands adjourned until Tuesday, January 3, 2012, and will 
reconvene for the Second Session of the 112th Congress at 12 noon.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 34 seconds a.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, January 3, 2012, at 12 noon.
```
