  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider Calendar No. 290; that the 
nomination be confirmed, the motion to reconsider be considered made 
and laid upon the table, with no intervening action or debate; that any 
statements be printed in the Record; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action; and that the Senate then resume 
legislative session.
