# BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILLS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House reports that on October 06, 2011 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bills.

       H.R. 771. To designate the facility of the United States 
     Postal Service located at 1081 Elbel Road in Schertz, Texas, 
     as the ``Schertz Veterans Post Office.''
       H.R. 1632. To designate the facility of the United States 
     Postal Service located at 5014 Gary Avenue in Lubbock, Texas, 
     as the ``Sergeant Chris Davis Post Office.''
```
