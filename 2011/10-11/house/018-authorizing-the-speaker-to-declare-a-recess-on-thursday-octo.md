# AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, OCTOBER 13, 2011, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY LEE MYUNG-BAK, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOREA

```
 AUTHORIZING THE SPEAKER TO DECLARE A RECESS ON THURSDAY, OCTOBER 13, 
2011, FOR THE PURPOSE OF RECEIVING IN JOINT MEETING HIS EXCELLENCY LEE 
             MYUNG-BAK, PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC OF KOREA

  Mr. MILLER of Florida. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that it 
may be in order at any time on Thursday, October 13, 2011, for the 
Speaker to declare a recess, subject to the call of the Chair, for the 
purpose of receiving in joint meeting His Excellency Lee Myung-bak, 
President of the Republic of Korea.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Florida?
  There was no objection.
```
