# Daily Digest/House Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
HYDROCEPHALUS TREATMENT IN UGANDA
Committee on Foreign Affairs: Subcommittee on Africa, Global Health, 
and Human Rights held a hearing on Hydrocephalus Treatment in Uganda: 
Leading the Way to Help Children. Testimony was heard from public 
witnesses.

Joint Meetings
RUSSIAN AND U.S. FIGHT AGAINST ALCOHOLISM
Commission on Security and Cooperation in Europe: Commission received a 
briefing on Russian-United States cooperation in the fight against 
alcoholism, focusing on prospects for sharing experience, strength, and 
hope on treating alcoholism from Margaret Murray, Director, 
International Research Program, National Institute on Alcohol Abuse and 
Alcoholism, National Institutes of Health; and Eugene Zubkov, House of 
Hope on a Hill, and Heidi Brown, Kroll Associates, both of New York, 
New York.
```
