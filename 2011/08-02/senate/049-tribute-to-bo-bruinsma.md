# TRIBUTE TO BO BRUINSMA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                         TRIBUTE TO BO BRUINSMA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Bo Bruinsma, an 
intern in my Sioux Falls, SD, office, for all of the hard work he has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
several weeks.
  Bo is a graduate of Elk Point-Jefferson High School in South Dakota. 
Currently he is attending the University of South Dakota, where he is 
majoring in political science and mass communications with a Spanish 
minor. He is a hard worker who has been dedicated to getting the most 
out of his internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Bo for 
all of the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
