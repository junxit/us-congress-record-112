# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 9:38 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 2480. An act to amend title 5, United States Code, to 
     authorize appropriations for the Administrative Conference of 
     the United States for fiscal years 2012, 2013, and 2014, and 
     for other purposes.

order of the House of January 5, 2011, the Speaker appoints the 
following Member of the House of Representatives to the Congressional-
Executive Commission on the People's Republic of China: Mr. Smith of 
New Jersey, Chairman.
