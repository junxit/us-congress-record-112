# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Under the previous order, the Senate will 
proceed to a period for the transaction of morning business until 3 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes 
each for the purpose of giving remarks relative to the upcoming 
centennial of the birth of President Ronald Reagan.
```
