# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 223

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, there has been a minor change in the 
schedule. It has been cleared on both sides. I ask unanimous consent 
that, following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed to a period of 
morning business until 10:30 a.m. this morning, with Senators permitted 
to speak during that period of time for up to 10 minutes each.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. REID. Following that morning business, the Senate will resume 
consideration of S. 223, the Federal Aviation Administration 
authorization bill. Currently, we have one amendment pending. Other 
Members are waiting to offer their amendments. It is my understanding 
Senator Wicker is ready to offer his as soon as the bill is reported. 
Senators should expect rollcall votes to occur throughout the day in 
order to make significant progress on this most important legislation.

                          ____________________
```
