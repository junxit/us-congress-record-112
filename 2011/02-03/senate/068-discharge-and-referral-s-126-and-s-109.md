# DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL--S. 126 AND S. 109

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 109, S. 126

```
               DISCHARGE AND REFERRAL--S. 126 AND S. 109

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that S. 126 be discharged from the 
Committee on Rules and Administration and be referred to the Committee 
on Foreign Relations.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so
```
