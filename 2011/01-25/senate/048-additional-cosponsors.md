# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

**Measures:** S.Res. 11

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                               S. RES. 11

  At the request of Mr. Wyden, the names of the Senator from Oklahoma 
(Mr. Inhofe), the Senator from Nebraska (Mr. Johanns) and the Senator 
from Montana (Mr. Tester) were added as cosponsors of S. Res. 11, a 
resolution to establish as a standing order of the Senate that a 
Senator publicly disclose a notice of intent to objecting to any 
measure or matter.
```
