# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following any leader remarks, the Senate 
will be in a period of morning business with Senators allowed to speak 
for up to 10 minutes each. The Senate will recess from 12:30 until 2:15 
p.m. for our weekly caucus luncheons. I will continue to work with my 
colleagues on the Senate rules and committee assignments as I have for 
the last week or so.
  At 9 o'clock tonight, the President will give his State of the Union 
Address to Congress from the House Chamber. Senators are asked to 
gather in the Senate Chamber at 8:30 so we can proceed as a body to the 
House of Representatives. We will leave about 8:40 or 8:45 this 
evening.

                          ____________________
```
