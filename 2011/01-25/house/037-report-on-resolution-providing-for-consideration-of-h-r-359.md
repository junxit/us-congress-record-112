# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 359, ELIMINATING TAXPAYER FINANCING OF PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS

```
     REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 359, 
        ELIMINATING TAXPAYER FINANCING OF PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS

  Ms. FOXX, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report 
(Rept. No. 112-5) on the resolution (H. Res. 54) providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 359) to reduce Federal spending and the 
deficit by terminating taxpayer financing of Presidential election 
campaigns and party conventions, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
