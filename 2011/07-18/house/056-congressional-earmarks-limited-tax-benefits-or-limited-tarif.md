# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 2560

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMITED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

                    Offered By Mr. Ryan of Wisconsin

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on the Budget in H.R. 2560, the Cut, Cap, and Balance Act of 
     2011, do not contain any congressional earmarks, limited

[[Page H5151]]

     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9 of rule XXI.

                         Offered By Mr. Dreier

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Rules in H.R. 2560, to cut, cap, and balance the Federal 
     budget, do not contain any congressional earmarks, limited 
     tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined in clause 
     9 of rule XXI.

                          Offered By Mr. Camp

       The provisions that warranted a referral to the Committee 
     on Ways and Means in H.R. 2560, to cut, cap, and balance the 
     Federal budget, do not contain any congressional earmarks, 
     limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits as defined 
     in clause 9 of rule XXI of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives.
```
