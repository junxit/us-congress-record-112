# Text of Senate Amendment 561

**Speaking:** Vitter, David (V000127) [R-LA]

```
  SA 561. Mr. VITTER submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill H.R. 2055, making appropriations for military 
construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies 
for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2012, and for other purposes; 
which was ordered to lie on the table; as follows:

       On page 117, between lines 12 and 13, insert the following:
       Sec. 410.  None of the funds appropriated or otherwise made 
     available by this Act may be obligated or expended at a rate 
     higher than the level of the Senate and House of 
     Representative concurrent budget resolution for fiscal year 
     2012.

                          ____________________
```
