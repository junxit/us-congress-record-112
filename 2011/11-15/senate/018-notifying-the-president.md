# NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT

**Speaking:** Leahy, Patrick J. (L000174) [D-VT]

```
                        NOTIFYING THE PRESIDENT

  Mr. LEAHY. I thank the Chair. After decades of doing this, I should 
have remembered, of course, we have to notify the President. I recall 
one day, when we went into the beginning of the session and swore in 
new Senators, one was the new Senator from New York, Hillary Clinton. 
The President of the United States was sitting in the gallery. When we 
convened as a Senate, the usual notice was said to notify the President 
that the Senate has convened for that session, at which point several 
of my colleagues rather honorably pointed out the President: You do not 
have to notify him. He is sitting right up in the gallery.

                          ____________________
```
