                                 ______

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 15, 2011

  Mr. COFFMAN of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, today our national debt is 
$14,977,884,880,834.39.

debt was $10,638,425,746,293.80.
