                       Tuesday, February 15, 2011

  Ms. WOOLSEY. Mr. Speaker, on February 14, 2011, I was unavoidably 
detained and was unable to record my vote for rollcall No. 35-37. Had I 
been present I would have voted: rollcall No. 35: ``yes''--On Motion to

provisions of the USA PATRIOT Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 
2005 and Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 
relating to access to business records, individual terrorists as agents 
of foreign powers, and roving wiretaps until December 8, 2011; rollcall 
No. 34: ``yes''--On Approving the Journal.
