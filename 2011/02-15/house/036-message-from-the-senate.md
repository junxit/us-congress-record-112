# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 514

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed with an amendment in which the concurrence 
of the House is requested, a bill of the House of the following title:

       H.R. 514. An act to extend expiring provisions of the USA 
     PATRIOT Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 2005 and 
     Intelligence Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 
     relating to access to business records, individual terrorists 
     as agents of foreign powers, and roving wiretaps until 
     December 8, 2011.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Committee will resume its sitting.

                          ____________________
```
