# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2021, JOBS AND ENERGY PERMITTING ACT OF 2011, AND PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1249, AMERICA INVENTS ACT

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2021, JOBS AND 
ENERGY PERMITTING ACT OF 2011, AND PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 
                       1249, AMERICA INVENTS ACT

  Mr. NUGENT (during the Special Order of Mrs. McMorris Rodgers), from 
the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged report (Rept. No. 112-
111) on the resolution (H. Res. 316) providing for consideration of the 
bill (H.R. 2021) to amend the Clean Air Act regarding air pollution 
from Outer Continental Shelf activities, and providing for 
consideration of the bill (H.R. 1249) to amend title 35, United States 
Code, to provide for patent reform, which was referred to the House 
Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
