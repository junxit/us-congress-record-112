# Text of House Amendment 5

**Speaking:** Cole, Tom (C001053) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                          Offered By: Mr. Cole

       Amendment No. 5: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following:
       Sec. _. None of the funds made available by this Act may be 
     used by the Central Intelligence Agency or the Department of 
     Defense until such a time that the President formally 
     requests and receives from Congress an authorization for the 
     use of military force in or against Libya.
```
