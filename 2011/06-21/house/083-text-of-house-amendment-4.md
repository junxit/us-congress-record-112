# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Cole, Tom (C001053) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                          Offered By: Mr. Cole

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following:
       Sec. _. None of the funds made available by this Act may be 
     used to implement any rule, regulation, or executive order 
     regarding the disclosure of political contributions that 
     takes effect on or after the date of enactment of this Act.
```
