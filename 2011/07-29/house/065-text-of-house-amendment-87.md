# Text of House Amendment 87

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 87: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

[[Page H5783]]

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used for enforcement activities under the Federal Water 
     Pollution Control Act (33 U.S.C. 1251 et seq.) against the 
     owner or operator of any concentrated animal feeding 
     operation consisting of less than 1,000 animal units.
```
