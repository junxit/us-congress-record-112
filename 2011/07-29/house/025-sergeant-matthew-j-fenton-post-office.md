# SERGEANT MATTHEW J. FENTON POST OFFICE

```
                 SERGEANT MATTHEW J. FENTON POST OFFICE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 789) to designate the 
facility

[[Page 12495]]

of the United States Postal Service located at 20 Main Street in Little 
Ferry, New Jersey, as the ``Sergeant Matthew J. Fenton Post Office.''
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Oklahoma (Mr. Lankford) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the bill was passed.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
