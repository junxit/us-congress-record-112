**Speaking:** Terry, Lee (T000459) [R-NE]

**Measures:** H.R. 2213

                  SERGEANT JASON W. VAUGHN POST OFFICE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and passing the bill (H.R. 2213) to designate the 
facility of the United States Postal Service located at 801 West 
Eastport Street in Iuka, Mississippi, as the ``Sergeant Jason W. Vaughn 
Post Office''.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Oklahoma (Mr. Lankford) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The question was taken.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. In the opinion of the Chair, two-thirds 
being in the affirmative, the ayes have it.
