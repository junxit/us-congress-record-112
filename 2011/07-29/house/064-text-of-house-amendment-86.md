# Text of House Amendment 86

**Speaking:** Burgess, Michael C. (B001248) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                        Offered By: Mr. Burgess

       Amendment No. 86: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to pay the salary of any officer or employee of the 
     Environmental Protection Agency who is receiving special pay 
     consideration under section 207 of the Public Health Service 
     Act (42 U.S.C. 209).
```
