# Text of Senate Amendment 603

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
  SA 603. Mr. REID proposed an amendment to amendment SA 602 proposed 
by Mr. Reid to the joint resolution H.J. Res. 66, approving the renewal 
of import restrictions contained in the Burmese Freedom and Democracy 
Act of 2003; as follows:

       At the end, add the following new section:

     SECTION __. EFFECTIVE DATE.
```
