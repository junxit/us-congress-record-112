                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 4 of rule XXII, memorials were presented and referred as

       130. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature of

     Resolution No. 7 commending its educators who teach about

     Affairs.

     Judiciary.

     the State of New Hampshire, relative to House Concurrent 
     Resolution 9 urging the President and the Congress to

     safety, and religious freedom concerns presented by advanced 
     imaging technology employed by the Transportation Security 
     Agency; to the Committee on Homeland Security.
       133. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     California, relative to Assembly Joint Resolution No. 10

     the Committees on Energy and Commerce and Education and the

     relative to Senate Resolution No. 90 opposing the budget cuts 
     proposed by the President and the Congress; jointly to the 
     Committees on Foreign Affairs and Energy and Commerce.
       135. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of Hawaii, 
     relative to Senate Resolution No. 27 urging the Congress to

       136. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of Hawaii,

     Commerce.
       137. Also, a memorial of the Council of the City of

     Oversight and Government Reform and Natural Resources.
       138. Also, a memorial of the Council of the City of

       139. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     California, relative to Assembly Joint Resolution No. 12 
     requesting the enactment of the Federal Strengthening
