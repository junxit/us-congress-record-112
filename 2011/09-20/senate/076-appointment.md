# APPOINTMENT

```
                              APPOINTMENT

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Chair, on behalf of the Republican leader, 
after consultation with chairman of the Select Committee on 
Intelligence, and pursuant to provisions of Public Law 107-306, as 
amended by Public Law 111-259, announces the appointment of the Senator

Commission for the Review of the Research and Development Programs of
```
