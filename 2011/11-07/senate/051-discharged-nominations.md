# DISCHARGED NOMINATIONS

```
                         DISCHARGED NOMINATIONS

  The Senate Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs 
was discharged from further consideration of the following nominations 
under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 7, 2009 and 
the nominations were placed on the Executive Calendar:

       * Irvin Charles McCullough III, of Maryland, to be 
     Inspector General of the Intelligence Community, Office of 
     the Director of National Intelligence.
       * David A. Montoya, of Texas, to be Inspector General, 
     Department of Housing and Urban Development.

  * Nominee has committed to respond to requests to appear and testify 
before any duly constituted committee of the Senate.

                          ____________________
```
