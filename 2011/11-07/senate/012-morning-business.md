# MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Wyden, Ron (W000779) [D-OR]

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, there 
will now be a period of morning business until 5 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein up to 10 minutes each.
  Mr. REID. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The clerk will call the roll.

  Mr. WYDEN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the order for the 
quorum call be rescinded.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. WYDEN. I ask to speak in morning business.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
```
