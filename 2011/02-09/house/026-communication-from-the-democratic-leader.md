# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

**Measures:** H.Res. 5

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, House Democratic Leader:

                                                 February 8, 2011.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker of the House, U.S. Capitol,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Boehner: Pursuant to section 4(b) of House 
     Resolution 5, 112th Congress, I am pleased to re-appoint the 
     Honorable James P. McGovern of Massachusetts as Co-Chair of 
     the Tom Lantos Human Rights Commission.
       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     House Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
