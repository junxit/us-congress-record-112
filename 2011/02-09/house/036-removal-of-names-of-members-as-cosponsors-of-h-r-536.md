# REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 536

**Speaking:** Cole, Tom (C001053) [R-OK]

**Measures:** H.R. 455, H.R. 536

```
         REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 536

  Mr. COLE. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the following 
cosponsors be removed from the permanent record as cosponsors of H.R. 
536: Jeff Duncan, South Carolina 3; Virginia Foxx, North Carolina 5; 
Adrian Smith, Nebraska 3.
  These Members intended to cosponsor my legislation, H.R. 455, the 
10th Amendment Regulatory Reform Act. A clerical error led to their 
names being added as original cosponsors of this legislation. These 
Members never agreed to cosponsor H.R. 536, and I ask that the record 
reflect that they were never cosponsors of this legislation.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the cosponsors will be 
removed.
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
