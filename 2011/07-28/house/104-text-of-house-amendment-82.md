# Text of House Amendment 82

**Speaking:** Rigell, E. Scott (R000589) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                         Offered By: Mr. Rigell

       Amendment No. 82: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available By: this Act may 
     be used to purchase lands that would result in a net increase 
     in Federal land holdings (other than lands acquired to be 
     held in trust for the benefit of a federally recognized 
     Indian tribe).
```
