# Text of House Amendment 81

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 81: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available By: this Act may 
     be used in contravention of section 104(k), or section 128, 
     of the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, 
     and Liability Act of 1980 (42 U.S.C. 9604(k), 9628).
```
