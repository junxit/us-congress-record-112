# WISHING THE UW MEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM THE BEST OF LUCK

**Speaking:** McDermott, Jim (M000404) [D-WA]

```
         WISHING THE UW MEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM THE BEST OF LUCK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JIM McDERMOTT

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 17, 2011

  Mr. McDERMOTT. Mr. Speaker, today is St. Patrick's Day--a day that is 
universally associated with good luck. Calling on my Irish heritage, I 
would like to convey continued good luck to the University of 
Washington men's basketball team. This past weekend they won the Pac-10 
Championship and tomorrow they seek to continue their run in the NCAA 
tournament when they open against the University of Georgia.
  Good luck and go Dawgs.

                          ____________________
```
