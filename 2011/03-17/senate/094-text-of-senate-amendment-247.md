# Text of Senate Amendment 247

**Speaking:** Hatch, Orrin G. (H000338) [R-UT]

```
  SA 247. Mr. HATCH submitted an amendment intended to be proposed by 
him to the bill S. 493, to reauthorize and improve the SBIR and STTR 
programs, and for other purposes; which was ordered to lie on the 
table; as follows:

       At the end, add the following:

     SEC. ___. MAXIMUM PURCHASE LIMIT UNDER THE SMALL BUSINESS 
                   LENDING FUND PROGRAM; TRANSFER OF FUNDS.

       (a) Maximum Purchase Limit.--Section 4103(a)(2) of the 
     Small Business Jobs Act of 2010 (12 U.S.C. 4741 note) is 
     amended by striking ``$30,000,000,000'' and inserting 
     ``$20,000,000,000''.
       (b) Transfer of Amounts in Fund.--Section 4108 of the Small 
     Business Jobs Act of 2010 (12 U.S.C. 4741 note) is amended by 
     adding at the end the following:
       ``(c) Transfer of Amounts.--On the date of enactment of the 
     SBIR/STTR Reauthorization Act of 2011, the Secretary shall 
     transfer $10,000,000,000 from the Fund to the general fund of 
     the Treasury for reduction of the public debt.''.
                                 ______
```
