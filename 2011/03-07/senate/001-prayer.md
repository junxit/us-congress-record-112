# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, known to us in countless ways and times, lead our 
Senators in Your way. Lord, keep them aware that they can depend on 
Your validation of every just cause and the forgiveness of every sin 
which they, in godly sorrow, confess to You. As they follow You, may 
their small successes prompt them to even greater understanding for 
human benefit. Guide them by Your higher wisdom and bring them to a 
desired destination with hearts at peace with You.
  We pray in Your holy Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
