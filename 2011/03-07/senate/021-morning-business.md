**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Grassley, Chuck (G000386) [R-IA]

```

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to a period for the transaction of morning business, and that 
the 30 hours postcloture run on the patent bill, and that Senator

business, and that following his statement, Senators be recognized for

wanted to do that. The unanimous consent agreement said whatever time 
he may consume.
```
