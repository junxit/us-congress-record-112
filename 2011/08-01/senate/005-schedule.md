# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following any leader remarks, the Senate 
will resume consideration of the motion to concur in the House message 
to accompany S. 627, which is the legislative vehicle for the debt 
limit increase.
  The Senate will recess from 11 a.m. until 12:30 p.m. When the Senate 
reconvenes at 12:30, the time until 2 p.m. will be equally divided and 
controlled between the two leaders or their designees.
  The Senate expects to vote on the compromise we have reached, 
hopefully, during today's session. When the vote is scheduled, Senators 
will be notified.
```
