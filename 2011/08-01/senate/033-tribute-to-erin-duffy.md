# TRIBUTE TO ERIN DUFFY

```
                         TRIBUTE TO ERIN DUFFY

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Erin Duffy, an 
intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
couple months.
  Erin is a graduate of St. Thomas More High School in Rapid City, SD. 
Currently, she is attending Stanford University, where she is majoring 
in international relations and economics. She is a hard worker who has 
been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Erin for 
all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in the 
years to come.●
```
