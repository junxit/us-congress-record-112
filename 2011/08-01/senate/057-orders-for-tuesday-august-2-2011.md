# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, AUGUST 2, 2011

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, AUGUST 2, 2011

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., August 2; 
that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, and the time for 
the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
