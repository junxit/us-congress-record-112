# TRIBUTE TO LAUREN HAAHR

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO LAUREN HAAHR

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Lauren Haahr, an 
intern in my Washington, DC office, for all of the hard work she has 
done for me, my staff, and the State of South Dakota over the past 
couple months.
  Lauren is a graduate of Lincoln High School in Sioux Falls, SD. 
Currently, she is attending the University of Iowa, where she is 
majoring in economics and ethics & public policy. She is a hard worker 
who has been dedicated to getting the most out of her internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Lauren 
for all of the fine work she has done and wish her continued success in 
the years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
