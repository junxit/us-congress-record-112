# TRIBUTE TO DANIEL ZABRISKIE

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                      TRIBUTE TO DANIEL ZABRISKIE

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Daniel Zabriskie for his hard work as an 
intern in my Casper office. I recognize his efforts and contributions 
to my office as well as to the State of Wyoming.
  Daniel is a native of Wyoming and graduated from Natrona County High 
School. He attends the University of Wyoming where he is majoring in 
criminal justice and minoring in communication. He has demonstrated a 
strong work ethic which has made him an invaluable asset to our office. 
The quality of his work is reflected in his great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I thank Daniel for the dedication he has shown while working for me 
and my staff. It was a pleasure to have him as part of our team. I know 
he will have continued success with all of his future endeavors. I wish 
him all my best on his next journey.

                          ____________________
```
