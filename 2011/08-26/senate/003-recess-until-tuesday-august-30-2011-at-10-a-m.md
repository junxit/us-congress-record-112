# RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, AUGUST 30, 2011 AT 10 A.M.

```
            RECESS UNTIL TUESDAY, AUGUST 30, 2011 AT 10 A.M.

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Tuesday, August 30, 2011, at 10 a.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11:15 and 31 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, August 30, 2011, at 10 a.m.
```
