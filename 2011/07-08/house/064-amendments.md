# AMENDMENTS

**Measures:** H.R. 2354

```
                               AMENDMENTS

  Under clause 8 of rule XVIII, proposed amendments were submitted as 
follows:

                               H.R. 2354

                         Offered By: Mr. Pompeo

       Amendment No. 24: Page 62, after line 2, insert the 
     following new section:
       Sec. 609.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to carry out the Vehicle Technologies Program of the 
     Department of Energy.

                               H.R. 2354

                        Offered By: Mr. McKinley

       Amendment No. 25: Page 24, line 18, after the dollar 
     amount, insert ``(increased by $39,000,000)''.
       Page 28, line 13, after the dollar amount, insert 
     ``(reduced by $39,000,000)''.

                               H.R. 2354

                          Offered By: Mr. Cole

       Amendment No. 26: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to implement any rule, regulation, or executive order 
     regarding the disclosure of political contributions that 
     takes effect on or after the date of enactment of this Act.
```
