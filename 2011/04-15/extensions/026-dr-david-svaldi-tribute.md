# DR. DAVID SVALDI TRIBUTE

```
                        DR. DAVID SVALDI TRIBUTE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SCOTT R. TIPTON

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, April 15, 2011

  Mr. TIPTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. David Svaldi 
of Alamosa, Colorado. Dr. Svaldi was recently appointed to El

[[Page 6327]]

Pomar Foundation's San Luis Valley Regional Council. As one of southern 
Colorado's most esteemed residents he will undoubtedly thrive in that 
position.
  Dr. Svaldi is president of Adams State College, a job he balances 
with his other volunteer work. He sits on the boards of the area's Boys 
and Girls Club, Health Education Council and the Southern Colorado 
Education Consortium.
  The education and health industries in southern Colorado are growing 
quickly. It is important that people like Dr. Svaldi, who have a 
history of involvement and success in those areas, lend their expertise 
to the growing infrastructure.
  Mr. Speaker, it is a honor to recognize Dr. David Svaldi today. He 
will continue to be a leader in his community and work to better the 
lives of his neighbors.
```
