# MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

**Measures:** H.R. 1363

```
                    MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR

  The following bill was read twice and ordered to be placed on the 
calendar:

       H.R. 1363, An act making appropriations for the Department 
     of Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2011, and 
     for other purposes.


 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page S2264, April 7, 2011, in the middle column, under the 
heading MEASURES PLACED ON THE CALENDAR, the following appears: 
H.R. 1363, a bill making appropriations for the Department of 
Defense for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2011, and for 
other purposes.
  
  The online Record has been corrected to read: H.R. 1363. An act 
making appropriations for the Department of Defense for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2011, and for other purposes.


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


                          ____________________
```
