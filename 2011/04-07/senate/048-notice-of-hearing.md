# NOTICE OF HEARING

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the 
information of the Senate and the public that a hearing scheduled 
before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural Resources has been 
postponed. This hearing was scheduled to be held on Thursday, April 14, 
2011, at 9:30 a.m., in room SD-366 of the Dirksen Senate Office 
Building.
  The purpose of this hearing was to review S. 343 a bill to amend 
Title I of PL 99-658 regarding the Compact of Free Association between 
the Government of the United States of America and the Government of 
Palau, to approve the results of the 15-year review of the Compact, 
including the Agreement Between the Government of the United States of 
America and the Government of the Republic of Palau following the 
Compact of Free Association Section 432 Review, to appropriate funds 
for the purposes of the amended PL 99-658 for fiscal years ending on or 
before September 30, 2024, and to carry out the agreements resulting 
from that review.
  For further information, please contact Al Stayman at (202) 224-7865 
or Abigail Campbell at (202) 224-1219.

                          ____________________
```
