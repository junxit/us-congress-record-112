# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DURBIN. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the period 
for morning business be extended until 10:30 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, and I ask that 
the time for morning business be for debate only.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
