# NOTICE OF HEARING

**Speaking:** Akaka, Daniel K. (A000069) [D-HI]

**Measures:** S. 1065, S. 1763

```
                           NOTICE OF HEARING


                      committee on indian affairs

  Mr. AKAKA. Mr. President, I would like to announce that the Committee 
on Indian Affairs will meet on Thursday, December 8, 2011, at 2:15 p.m. 
in room 628 of the Dirksen Senate Office Building to conduct a business 
meeting to consider S. 1763, the SAVE Native Women Act, and S. 1065, 
the Blackfeet River Land Settlement Act, to be followed by a hearing 
entitled ``State and Federal Tax Policy: Building New Markets in Indian 
Country.''
  Those wishing additional information may contact the Indian Affairs 
Committee at (202) 224-2251.

                          ____________________
```
