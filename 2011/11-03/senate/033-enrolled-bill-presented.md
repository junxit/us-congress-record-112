# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate announced that on today, November 3, 
2011, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bill:

       S. 894. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     provide for an increase, effective December 1, 2011, in the 
     rates of compensation for veterans with service-connected 
     disabilities and the rates of dependency and indemnity 
     compensation for the survivors of certain disabled veterans, 
     and for other purposes.
```
