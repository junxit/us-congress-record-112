# FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

```
                    FURTHER MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A further message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the 
House of the following title:

       H.R. 818. An act to direct the Secretary of the Interior to 
     allow for prepayment of repayment contracts between the 
     United States and the Uintah Water Conservancy District.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1487. An act to authorize the Secretary of Homeland 
     Security, in coordination with the Secretary of State, to 
     establish a program to issue Asia-Pacific Economic 
     Cooperation Business Travel Cards, and for other purposes.

                          ____________________
```
