# LEAHY-SMITH AMERICA INVENTS ACT--MOTION TO PROCEED

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ], Brown, Sherrod (B000944) [D-OH]

**Measures:** H.R. 1249

Code, to provide for patent reform, shall be brought to a close?
