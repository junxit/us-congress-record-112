# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** S.Res. 257

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that it adjourn under the 
provisions of S. Res. 257 as a further mark of respect to the memory of 
the late Senator Mark O. Hatfield, of Oregon.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 6:53 p.m., adjourned until 
Wednesday, September 7, 2011, at 10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
