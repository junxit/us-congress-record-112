# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2011

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 23, 2011

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I now ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9 a.m., on 
Friday, September 23; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; following any leader remarks, the Senate be in a period of morning 
business with Senators permitted to speak up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
