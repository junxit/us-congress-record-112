# ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

**Measures:** S. 846

```
                        ENROLLED BILL PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, September 22, 
2011, she had presented to the President of the United States the 
following enrolled bill:

       S. 846. An act to designate the United States courthouse 
     located at 80 Lafayette Street in Jefferson City, Missouri, 
     as the Christopher S. Bond United States Courthouse.
```
