# Text of House Amendment 63

**Speaking:** Burton, Dan (B001149) [R-IN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                   Offered By: Mr. Burton of Indiana

       Amendment No. 63: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to initiate or carry out training for members of the 
     Armed Forces serving in combat zones related to 
     implementation of any repeal of section 654 of title 10, 
     United States Code, by section 2 of Public Law 111-321.
```
