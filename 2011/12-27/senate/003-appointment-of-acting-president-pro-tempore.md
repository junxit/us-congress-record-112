# APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

```
              APPOINTMENT OF ACTING PRESIDENT PRO TEMPORE

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will please read a communication to 
the Senate from the President pro tempore (Mr. Inouye).
  The bill clerk read the following letter:

                                                      U.S. Senate,


                                        President pro tempore,

                                Washington, DC, December 27, 2011.
     To the Senate:
       Under the provisions of rule I, section 3, of the Standing 
     Rules of the Senate, I hereby appoint the Honorable Harry 
     Reid, a Senator from the State of Nevada, to perform the 
     duties of the Chair.
                                                 Daniel K. Inouye,
                                            President pro tempore.

  Mr. REID thereupon assumed the chair as Acting President pro tempore.

                          ____________________
```
