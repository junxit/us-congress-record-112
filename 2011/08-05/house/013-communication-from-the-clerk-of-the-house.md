# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:


[[Page 12892]]


                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                   Washington, DC, August 3, 2011.
     Hon. John A. Boehner,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to section 4(c) of House 
     Resolution 5, One Hundred Twelfth Congress, and section 
     1(k)(2) of House Resolution 895, One Hundred Tenth Congress, 
     I transmit to you notification that Jay Eagen, Allison 
     Hayward, and Kelly Brewington each have signed an agreement 
     not to be a candidate for the office of Senator or 
     Representative in, or Delegate or Resident Commissioner to, 
     the Congress for purposes of the Federal Election Campaign 
     Act of 1971 until at least 3 years after he or she is no 
     longer a member of the board or staff of the Office of 
     Congressional Ethics.
       Copies of the signed agreements shall be retained by the 
     Office of the Clerk as part of the records of the House.
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                                    Karen L. Haas,
     Clerk of the House.

                          ____________________
```
