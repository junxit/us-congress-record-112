# DISCHARGED NOMINATION

```
                         DISCHARGED NOMINATION

  The Senate Committee on Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs 
was discharged from further consideration of the following nomination 
pursuant to the order of January 7, 2009 and the nomination was placed 
on the Executive Calendar pursuant to an order of January 7, 2009:
       *JONATHAN ANDREW HATFIELD, OF VIRGINIA, TO BE INSPECTOR 
     GENERAL, CORPORATION FOR NATIONAL AND COMMUNITY SERVICE.

  *Nominee has committed to respond to requests to appear and testify 
before any duly constituted committee of the Senate.
```
