# RECESS UNTIL FRIDAY, AUGUST 12, 2011, AT 12 NOON

```

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until Friday, August 12, 2011, at 12 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 11 and 33 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Friday, August 12, at 12 noon.
```
