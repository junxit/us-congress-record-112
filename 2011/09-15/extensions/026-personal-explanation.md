# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Heinrich, Martin (H001046) [D-NM]

**Measures:** H.R. 1059, H.R. 2076, H.R. 2633

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARTIN HEINRICH

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, September 15, 2011

  Mr. HEINRICH. Mr. Speaker, I unfortunately missed three votes on 
September 12, 2011, which included roll call votes 699, 700 and 701.
  If I had been present, I would have voted in favor of rollcall vote 
699, H.R. 2076, the Investigative Assistance for Violent Crimes Act of 
2011.
  If I had been present, I would have voted in favor of rollcall vote 
700, H.R. 2633, the Appeal Time Clarification Act of 2011.
  Finally, if I had been present, I would have voted in favor of 
rollcall vote 701, H.R. 1059, to protect the safety of judges by 
extending

[[Page E1644]]

the authority of the Judicial Conference to redact sensitive 
information contained in their financial disclosure reports, and for 
other purposes.
```
