# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2942

**Measures:** H.R. 2942

```
           By Mr. KING of Iowa:
       H.R. 2942.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 14 gives Congress the 
     authority to make rules for the government and regulation of 
     the land and naval forces.
```
