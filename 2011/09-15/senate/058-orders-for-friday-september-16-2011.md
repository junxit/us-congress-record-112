# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 16, 2011

```
                 ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, SEPTEMBER 16, 2011

  Mr. BENNET. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. on 
Friday, September 16; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate be in a period of 
morning business, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 
minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
