amendment to H.R. 2838);
  ``Yes'' on rollcall vote No. 833 (on agreeing to the Thompson 
amendment to H.R. 2838);
  ``Yes'' on rollcall vote No. 834 (on agreeing to the Napolitano 
amendment to H.R. 2838);
  ``Yes'' on rollcall vote No. 835 (on agreeing to the Bishop amendment 
to H.R. 2838); and
  ``Yes'' on rollcall vote No. 836 (on agreeing to the Slaughter 
amendment to H.R. 2838).
