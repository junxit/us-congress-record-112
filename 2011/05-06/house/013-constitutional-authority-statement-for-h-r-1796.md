# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 1796

**Measures:** H.R. 1796

```
           By Mr. HONDA:
       H.R. 1796.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Clause 4 of Section 8 of Article I of the Constitution, 
     which grants Congress the power ``[t]o establish an uniform 
     Rule of Naturalization . . . throughout the UnitedStates.''
```
