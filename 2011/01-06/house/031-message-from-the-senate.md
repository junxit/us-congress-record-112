# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** S.Con.Res. 2

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed a concurrent resolution of the following 
titles in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. Con. Res. 2. Concurrent resolution authorizing the use 
     of the rotunda of the Capitol for an event marking the 50th 
     anniversary of the inaugural address of President John F. 
     Kennedy.

  The message also announced that pursuant to provisions of Public Law

[[Page H69]]

107-306, as amended by Public Law 111-259, the Chair, on behalf of the 
Majority Leader, after consultation with the Chairman of the Select 
Committee on Intelligence, announces the appointment of the following 
individuals to serve as members of the National Commission for Review 
of Research and Development Programs of the United States Intelligence 
Community:
  Gilman Louie of California.
  Troy Wade of Nevada.

                          ____________________
```
