# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 4, 2011

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, APRIL 4, 2011

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate

[[Page S2061]]

completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, April 
4; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of proceedings be 
approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, the time for the 
two leaders be reserved for their use later in the day, and the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business until 4:30 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each. Further, I ask that 
following morning business, the Senate proceed to executive session 
under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
