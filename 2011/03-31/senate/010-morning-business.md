# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business, with Senators permitted 
to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the first hour equally 
divided and controlled between the two

[[Page 4767]]

leaders or their designees, with the majority controlling the first 30 
minutes and the Republicans controlling the next 30 minutes.
  The Senator from Washington is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
