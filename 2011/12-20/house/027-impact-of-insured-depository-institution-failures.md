# IMPACT OF INSURED DEPOSITORY INSTITUTION FAILURES

```
           IMPACT OF INSURED DEPOSITORY INSTITUTION FAILURES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Womack). The unfinished business is the 
question on suspending the rules and concurring in the Senate 
amendments to the bill (H.R. 2056) to instruct the Inspector General of 
the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation to study the impact of 
insured depository institution failures, and for other purposes.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Georgia (Mr. Westmoreland) that the House suspend the 
rules and concur in the Senate amendments.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the Senate amendments were concurred in.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
```
