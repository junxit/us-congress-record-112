# WE REJOICE IN THE TRIUMPH OF JUSTICE

```
                  WE REJOICE IN THE TRIUMPH OF JUSTICE

  (Mr. McCOTTER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McCOTTER. Mr. Speaker, today we do not revel over the death of 
bin Laden, the butcher; we rejoice in the triumph of justice. We thank 
our citizen soldiers, the intelligence community, and the Obama 
administration for finishing this mission; and we forever remember the 
fallen and comfort the families who so grievously suffered on September 
11, 2001.
  Now let us press our efforts against al Qaeda and their terrorist 
cohorts, including Hamas, Hezbollah, and the tyrannical Iranian regime. 
For while our world has witnessed justice, our world remains dangerous; 
and we must be ever vigilant against such clear and present threats to 
our liberty and security.
  We have dispatched a murderer, not his movement. We have won a 
battle, not our struggle. But in so succeeding, we have served notice 
to every terrorist, tyrant, and tyrannical regime that we will win.

                          ____________________
```
