# Text of House Amendment 7

**Speaking:** Waters, Maxine (W000187) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 1214

```
H.R. 1214

                         Offered By: Ms. Waters

       Amendment No. 7: In section 1, add at the end the 
     following:
       (c) Report on Number of Children in Public Schools Who Do 
     Not Have Health Insurance.--Not later than 6 months after the 
     date of the enactment of this Act, the Secretary of Health 
     and Human Services shall submit to the Congress a report on 
     the number of children in public schools who do not have 
     health insurance.
```
