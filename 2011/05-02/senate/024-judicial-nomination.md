# JUDICIAL NOMINATION

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                          JUDICIAL NOMINATION

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I hope, as I mentioned this morning, we are 
not in a situation where we have to file cloture on district court 
judges. That is a little much. I filed cloture on this because I 
couldn't get agreement not to do it, but I hope we don't have to have 
cloture on all the district court judges whom somebody has some concern 
about.
  This is a very good man. Morally his record is impeccable. As a 
lawyer, he is certainly one of the two or three best lawyers in the 
State of Rhode Island, and I would hope we could get this done on 
Wednesday when this cloture motion ripens.

                          ____________________
```
