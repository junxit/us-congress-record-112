# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT

**Measures:** H.R. 1308, H.R. 1473

```
          MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE RECEIVED DURING ADJOURNMENT


                          enrolled bill signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 5, 2011, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on April 15, 2011, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker had signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 1473. An act making appropriations for the Department 
     of Defense and the other departments and agencies of the 
     Government for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2011, and 
     for other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the Acting President pro 
tempore (Mr. Rockefeller) on April 15, 2011.


                          enrolled bill signed

  Under the authority of the order of the Senate of January 5, 2011, 
the Secretary of the Senate, on April 18, 2011, during the adjournment 
of the Senate, received a message from the House of Representatives 
announcing that the Speaker had signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 1308. An act to amend the Ronald Reagan Centennial 
     Commission Act to extend the termination date for the 
     Commission, and for other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the Acting President pro 
tempore (Mr. Rockefeller) on April 29, 2011.

                          ____________________
```
