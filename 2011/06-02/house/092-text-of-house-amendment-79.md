# Text of House Amendment 79

**Speaking:** Eshoo, Anna G. (E000215) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                         Offered By: Ms. Eshoo

       Amendment No. 79: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to enter into a contract with a corporation or other 
     business entity that does not disclose its political 
     expenditures.
```
