# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the period of 
morning business for debate only be extended until 6 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, and that at 6 
p.m. I be recognized.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.

                          ____________________
```
