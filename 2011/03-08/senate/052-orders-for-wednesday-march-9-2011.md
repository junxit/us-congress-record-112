# ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY, MARCH 9, 2011

**Speaking:** Bennet, Michael F. (B001267) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
                         ORDERS FOR WEDNESDAY,
                             MARCH 9, 2011

  Mr. BENNET. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m., on 
Wednesday, March 9; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks, there be a period of morning 
business until 10:40 a.m., with Senators permitted to speak therein for 
up to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and controlled 
between the two leaders or their designees, with the majority 
controlling the first half and the Republicans controlling the final 
half; that at 10:40 a.m., the Senate recess

[[Page S1414]]

until 12 noon for a joint meeting with the Honorable Julia Gillard, 
Prime Minister of Australia; that following the joint meeting, the 
Senate reconvene at 12 noon and proceed to the consideration of H.R. 1, 
as provided under the previous order.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
