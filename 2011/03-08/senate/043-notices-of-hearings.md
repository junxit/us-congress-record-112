               COMMITTEE ON ENERGY AND NATURAL RESOURCES

  Mr. BINGAMAN. Mr. President, I would like to announce for the

been scheduled before the Senate Committee on Energy and Natural 
Resources. The meeting will be held on Tuesday, March 15, 2011, at 10 
a.m., in room SVC-217, U.S. Capitol Building in Washington, DC.
  The purpose of the meeting will be to receive information regarding

6689 or Meagan Gins at (202) 224-0883.
