# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate will be in a period of morning business for 2 hours, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
time divided or controlled between the two leaders or their designees, 
with the minority controlling the first hour and the majority 
controlling the next hour, with 30 minutes under the control of the 
Senator from Massachusetts.
  The Senator from Wyoming is recognized.

                          ____________________
```
