# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord, our God and Savior, You have given us a powerful calling. 
Destined in our journey of life to find eternal happiness in Your 
presence; each day, we hope to draw closer to You and at the same time 
accomplish great deeds for the good of this Nation and for the world 
community.
  By Your guidance of our judgments and decisions may we respond to 
Your commands and holy inspirations to build Your kingdom of peace and 
justice.
  In so doing, may we give You glory now and forever. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
