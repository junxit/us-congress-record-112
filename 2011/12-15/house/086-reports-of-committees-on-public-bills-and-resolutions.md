     appropriations for disaster relief requirements for the 
     fiscal year ending September 30, 2012, and for other 
     purposes; providing for consideration of the concurrent 
     resolution (H. Con. Res. 94) directing the Clerk of the House 
     of Representatives to make corrections in the enrollment of 
     H.R. 3672; and for other purposes (Rept. 112-332). Referred 
     to the House Calendar.
