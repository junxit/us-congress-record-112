**Speaking:** Whitehouse, Sheldon (W000802) [D-RI], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL], Kyl, Jon (K000352) [R-AZ], Kerry, John F. (K000148) [D-MA]

of Morgan Christen, of Alaska, to be United States Circuit Judge for 
the Ninth Circuit?

Massachusetts (Mr. Kerry) would vote ``yea.''

from Kansas (Mr. Moran).
