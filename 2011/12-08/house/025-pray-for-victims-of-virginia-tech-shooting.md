# PRAY FOR VICTIMS OF VIRGINIA TECH SHOOTING

```
               PRAY FOR VICTIMS OF VIRGINIA TECH SHOOTING

  (Mr. GRIFFITH of Virginia asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. GRIFFITH of Virginia. I ask everyone here and across the Nation 
to pray for those individuals at Virginia Tech in Blacksburg, Virginia, 
who are currently dealing with the shootings that took place there 
today and the two people who, regrettably, have passed away.
```
