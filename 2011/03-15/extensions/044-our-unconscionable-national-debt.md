# OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

**Speaking:** Coffman, Mike (C001077) [R-CO]

```
                    OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 15, 2011

  Mr. COFFMAN of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, today our national debt is 
$14,166,030,787,779.80.
  On January 6, 2009, the start of the 111th Congress, the national 
debt was $10,638,425,746,293.80.
  This means the national debt has increased by $3,527,605,041,486.00 
since then.
  This debt and its interest payments we are passing to our children 
and all future Americans.
```
