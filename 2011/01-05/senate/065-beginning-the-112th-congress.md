# BEGINNING THE 112TH CONGRESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                      BEGINNING THE 112TH CONGRESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, this has been an exciting day, the beginning 
of the 112th Congress. It has been I think a historic day. The debate 
has been very good. The exchange between the Republican leader and I 
set the stage, I hope--at least that is what I believe--for the 
conversation that came later from Democrats and Republicans about how 
this place is going to run. I think that has been very constructive for 
the Senate and for the country.
```
