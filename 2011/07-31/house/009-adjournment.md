# ADJOURNMENT

**Speaking:** Sensenbrenner, F. James, Jr. (S000244) [R-WI]

```
                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. SENSENBRENNER. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 1 o'clock and 37 minutes
```
