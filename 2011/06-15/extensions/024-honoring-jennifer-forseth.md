# HONORING JENNIFER FORSETH

```
                       HONORING JENNIFER FORSETH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. GEOFF DAVIS

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 15, 2011

  Mr. DAVIS of Kentucky. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Ms. 
Jennifer Forseth, a teacher at Centerfield Elementary School, and her 
fourth grade class from Oldham County in the fourth district of 
Kentucky.
  Ms. Forseth and her students have been recognized by the National 
Energy Education Development Project for their outstanding work on 
energy programs this year.
  Ms. Forseth's class conducted a study detailing how to improve energy 
efficiency in their school that culminated in a presentation of their 
findings. Their academic initiative earned them a scholarship and 
recognition by the NEED Youth Awards.
  It is my hope that the accomplishments of Ms. Forseth and the fourth 
grade students from Centerfield Elementary School serve as inspiration 
for others to increase their own energy efficiency. I ask my colleagues 
in the U.S. House of Representatives to join me in recognizing their 
accomplishments.
```
