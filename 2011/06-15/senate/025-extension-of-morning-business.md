# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Hagan, Kay R. (H001049) [D-NC], Inhofe, James M. (I000024) [R-OK]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mrs. HAGAN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the period 
for morning business be extended until 6 p.m., with Senators permitted 
to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mrs. HAGAN. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The assistant legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. INHOFE. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order for 
the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
