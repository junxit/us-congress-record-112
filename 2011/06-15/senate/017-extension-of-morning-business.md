# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the period 
for morning business be extended until 5 p.m., with Senators permitted 
to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER (Mr. Merkley). Is there objection?
  Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I yield the floor.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senator from Tennessee.

                          ____________________
```
