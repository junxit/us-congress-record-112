# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:54 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mr. Novotny, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 2055. An act making appropriations for military 
     construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related 
     agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2012, and 
     for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to 22 U.S.C. 276H, and the 
order of the House of January 5, 2011, the Speaker appoints the 
following Members of the House of Representatives to the Mexico-United 
States Interparliamentary Group: Mr. Mack of Florida, Mr. Nunes of 
California, Mr. Bilbray of California, and Mr. Canseco of Texas.

                          ____________________
```
