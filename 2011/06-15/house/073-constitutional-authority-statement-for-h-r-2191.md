# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2191

**Measures:** H.R. 2191

```
           By Mr. CLAY:
       H.R. 2191.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       The Commerce Clause (Art. I & 8, cl. 3) of the United 
     States Constitution provide that the Congress shall have the 
     power to regulate interstate and foreign commerce.
```
