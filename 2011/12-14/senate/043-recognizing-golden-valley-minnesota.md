# RECOGNIZING GOLDEN VALLEY, MINNESOTA

```
                  RECOGNIZING GOLDEN VALLEY, MINNESOTA

  Mr. FRANKEN. Mr. President, I want to take this opportunity to honor 
the 125th Anniversary of the incorporation of Golden Valley, MN. As a 
child growing up in St. Louis Park, I have many fond memories of time 
spent in my neighboring town to the north, Golden Valley. As next-door 
neighbors, our cities shared a commitment to civic engagement, strong 
families, and a tight-knit community that worked for the wellbeing of 
all its citizens. We can see the results of those values today.
  On its 125th birthday, Golden Valley has much to be proud of, a high 
quality education system, high living standards, and model businesses 
ranging from Fortune 500 companies to family-owned small businesses. 
Clearly, Golden Valley is doing something right.
  As a representative of the great people of Minnesota, I can see that 
it's cities like Golden Valley make my State the best place to live in 
the country. My colleagues here might get tired of hearing how our 
State consistently does things better, but I will never get tired of 
telling those stories. Congratulations to the residents of Golden 
Valley.

                          ____________________
```
