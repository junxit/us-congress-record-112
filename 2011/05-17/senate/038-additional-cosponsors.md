                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 20

  At the request of Mr. Hatch, the name of the Senator from Nevada (Mr. 
Heller) was added as a cosponsor of S. 20, a bill to protect American 
job creation by striking the job-killing Federal employer mandate.


                                 S. 28

  At the request of Mr. Rockefeller, the name of the Senator from 
Minnesota (Mr. Franken) was added as a cosponsor of S. 28, a bill to 
amend the Communications Act of 1934 to provide public safety providers 
an additional 10 megahertz of spectrum to support a national, 
interoperable wireless broadband network and authorize the Federal 
Communications Commission to hold incentive auctions to provide funding 
to support such a network, and for other purposes.

(Mr. Heller) was added as a cosponsor of S. 84, a bill to amend the
