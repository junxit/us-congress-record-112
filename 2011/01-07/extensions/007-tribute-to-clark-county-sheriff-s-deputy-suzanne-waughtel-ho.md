# TRIBUTE TO CLARK COUNTY SHERIFF'S DEPUTY SUZANNE WAUGHTEL HOPPER

**Speaking:** Austria, Steve (A000365) [R-OH]

```
    TRIBUTE TO CLARK COUNTY SHERIFF'S DEPUTY SUZANNE WAUGHTEL HOPPER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. STEVE AUSTRIA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 7, 2011

  Mr. AUSTRIA. Mr. Speaker, today I will be traveling back to Ohio to 
express my sympathy to the family of Clark County Sheriff's Deputy 
Suzanne Waughtel Hopper. Deputy Hopper's funeral will be at 11:00 a.m. 
on Friday, January 7, 2011.
  Deputy Hopper was killed in the line of duty on New Year's Day while 
responding to shots fired in a local community. Because of her decisive 
action, courage and bravery lives were saved. She is a true American 
hero.

                          ____________________
```
