# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                  Washington, DC, January 7, 2011.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Under Clause 2(g) of Rule II of the Rules 
     of the U.S. House of Representatives, I herewith designate 
     Robert Reeves, Deputy Clerk, to sign any and all papers and 
     do all other acts for me under the name of the Clerk of the 
     House which they would be authorized to do by virtue of this 
     designation, except such as are provided by statute, in case 
     of my temporary absence or disability.
       This designation shall remain in effect for the 112th 
     Congress or until modified by me.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
                                                    Karen L. Haas,
     Clerk of the House.

                          ____________________
```
