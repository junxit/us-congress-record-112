# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 255

**Measures:** H.R. 255

```
           By Mr. SERRANO:
       H.R. 255.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3, which gives Congress the 
     power ``To regulate Commerce

[[Page H138]]

     with foreign Nations,'' and Article I, Section 8, Clause 18, 
     which gives Congress the power ``To make all Laws which shall 
     be necessary and proper for carrying into Execution the 
     foregoing Powers.''
```
