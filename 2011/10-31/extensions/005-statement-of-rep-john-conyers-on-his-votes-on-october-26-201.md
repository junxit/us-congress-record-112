# STATEMENT OF REP. JOHN CONYERS ON HIS VOTES ON OCTOBER 26, 2011

**Speaking:** Conyers, John, Jr. (C000714) [D-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 1904

```
    STATEMENT OF REP. JOHN CONYERS ON HIS VOTES ON OCTOBER 26, 2011

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOHN CONYERS, JR.

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, October 31, 2011

  Mr. CONYERS. Mr. Speaker, on October 26, 2011, I was absent and 
unable to vote on two amendments to H.R. 1904 offered by my colleagues 
Mr. Markey of Massachusetts and Mr. Lujan of New Mexico. Had I been 
present, I would have voted ``yea'' on both amendments.
```
