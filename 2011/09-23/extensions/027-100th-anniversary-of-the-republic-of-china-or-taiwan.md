# 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE REPUBLIC OF CHINA--OR TAIWAN

**Speaking:** Myrick, Sue Wilkins (M001134) [R-NC]

```
         100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE REPUBLIC OF CHINA--OR TAIWAN

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. SUE WILKINS MYRICK

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 23, 2011

  Mrs. MYRICK. Mr. Speaker, October 10 marks the 100th Anniversary of 
The Republic of China--or Taiwan. I urge my colleagues to join me in 
recognizing this important occasion.
  The partnership between the United States and Taiwan is vital within 
the international community. Both of our countries benefit from a 
robust trading relationship, and the support that Taiwan has shown the 
United States in times of need cannot be overlooked.
  In recognizing the centennial of Taiwan, we are also recognizing the 
numerous cultural and political accomplishments of this proud nation. 
Within Taiwan, we see a flourishing democracy--where free speech and 
political discourse are encouraged--and applaud the perseverance of 
those who have worked to protect the civil rights of the Taiwanese 
people.
  I ask that my colleagues join me in recognizing the accomplishments 
Taiwan has made over the past 100 years, and that we will all continue 
to foster the important relationship that exists between the United 
States and one of our most important Asian allies.

                          ____________________
```
