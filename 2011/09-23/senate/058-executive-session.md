# EXECUTIVE SESSION

```
                           EXECUTIVE SESSION

                                 ______
                                 

                         NOMINATIONS DISCHARGED

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to executive session and the HELP Committee be discharged from 
further consideration of PN-924, 567 nominations in the Public Health 
Service received by the Senate on September 8, 2011, beginning with 
Aysha Z. Akhtar and ending with Mykah N. Wynter; that the nominations 
be confirmed en bloc, the motions to reconsider be considered made and 
laid upon the table, with no intervening action or debate; that no 
further motions be in order to the nominations; that any related 
statements be printed in the Record; and that the President be 
immediately notified of the Senate's action.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
