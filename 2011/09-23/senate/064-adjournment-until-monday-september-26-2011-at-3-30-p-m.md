# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2011, AT 3:30 P.M.

```
       ADJOURNMENT UNTIL MONDAY, SEPTEMBER 26, 2011, AT 3:30 P.M.

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate adjourn 
under the previous order, with the understanding that the vote at 5:30

be reasonable. We cannot leave it open forever.

Monday, September 26, 2011, at 3:30 p.m.
```
