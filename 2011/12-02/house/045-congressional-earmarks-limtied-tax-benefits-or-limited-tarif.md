# CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMTIED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF BENEFITS

**Measures:** H.R. 10

```
    CONGRESSIONAL EARMARKS, LIMTIED TAX BENEFITS, OR LIMITED TARIFF 
                                BENEFITS

  Under clause 9 of rule XXI, lists or statements on congressional 
earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited tariff benefits were 
submitted as follows:

       The amendment to be offered by Representative Sessions, or 
     a designee, to H.R. 10, the Regulations From the Executive in 
     Need of Scrutiny Act of 2011, does not contain any 
     congressional earmarks, limited tax benefits, or limited 
     tariff benefits as defined in clause 9 of rule XXI.
```
