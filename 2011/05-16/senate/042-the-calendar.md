# The CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S. 349, S. 655, H.R. 793

```
                              The CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the Senate proceed 
to the following postal naming bills, en bloc: Calendar No. 46, 47, 48; 
S. 349, S. 655, and H.R. 793.

ordered.
  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent the bills be read the third time 
and passed en bloc; that the motions to reconsider be laid upon the 
table en bloc, with no intervening action or debate; and any statements 
relating to this matter be printed in the Record.
```
