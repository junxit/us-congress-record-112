# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

**Speaking:** Cicilline, David N. (C001084) [D-RI]

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. DAVID N. CICILLINE

                            of rhode island

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 10, 2011

  Mr. CICILLINE. Mr. Speaker, on January 25, 2011, I was listed as 
``not voting'' for House Resolution 49, to provide Capitol-flown flags 
for recipients of the Medal of Honor. I intended to vote ``yea'' on 
this resolution, as I support the provision of flags flown over the 
Capitol for those who receive the Medal of Honor. I also wish to 
express my appreciation for Staff Sergeant Salvatore A. Giunta for his 
valiant and courageous service to our country.
```
