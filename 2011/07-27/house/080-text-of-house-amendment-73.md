# Text of House Amendment 73

**Speaking:** Posey, Bill (P000599) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                          Offered by Mr. Posey

       Amendment No. 73: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) add the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used by the Department of the Interior for any new oil or 
     gas drilling above the Outer Continental Shelf within 25-
     miles of the State of Florida.
```
