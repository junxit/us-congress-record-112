# Text of House Amendment 77

**Speaking:** Landry, Jeffrey M. (L000574) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                         Offered by Mr. Landry

       Amendment No. 77: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


     funding limitation related to regulation of offshore service 
                              contractors

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used to regulate, under the Outer Continental Shelf 
     Lands Act (43 U.S.C. 1331 et seq.), any person that is not a 
     lessee under that Act.
```
