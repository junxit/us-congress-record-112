# Text of House Amendment 74

**Speaking:** Labrador, Raul R. (L000573) [R-ID]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                        Offered by Mr. Labrador

       Amendment No. 74: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to develop or implement a comprehensive conservation 
     plan under the National Wildlife Refuge System Administration 
     Act of 1966 (16 U.S.C. 668dd et seq.) for the Lake Lowell 
     Unit of the Deer Flat National Wildlife Refuge.
```
