# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that we extend the 
morning business hour until 7 p.m.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. REID. I also ask that the consent agreement be modified that 
Senators be allowed to speak for up to 10 minutes each during that 
period of time.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
