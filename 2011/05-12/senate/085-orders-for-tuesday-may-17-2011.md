# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 17, 2011

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                    ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MAY 17, 2011

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent on Tuesday, May 17, 2011, at 10 
a.m., the Senate proceed to executive session to consider Calendar No. 
31, that there be 2 hours for debate equally divided in the usual form, 
that upon the use or yielding back of that time the Senate proceed to 
vote, without intervening action or debate, on Calendar No. 31; the 
motion to reconsider be considered made and laid on the table, with no 
intervening action or debate; that no further motions be in order to 
the nomination, and statements related to the nomination be printed in 
the Record; the President be immediately notified and the Senate resume 
legislative session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
