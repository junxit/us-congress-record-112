  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I am told that S. 953 is at the desk and due 
for a second reading.

title for the second time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A bill (S. 953) to authorize the conduct of certain lease

     Continental Shelf Lands Act to modify the requirements for

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I object to any further proceedings with 
respect to this bill.
