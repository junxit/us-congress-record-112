participants of Virginia's Area 2 Special Olympics competition in 
Virginia Beach on Saturday, May 14, 2011.

congratulations as they compete and use the skills they have worked 
hard to develop and finesse. My wife and I consider it a high honor to 
attend Saturday's event, and today, I want to acknowledge each 
competitor.
