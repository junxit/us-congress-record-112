                            PETITIONS, ETC.

     Energy and Commerce.

     Georgia, relative to submitting to Congress a piece of

       31. Also, a petition of Southern States Energy Board, 
     Georgia, relative to submitting a piece of proposed 
     legislation; to the Committee on Ways and Means.

     legislation; to the Committee on Ways and Means.
