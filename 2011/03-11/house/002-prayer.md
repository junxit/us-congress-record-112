# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  From the depths, O Lord, You call out for us to be one in mind and 
one in heart. In an ever-changing world, the focus of our prayer, our 
energies, and our concern can easily shift day by day. With all our 
diversity, even united we stand before You very vulnerable.
  Today, we pray for our families, friends, and constituents out in the 
Pacific and its surrounding coasts. Be with these island people who 
touch the Members and Delegates of this Congress who have represented 
them always, even in their hour of need.
  From our depths, O Lord, we cry out: Have mercy, O Lord. Have mercy.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
