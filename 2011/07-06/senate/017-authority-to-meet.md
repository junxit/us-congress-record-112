# AUTHORITY TO MEET

**Speaking:** Lautenberg, Frank R. (L000123) [D-NJ]

```

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that if the 
Finance Committee meets tomorrow at 9 a.m., it be authorized to meet 
during tomorrow's session of the Senate.

  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The bill clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
