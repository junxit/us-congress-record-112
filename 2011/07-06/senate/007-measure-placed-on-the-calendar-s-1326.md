# MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1326

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                MEASURE PLACED ON THE CALENDAR--S. 1326

  Mr. REID. S. 1326 is at the desk. It is due for a second reading.
```
