# Text of House Amendment 76

**Speaking:** Shuler, Heath (S001171) [D-NC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                         Offered By: Mr. Shuler

       Amendment No. 76: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to restrict cooperation between employees of the 
     Department of Defense and employees of the Department of 
     Homeland Security.
```
