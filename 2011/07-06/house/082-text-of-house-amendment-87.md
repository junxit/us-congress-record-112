# Text of House Amendment 87

**Speaking:** Murphy, Christopher (M001169) [D-CT]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                 Offered By: Mr. Murphy of Connecticut

       Amendment No. 87: Page 160, after line 3, add the 
     following:
       (G) A discussion of whether and how Pakistan discriminates 
     against religious minorities by requiring denunciations of 
     particular religious minorities or sects on passport 
     applications and other instruments of state.
```
