# Text of House Amendment 91

**Speaking:** Lipinski, Daniel (L000563) [D-IL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2219

```
H.R. 2219

                        Offered By: Mr. Lipinski

       Amendment No. 91: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used by the Department of Defense to replace an 
     information technology system that stores classified 
     information in the United States with an information 
     technology system that stores such classified information 
     outside the United States.
```
