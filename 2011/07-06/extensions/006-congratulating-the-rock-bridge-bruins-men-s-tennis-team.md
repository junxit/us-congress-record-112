# CONGRATULATING THE ROCK BRIDGE BRUINS MEN'S TENNIS TEAM

```
        CONGRATULATING THE ROCK BRIDGE BRUINS MEN'S TENNIS TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 6, 2011

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating the Rock Bridge Bruins Men's Tennis team for winning the 
Class 2 A Missouri State Championship on May 26.
  These young men and their coaches should be commended for all their 
hard work throughout the regular season and bringing home the 2 A 
Tennis Championship to their school and community.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing the Rock Bridge Bruins for a 
job well done.

                          ____________________
```
