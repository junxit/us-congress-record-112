# CONGRATULATING LA PLATA HIGH SCHOOL BULLDOGS MEN'S BASEBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
    CONGRATULATING LA PLATA HIGH SCHOOL BULLDOGS MEN'S BASEBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 6, 2011

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating the La Plata High School Bulldogs Men's Baseball Team 
for winning the Class 1 A Missouri State Championship on June 2.
  These young men and their coaches should be commended for all their 
hard work throughout the regular season and bringing home the 1 A 
Baseball Championship to their school and community.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing the La Plata Bulldogs for a job 
well done.

                          ____________________
```
