# CONGRATULATING THE NEW BLOOMFIELD WILDCATS MEN'S BASEBALL TEAM

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
     CONGRATULATING THE NEW BLOOMFIELD WILDCATS MEN'S BASEBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 6, 2011

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating the New Bloomfield Wildcats Men's Baseball Team for 
winning the Class 2A Missouri State Championship on June 2.
  These young men and their coaches should be commended for all their 
hard work throughout the regular season and bringing home the 2A 
Baseball Championship to their school and community.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing the New Bloomfield Wildcats for 
a job well done.

                          ____________________
```
