# WITHDRAWAL

```
                               WITHDRAWAL

  Executive message transmitted by the President to the Senate on June

       PETER A. DIAMOND, OF MASSACHUSETTS, TO BE A MEMBER OF THE

     FREDERIC S. MISHKIN, WHICH WAS SENT TO THE SENATE ON JANUARY 
     5, 2011.
```
