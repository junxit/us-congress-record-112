# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Almighty God, life of our life, You have given us this Nation for our 
heritage. Today, we ask that You will keep us mindful of Your favor and 
glad to do Your will. Use the Members of this body to uphold the public 
interest, to labor for justice, to love mercy, and to walk humbly with 
You. Give them the wisdom to use their power for the healing of our 
land. Keep their goals high, vision clear, and minds keen.
  And, Lord, we ask Your choicest blessings upon our departing page 
class.
  We pray in Your righteous name.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
