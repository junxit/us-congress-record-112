# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE POMPEO

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, July 20, 2011

  Mr. POMPEO. Mr. Speaker, on July 18th, I missed rollcall votes 
numbered 601 and 602 because I was in Kansas on official business.
  Rollcall No. 601 was a vote on passage of H.R. 33, to amend the 
Securities Act of 1933 to specify when certain securities issued in 
connection with church plans are treated as exempted securities. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``yes.''
  Rollcall No. 602 was a vote on the Approval of the Journal. Had I 
been present, I would have voted ``yes.''
```
