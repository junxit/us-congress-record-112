# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following any leader remarks, the Senate 
will be in a period of morning business for 1 hour. The majority will 
control the first half and the Republicans the final half. Following 
morning business, the Senate will resume consideration of the military 
construction appropriations bill. There will be a rollcall vote at noon 
on that matter in relation to the Vitter amendment. There are four 
other amendments pending. We hope to complete action on this bill 
today. We will notify all Senators when the votes will occur.
```
