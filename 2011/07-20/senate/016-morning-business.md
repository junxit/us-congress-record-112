# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent 
that the Senate proceed to a period of morning business until 2:15 
p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.

[[Page 11484]]

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. DURBIN. I ask unanimous consent that the order for the quorum 
call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
