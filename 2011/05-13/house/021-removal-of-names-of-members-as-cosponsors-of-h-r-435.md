# REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 435

**Speaking:** Miller, Candice S. (M001150) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 435

```
         REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 435

  Mrs. MILLER of Michigan. Mr. Speaker, due to a clerical error, I ask 
unanimous consent that the following cosponsors be removed from the 
permanent record as cosponsors of H.R. 435: Representative Rigell, 
Representative Adams, and Representative Walden.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Michigan?
  There was no objection.
```
