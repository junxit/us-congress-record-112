# PETITIONS, ETC.

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
Clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       19. The SPEAKER presented a petition of AFL-CIO, Illinois, 
     relative to supporting the AT&T/T-Mobile Merger Resolution; 
     to the Committee on Education and the Workforce.
       20. Also, a petition of Charter Township of Brownstown, 
     Michigan, relative to a letter urging the Congress to 
     reconsider the budget reductions to the Fish and Wildlife 
     department; to the Committee on Natural Resources.
```
