# APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO JOINT SELECT COMMITTEE ON DEFICIT REDUCTION

```
 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBERS TO JOINT SELECT COMMITTEE ON DEFICIT REDUCTION
```
