# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:09 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 1343. An act to return unused or reclaimed funds made 
     available for broadband awards in the American Recovery and 
     Reinvestment Act of 2009 to the Treasury of the United 
     States.


                         Enrolled Bills Signed

  The President pro tempore (Mr. Inouye) announced that on today, 
October 6, 2011, he had signed the following enrolled bills, previously 
signed by the Speaker of the House:

       H.R. 771. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1081 Elbel Road in Schertz, 
     Texas, as the ``Schertz Veterans Post Office''.
       H.R. 1632. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 5014 Gary Avenue in Lubbock, 
     Texas, as the ``Sergeant Chris Davis Post Office''.
```
