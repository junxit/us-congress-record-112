  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Resolution 419 and rule 
XVIII, the Chair declares the House in the Committee of the Whole House 
on the state of the Union for the further consideration of the bill, 
H.R. 2681.

                              {time}  1030

Whole House on the state of the Union for the further consideration of 
the bill (H.R. 2681) to provide additional time for the Administrator

Denham (Acting Chair) in the chair.

proceedings were postponed, in the following order:
  Amendment No. 23 by Mr. Cohen of Tennessee.
