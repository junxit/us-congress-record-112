# SIGNING AUTHORITY

```
                           SIGNING AUTHORITY

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that during the 
adjournment of the Senate, the majority leader or Senator Rockefeller 
be authorized to sign duly enrolled bills or joint resolutions.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
