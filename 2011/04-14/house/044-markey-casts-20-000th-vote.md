# MARKEY CASTS 20,000TH VOTE

**Speaking:** Dingell, John D. (D000355) [D-MI]

```
                              {time}  1630
                       MARKEY CASTS 20,000TH VOTE

  (Mr. DINGELL asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. DINGELL. Mr. Speaker, I take pride in announcing to the House 
that our good friend and colleague, Mr. Markey of Massachusetts, is 
joining a select group of Members who have cast 20,000 votes in this 
great body.
  I know, Mr. Speaker, that the Members wish to congratulate this 
distinguished Member for a long period of outstanding service to the 
public.
```
