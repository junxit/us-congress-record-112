# REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 61

**Speaking:** Scalise, Steve (S001176) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 61

```

  Mr. SCALISE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the following
```
