# TRIBUTE TO ANDREW MIROLLI

```
                       TRIBUTE TO ANDREW MIROLLI

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PHIL GINGREY

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, February 17, 2011

  Mr. GINGREY of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
Andrew Mirolli, a young student from Acworth, Georgia, who has been 
awarded the 2011 Prudential Spirit of Community Award. Andrew's efforts 
in combating poverty, both in our community and around the world, are 
truly deserving of this great honor.
  Mr. Speaker, Andrew is working to raise money for a local food pantry 
and a project in Uganda to rebuild a girls' dormitory. So far, he has 
raised $12,480. The world is made a better place by selfless service 
like Andrew has exemplified.
  With his receipt of the Prudential Spirit of Community Award, Andrew 
has been named one of the top youth volunteers in the State of Georgia. 
I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating this young man on his 
achievements.

                          ____________________
```
