# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. I ask unanimous consent that a time to be determined by the 
majority leader in consultation with the Republican leader, the Senate 
proceed to executive session to consider Calendar No. 410; that there 
be 4 hours of debate equally divided in the usual form; that upon the 
use or yielding back of that time, the Senate proceed to vote, without 
intervening action or debate, on Calendar No. 410, and that the 
nomination be subject to a 60-vote threshold; that no further motions 
be in order to the nomination; that any statements related to the 
nomination be printed in the Record; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action, and the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
