# PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Nelson, Bill (N000032) [D-FL]

**Measures:** S. 1323, H.R. 2055

```
                                PROGRAM

  Mr. NELSON of Florida. Madam President, there will be up to two 
rollcall votes at approximately 10:30 a.m. tomorrow. The first vote 
will be on the motion to invoke cloture on S. 1323, the sense-of-the-
Senate bill on shared sacrifice in resolving the budget deficit. If 
cloture is not invoked, there will be a second cloture vote on the 
motion to proceed to H.R. 2055, the Military Construction, Veterans 
Affairs and Related Agencies appropriations bill.
```
