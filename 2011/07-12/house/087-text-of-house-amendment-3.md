# Text of House Amendment 3

**Speaking:** Rigell, E. Scott (R000589) [R-VA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2434

```
H.R. 2434

                         Offered By: Mr. Rigell

       Amendment No. 3: At the end of the bill (before the short
```
