# MEASURE READ FIRST TIME--S.J. RES. 26

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.J.Res. 26

```
                 MEASURE READ FIRST TIME--S.J. RES. 26

  Mr. REID. I understand there is a joint resolution at the desk due 
for its first reading.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will report the joint resolution by 
title for the first time.
  The assistant legislative clerk read as follows:

       A joint resolution (S.J. Res. 26) expressing the sense of 
     the Congress that Secretary of the Treasury Timothy Geithner 
     no longer holds the confidence of Congress or of the people 
     of the United States.

  Mr. REID. I now ask for its second reading, and in order to place the 
joint resolution on the calendar under the provisions of rule XIV, I 
object to my own request.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Objection is heard. The joint resolution will 
receive its second reading on the next legislative day.

                          ____________________
```
