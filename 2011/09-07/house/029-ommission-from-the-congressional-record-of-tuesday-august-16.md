OMMISSION FROM THE CONGRESSIONAL RECORD OF TUESDAY, AUGUST 16, 2011 AT

                                 ______
                                 

               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                         House of Representatives,


                                          Office of the Clerk,

                                  Washington, DC, August 12, 2011.
     Hon. John A. Boehner,
     The Speaker, The Capitol, House of Representatives, 
         Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, I have the honor to transmit a sealed 
     envelope received from the White House on August 12, 2011, at 
     11:20 a.m., and said to contain a message from the President 
     whereby he notifies the Congress that he has extended the 
     national emergency with respect to the lapse of the Export 
     Administration Act of 1979, as amended.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
                                                 Robert F. Reeves,
     Deputy Clerk.
