# prayer

```
                               prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Merciful God, take possession of our hearts so that we will do Your 
will. Use us for Your glory as beacons of light and inspiration in our 
Nation and world. We desire for Your name to receive the honor it is 
due. So show us Your ways and teach us Your path. Lord, be gracious to 
the Members of this body, showering them liberally with Your wisdom. 
Let Your love fill and rule their lives, preparing them for that bliss 
You will give to those who love You.
  We pray in Your great name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
