# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, last night we were finally able to arrive 
at an agreement on the small business jobs bill--or at least a way to 
get rid of some very important amendments that we will vote on around 4

a.m., with the time until 10:40 a.m. equally divided and controlled
```
