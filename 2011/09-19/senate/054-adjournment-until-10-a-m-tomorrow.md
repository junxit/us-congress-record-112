# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 271

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that it adjourn under the 
provisions of S. Res. 271, as a further mark of respect to the memory 
of the late Senator Charles Percy of Illinois.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 6:35 p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, September 20, 2011, at 10 a.m.

                          ____________________
```
