# Text of House Amendment 152

**Speaking:** Jenkins, Lynn (J000290) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 1

```
H.R. 1

                        Offered By: Ms. Jenkins

       Amendment No. 152: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following new section:

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to remove (or to require the removal) at any former 
     Army ammunition plant closed under the base closure process 
     of pesticides that were applied in compliance with laws at 
     the time of application and of polychlorinated biphenyls to 
     an extent beyond that required by law.
```
