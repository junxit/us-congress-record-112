# AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 1249, AMERICA INVENTS ACT

```
AUTHORIZING THE CLERK TO MAKE CORRECTIONS IN ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 1249, 
                          AMERICA INVENTS ACT

  Mr. SMITH of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
Clerk be authorized to make technical corrections in the engrossment of 
H.R. 1249, to include corrections in spelling, punctuation, section

gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
```
