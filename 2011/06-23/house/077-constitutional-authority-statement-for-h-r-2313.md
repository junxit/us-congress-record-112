# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2313

**Measures:** H.R. 2313

```
           By Mrs. McMORRIS RODGERS:
       H.R. 2313.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is enacted pursuant to Congress' legislative 
     powers under Article I, Section 9, that no money shall be 
     drawn from the Treasury but in consequence of Appropriations 
     made by Law, and a regular Statement and Account of the 
     Receipts and Expenditures of all public Money shall be made 
     from time to time.
```
