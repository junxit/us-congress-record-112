# TRIPP, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                          TRIPP, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Tripp, SD. The 
town of Tripp will commemorate its 125th anniversary this year.
  Tripp was founded in 1886 and was named after Judge Bartlett C. 
Tripp, who served as President of Dakota Territory's first Territorial 
Constitutional Convention. Located in Hutchinson County, today Tripp is 
home to beautiful prairies and excellent hunting.
  Tripp has been a successful and thriving community for the past 125 
years, and I am confident that it will continue to serve as an example 
of South Dakota values and traditions. I would like to offer my 
congratulations to the citizens of Tripp on this landmark date.●

                          ____________________
```
