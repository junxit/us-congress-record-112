# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the period of 
morning business for debate only be extended until 7 p.m. tonight, with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each, and that at 7 
p.m. I be recognized.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Is there objection? Without objection, it is 
so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
