# CELEBRATING NATIONAL AUCTION WEEK

**Speaking:** Duncan, Jeff (D000615) [R-SC]

```
                              {time}  1210
                   CELEBRATING NATIONAL AUCTION WEEK

  (Mr. DUNCAN of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute.)
  Mr. DUNCAN of South Carolina. On this day in 1743, our Founding 
Father, Thomas Jefferson, was born.
  But I rise today to talk about the National Auctioneers Association 
celebrating National Auction Week. I'm proud, as an auctioneer for over 
16 years, to serve with fellow auctioneer in our Auction Caucus here in 
Congress, Representative Billy Long from Missouri.
  Auction and auctioneers help families and businesses all over this 
great land sell trillions of dollars worth of assets every year.
  And so I will leave you with this thought: Hey, now, wouldya give 25 
now, 35--sold.
  And we sell it every day.
```
