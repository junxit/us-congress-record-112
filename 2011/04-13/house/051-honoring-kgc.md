# HONORING KGC

```
                              {time}  1750
                              HONORING KGC

  (Mr. DOLD asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. DOLD. Mr. Speaker, today I rise because I had the opportunity to 
attend the KGC this last weekend, an event raising resources to battle 
depression. Depression affects over 20 million adults in our Nation. 
This is something that we all need to be paying more attention to.
  I want to thank Chairman Bennett for his leadership. I also want to 
thank Kevin Haggard, Andrew Boyle, Phil Furse and Tom Joyce for their 
generous contributions to the event. I also want to extend my heartfelt 
thanks to Andrew Boyle for his leadership for next year's event.

                          ____________________
```
