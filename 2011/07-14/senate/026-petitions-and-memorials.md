# PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

```
                        PETITIONS AND MEMORIALS

  The following petitions and memorials were laid before the Senate and 
were referred or ordered to lie on the table as indicated:

       POM-57. A resolution adopted by the Legislature of Rockland 
     County, New York, urging the Federal Communications 
     Commission to adopt and implement proposed rules that would 
     require mobile service providers to provide usage alerts and 
     information that will assist consumers in avoiding unexpected 
     charges on their bills; to the Committee on Commerce, 
     Science, and Transportation.
       POM-58. A resolution adopted by the Legislature of Rockland 
     County, New York, requesting that the United States House of 
     Representatives pass bill H.R. 1268--The Nuclear Power 
     Licensing Reform Act of 2011; to the Committee on Environment 
     and Public Works.
```
