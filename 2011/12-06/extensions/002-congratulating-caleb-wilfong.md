# CONGRATULATING CALEB WILFONG

```
                      CONGRATULATING CALEB WILFONG

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 6, 2011

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating Caleb Wilfong of the Rock Bridge High School 
Bruins Boys Cross Country team for winning the individual Class 4 
Missouri State Championship.
  Mr. Wilfong should be commended for all of his hard work throughout 
the regular season and bringing home the individual state title to his 
school, family and community. At the State Cross Country Championships 
in Jefferson City, Caleb won the individual state title with a time of 
15 minutes 54.13 seconds. He holds Rock Bridge's school record for a 
3.1-mile race and is the school's second individual cross country 
champion.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing Caleb Wilfong for a job well 
done.

                          ____________________
```
