# CONGRATULATING THE ROCK BRIDGE HIGH SCHOOL BRUINS BOYS CROSS COUNTRY TEAM

**Speaking:** Luetkemeyer, Blaine (L000569) [R-MO]

```
 CONGRATULATING THE ROCK BRIDGE HIGH SCHOOL BRUINS BOYS CROSS COUNTRY 
                                  TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BLAINE LUETKEMEYER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 6, 2011

  Mr. LUETKEMEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to ask my colleagues to 
join me in congratulating the Rock Bridge High School Bruins Boys Cross 
Country team for winning the Class 4 Missouri State Championship.
  The young men and their coaches should be commended for all of their 
hard work throughout the regular season and bringing home the state 
title to their school and community. At the State Cross Country 
Championships in Jefferson City, the Bruins won the state title with a 
score of 61, 28 points better than second place. They have made history 
for the school, as it is the Rock Bridge boys' first state title.
  I ask that you join me in recognizing the Rock Bridge Bruins for a 
job well done!

                          ____________________
```
