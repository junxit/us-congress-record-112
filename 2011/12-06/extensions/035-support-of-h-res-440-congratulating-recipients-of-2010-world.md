# SUPPORT OF H. RES. 440 CONGRATULATING RECIPIENTS OF 2010 WORLD PEACE PRIZE H.H. DORJE CHANG BUDDHA III AND THE HONORABLE BEN GILMAN

```
 SUPPORT OF H. RES. 440 CONGRATULATING RECIPIENTS OF 2010 WORLD PEACE 
     PRIZE H.H. DORJE CHANG BUDDHA III AND THE HONORABLE BEN GILMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ELIOT L. ENGEL

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 6, 2011

  Mr. ENGEL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to express my strong support of 
H. Res. 440 which congratulates H.H. Dorje Chang Buddha III and the 
Honorable Ben Gilman for winning the 2010 World Peace Prize.
  Recently, I introduced this resolution to highlight the awarding of 
the World Peace Prize to both H.H. Dorje Chang Buddha III and 
Congressman Gilman. The World Peace Prize is a very distinguished honor 
granted by the World Peace Council in recognition of individuals who 
exemplify selflessness in their devotion to humanity.
  I commend H.H. Dorje Chang Buddha III and the Honorable Ben Gilman 
for their multiple contributions to our society and urge my colleagues 
to support H. Res. 440.

                          ____________________
```
