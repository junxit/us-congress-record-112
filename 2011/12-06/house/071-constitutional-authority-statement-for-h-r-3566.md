# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3566

**Measures:** H.R. 3566

```
           By Mr. TOWNS:
       H.R. 3566.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This Bill is enacted pursuant to Article I, Section 8, 
     Clause 3 of the United States Constitution. This provision 
     grants Congress the power to regulate Commerce with foreign 
     Nations, and among the several States and with the Indian 
     Tribes.
```
