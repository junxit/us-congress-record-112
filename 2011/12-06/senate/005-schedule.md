# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
be in a period of morning business until 11 a.m. this morning. The 
majority will control the first half and the Republicans will control 
the second half.


                           Order of Procedure

  I ask unanimous consent that the order be changed to allow both sides 
a half an hour.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  Mr. REID. Following morning business, the Senate will be in executive 
session to consider the nomination of Caitlin Halligan to be a judge 
for the District of Columbia Circuit. At noon there will be a cloture 
vote. I want to make sure that the consent I asked doesn't change that 
at all. There will be a little less time to debate that, but I think it 
will be sufficient. So at noon there will be a cloture vote on 
Halligan.
  Following the vote, the Senate will recess until 2:15 this afternoon 
to allow for our weekly caucus meetings.

                          ____________________
```
