# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

**Measures:** S. 1947, S. 1948

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. BLUMENTHAL (for himself, Mr. Kirk, Ms. Cantwell, 
             and Mr. Brown of Massachusetts):
       S. 1947. A bill to prohibit attendance of an animal 
     fighting venture, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Agriculture, Nutrition, and Forestry.
           By Mr. PRYOR (for himself and Mr. Wicker):
       S. 1948. A bill to establish an Innovation in Investment 
     pilot program, to improve and expand a national registered

     Education and Training Advisory Committees, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Finance.
```
