# RECESS

**Speaking:** Lautenberg, Frank R. (L000123) [D-NJ]

```
                                 RECESS

  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The Senate stands in recess until 2 p.m.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 1:10 p.m., recessed until 2 p.m. and 
reassembled when called to order by the Presiding Officer (Mr. 
Blumenthal).
  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. LAUTENBERG. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
