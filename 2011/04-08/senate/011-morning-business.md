# MORNING BUSINESS

```
                            MORNING BUSINESS

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. The Senate will be in a period of 
morning business until 4 p.m., with Senators permitted to speak for up 
to 10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and controlled 
between the two leaders or their designees, and any time spent in a 
quorum call will be equally divided.
  The Senator from Arizona.

```
