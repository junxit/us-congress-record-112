# TRIBUTE TO LAURA CURRAN

**Speaking:** Barrasso, John (B001261) [R-WY]

```
                        TRIBUTE TO LAURA CURRAN

  Mr. BARRASSO. Mr. President, I would like to take the opportunity to 
express my appreciation to Laura Curran for her hard work as an intern 
in my Cheyenne office. I recognize her efforts and contributions to my 
office as well as to the State of Wyoming.

[[Page S2345]]

  Laura is a native of Wyoming and graduated from Central High School. 
She graduated from the University of Wyoming where she majored in 
English and minored in creative writing. She has demonstrated a strong 
work ethic which has made her an invaluable asset to our office. The 
quality of her work is reflected in her great efforts over the last 
several months.
  I thank Laura for the dedication she has shown while working for me 
and my staff. It was a pleasure to have her as part of our team. I know 
she will have continued success with all of her future endeavors. I 
wish her all my best on her next journey.

                          ____________________
```
