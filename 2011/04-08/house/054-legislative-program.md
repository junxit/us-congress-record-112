# LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Cantor, Eric (C001046) [R-VA]

```
                          LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

  (Mr. CANTOR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CANTOR. Mr. Speaker, I would like to inform my colleagues that 
additional legislative business and votes are possible today.
  I would expect Members to have at least 1 hour's notice prior to any 
recorded votes. Due to ongoing negotiations, it is critical for the 
House to remain in legislative session.
  In addition, Mr. Speaker, I would remind my colleagues that in the 
case of a lapse in appropriations, I fully expect the House to meet 
tomorrow.
  We will provide further information as soon as it's available, but 
Members should continue to keep their schedule for this weekend as 
flexible as possible.

                          ____________________
```
