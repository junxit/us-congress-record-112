# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2553, AIRPORT AND AIRWAY EXTENSION ACT OF 2011, PART IV

```
REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 2553, AIRPORT 
               AND AIRWAY EXTENSION ACT OF 2011, PART IV

  Mr. WEBSTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 112-155) on the resolution (H. Res. 357) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 2553) to amend the Internal Revenue 
Code of 1986 to extend the funding and expenditure authority of the 
Airport and Airway Trust Fund, to amend title 49, United States Code, 
to extend the airport improvement program, and for other purposes, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
