# Text of House Amendment 1

**Speaking:** Thompson, Glenn (T000467) [R-PA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2551

```
H.R. 2551

                Offered by Mr. Thompson of Pennsylvania

       Amendment No. 1: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 211.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to purchase, acquire, install, or use any medium 
     screw base compact fluorescent lamp or light bulb.
```
