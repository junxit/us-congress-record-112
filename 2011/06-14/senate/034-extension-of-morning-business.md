**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV], Durbin, Richard J. (D000563) [D-IL]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that we be in a 
period of morning business, with Senators allowed to speak for up to 10 
minutes each, for debate only, until 7:30 today.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

the quorum call be rescinded.

is so ordered.
  Mr. DURBIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent to speak as in 
morning business.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
