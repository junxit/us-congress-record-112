# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate 
proceed to a period of morning business from now until 6:30 this 
evening, with Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each; 
that at 6:30 p.m. the majority leader be recognized, and that this work 
we are going to do during the next hour and a half be for debate only.
  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Without objection, it is so 
ordered.
  The Senator from Oregon.

                          ____________________
```
