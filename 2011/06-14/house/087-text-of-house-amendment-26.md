# Text of House Amendment 26

**Speaking:** Flores, Bill (F000461) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2112

```
H.R. 2112

                         Offered By: Mr. Flores

       Amendment No. 26: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. 4 __.  None of the funds made available by this Act 
     may be used to enforce section 526 of the Energy Independence 
     and Security Act of 2007 (Public Law 110-140; 42 U.S.C. 
     17142).
```
