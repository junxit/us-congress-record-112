# Text of House Amendment 25

**Speaking:** Kind, Ron (K000188) [D-WI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2112

```
H.R. 2112

                          Offered By: Mr. Kind

       Amendment No. 25: At the end of the bill (before any short 
     title), insert the following new section:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to provide payments (or to pay the salaries and 
     expenses of personnel to provide payments) to the Brazil 
     Cotton Institute.
```
