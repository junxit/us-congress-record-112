# Text of House Amendment 24

**Speaking:** Royce, Edward R. (R000487) [R-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2112

```
H.R. 2112

                         Offered By: Mr. Royce

       Amendment No. 24: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. 7xx.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to provide assistance under title II of the Food for 
     Peace Act (7 U.S.C. 1721 et seq.) to the Democratic People's 
     Republic of Korea (North Korea).
```
