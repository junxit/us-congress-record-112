# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3461

**Measures:** H.R. 3461

```
           By Mrs. CAPITO:
       H.R. 3461.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3, authorizing Congress to 
     regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the several 
     States, and with the Indian Tribes.
```
