# BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                    BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House reports that on November 16, 2011 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bill.

       H.R. 398. To amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to 
     toll, during active-duty service abroad in the Armed Forces, 
     the periods of time to file a petition and appear for an 
     interview to remove the conditional basis

[[Page 17795]]

     for permanent resident status, and for other purposes.
```
