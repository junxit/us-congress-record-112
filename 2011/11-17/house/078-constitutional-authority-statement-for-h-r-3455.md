# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 3455

**Measures:** H.R. 3455

```
           By Mr. PALAZZO:
       H.R. 3455.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1: Section 8 of the Constitution of the United 
     States of America,
       ``Congress shall have the power . . . To make laws for the 
     government and regulation of the land and naval forces.''
```
