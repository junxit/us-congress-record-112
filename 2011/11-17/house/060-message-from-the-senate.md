# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 2112

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

the disagreeing votes of the two Houses on the amendments of the Senate 
to the bill (H.R. 2112) ``An Act making consolidated appropriations for

Housing and Urban Development, and related programs for the fiscal year 
ending September 30, 2012, and for other purposes.''.
```
