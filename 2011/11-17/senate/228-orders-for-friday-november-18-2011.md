# ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, NOVEMBER 18, 2011

**Speaking:** Levin, Carl (L000261) [D-MI]

**Measures:** S. 1867

```
                          ORDERS FOR FRIDAY, 
                           NOVEMBER 18, 2011

  Mr. LEVIN. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
Senate completes its business today, it adjourn until 9 a.m. tomorrow, 
Friday, November 18, 2011; that following the prayer and pledge, the 
Journal of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed 
expired, and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use 
later in the day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate resume 
consideration of S. 1867, the Defense Authorization Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
```
