# Text of House Amendment 73

**Speaking:** Broun, Paul C. (B001262) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2354

```
H.R. 2354

                    Offered By: Mr. Broun of Georgia

       Amendment No. 73: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  The amounts otherwise provided by this Act are 
     revised by increasing the amount made available for the 
     Spending Reduction Account, and by reducing the amount made 
     available for ``Department of Energy--Energy Programs--Energy 
     Efficiency and Renewable Energy'', by $1,304,636,000.
```
