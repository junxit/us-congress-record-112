# Text of House Amendment 76

**Speaking:** Landry, Jeffrey M. (L000574) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2354

```
H.R. 2354

                         Offered By: Mr. Landry

       Amendment No. 76: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to pay the salary of individuals appointed to their 
     current position through, or otherwise carry out, paragraphs 
     (1), (2), and (3) of section 5503(a) of title 5, United 
     States Code.
```
