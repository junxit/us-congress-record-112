# Text of House Amendment 80

**Speaking:** Cravaack, Chip (C001086) [R-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2354

```
H.R. 2354

                        Offered By: Mr. Cravaack

       Amendment No. 80: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to develop or submit a proposal to expand the 
     authorized uses of the Harbor Maintenance Trust Fund 
     described in section 9505(c) of the Internal Revenue Code (26 
     U.S.C. 9505(c)).
```
