# UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
            UNANIMOUS CONSENT AGREEMENT--EXECUTIVE CALENDAR

  Mr. REID. Madam President, I ask unanimous consent that on Monday, 
October 17, at 5:15 p.m., the Senate proceed to executive session to 
consider Calendar No. 271; that there be 15 minutes for debate equally 
divided in the usual form; that upon the use or yielding back of time, 
the Senate proceed to vote with no intervening action or debate on 
Calendar No. 271; the motion to reconsider be considered made and laid 
upon the table, with no intervening action or debate, and any related 
statements be printed in the Record; that the President be immediately 
notified of the Senate's action and the Senate then resume legislative 
session.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
