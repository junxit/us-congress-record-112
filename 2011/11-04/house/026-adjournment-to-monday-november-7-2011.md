# ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 2011

**Speaking:** Farenthold, Blake (F000460) [R-TX]

```
                ADJOURNMENT TO MONDAY, NOVEMBER 7, 2011

  Mr. FARENTHOLD. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the 
House adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. on Monday, November 
7, 2011; when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to meet at 
2:30 p.m. on Thursday, November 10, 2011; and when the House adjourns 
on that day, it adjourn to meet at 2 p.m. on Monday, November 14, 2011.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.

                          ____________________
```
