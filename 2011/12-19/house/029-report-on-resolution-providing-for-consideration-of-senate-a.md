 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENTS

final measure to extend the payroll tax holiday, extend Federally

reimbursement for physicians who provide care to Medicare 
beneficiaries; and for other purposes, which was referred to the House
