# PERSONAL EXPLANATION

```
                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARTIN HEINRICH

                             of new mexico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 8, 2011

  Mr. HEINRICH. Mr. Speaker, I unfortunately missed three votes on 
January 26, 2011, which included rollcall votes Nos. 23, 24 and 25.
  If I had been present, I would have voted in favor of rollcall vote 
No. 23, Representative Gary Peters' (MI-09) amendment to H.R. 359.
  I would have also voted in favor of rollcall vote No. 24, the Motion 
to Recommit H.R. 359 offered by Representative Tim Walz (MN-01).
  Lastly, I would have voted against rollcall vote No. 25, the final 
passage of H.R. 359.
```
