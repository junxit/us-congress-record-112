# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following leader remarks the Senate will 
resume consideration of the Federal Aviation Administration Authority 
bill. At 10:20 there will be up to 10 minutes of debate equally divided

NASA.

relation to the Nelson of Florida amendment. Today will be only a short
```
