# COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

```
                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Nancy Pelosi, House Democratic Leader:

                                                     Nancy Pelosi,


                                            Democratic Leader,

                                                 February 8, 2011.
     Hon. John Boehner,
     Speaker of the House, U.S. Capitol, Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Boehner: Pursuant to Section 4404(c)(2) of the 
     Congressional Hunger Fellows Act of 2002 (2 U.S.C) 1161, I am 
     pleased to re-appoint Mr. James P. McGovern of Worcester, 
     Massachusetts to the Board of Trustees of the Congressional 
     Hunger Fellows Program.
       Thank you for your attention to this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Nancy Pelosi,
     House Democratic Leader.

                          ____________________
```
