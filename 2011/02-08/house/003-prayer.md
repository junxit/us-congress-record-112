# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Eternal and gracious Lord, nothing escapes Your attention. You read 
the intentions of our minds and the true desires of our hearts.
  May everything we do begin with Your holy inspiration, continue with 
Your sustaining grace, and reach Your divine purpose for the good of 
Your people, not just some people, but for the good of the entire 
Nation.
  So both in word and deed, may we give You glory now and forever. 
Amen.

                          ____________________
```
