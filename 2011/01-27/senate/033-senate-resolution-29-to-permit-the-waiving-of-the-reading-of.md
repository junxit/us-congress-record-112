# SENATE RESOLUTION 29--TO PERMIT THE WAIVING OF THE READING OF AN AMENDMENT IF THE TEXT AND ADEQUATE NOTICE ARE PROVIDED

**Measures:** S.Res. 29

```
   SENATE RESOLUTION 29--TO PERMIT THE WAIVING OF THE READING OF AN 
         AMENDMENT IF THE TEXT AND ADEQUATE NOTICE ARE PROVIDED

  Mr. UDALL of Colorado (for himself and Mr. Merkley) submitted the 
following resolution; which was considered and agreed to:

                               S. Res. 29

       Resolved,

     SECTION 1. READING OF AMENDMENTS.

       (a) Standing Order.--This section shall be a standing order 
     of the Senate.
       (b) Waiver.--The reading of an amendment may be waived by a 
     non-debatable motion if the amendment--
       (1) has been submitted at least 72 hours before the motion; 
     and
       (2) is available in printed or electronic form in the 
     Congressional Record.
```
