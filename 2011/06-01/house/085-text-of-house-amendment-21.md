# Text of House Amendment 21

**Speaking:** Polis, Jared (P000598) [D-CO]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                         Offered By: Mr. Polis

       Amendment No. 21: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. ___.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to detain any alien pending a decision on whether the 
     alien is to be removed from the United States, or an alien 
     ordered removed, if the alien has never been charged with a 
     felony in the United States.
```
