# Text of House Amendment 76

**Speaking:** Gohmert, Louie (G000552) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                        Offered By: Mr. Gohmert

       Amendment No. 76: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used for the new construction, purchase, or lease of any 
     building or space in the District of Columbia for any branch 
     of the United States Government except if a contract for the 
     construction, purchase, or lease was entered into before the 
     date of the enactment of this Act.
```
