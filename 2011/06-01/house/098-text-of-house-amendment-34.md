# Text of House Amendment 34

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 34: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. ___.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to carry out the provisions of Public Law 111-148, 
     Public Law 111-152, or any amendment made by either of such 
     laws.
```
