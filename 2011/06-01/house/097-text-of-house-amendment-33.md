# Text of House Amendment 33

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 33: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:

[[Page H3872]]

       Sec. ____.  None of the funds made available by this Act 
     may be used to delay compliance with title II of the REAL ID 
     Act of 2005 (49 U.S.C. 30301 note) beyond the January 15, 
     2013, deadline.
```
