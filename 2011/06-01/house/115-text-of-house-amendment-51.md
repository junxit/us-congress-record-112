# Text of House Amendment 51

**Speaking:** Gosar, Paul A. (G000565) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                         Offered By: Mr. Gosar

       Amendment No. 51: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to comply with subchapter IV of chapter 31 of title 
     40, United States Code, popularly known as the Davis-Bacon 
     Act.
```
