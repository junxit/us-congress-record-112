# Text of House Amendment 73

**Speaking:** Sherman, Brad (S000344) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                        Offered By: Mr. Sherman

       Amendment No. 73: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used in contravention of the War Powers Resolution (50 
     U.S.C. 1541 et seq.).
```
