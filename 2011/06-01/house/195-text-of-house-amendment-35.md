# Text of House Amendment 35

**Speaking:** King, Steve (K000362) [R-IA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                      Offered By: Mr. King of Iowa

       Amendment No. 35: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to administer the wage-rate requirements of 
     subchapter IV of chapter 31 of title 40, United States Code, 
     with respect to any project or program funded by this Act.
```
