# Text of House Amendment 46

**Speaking:** McCollum, Betty (M001143) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                        Offered By: Ms. McCollum

       Amendment No. 46: At the end of the bill (before any short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to enter into a contract, memorandum of 
     understanding, or cooperative agreement with or to make a 
     grant to any corporation that was convicted (or had an 
     officer or agent of such corporation acting on behalf of the 
     corporation convicted) of a felony criminal violation under 
     any Federal or State law within the preceding 24 months.
```
