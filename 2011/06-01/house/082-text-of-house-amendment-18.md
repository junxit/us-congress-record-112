# Text of House Amendment 18

**Speaking:** Speier, Jackie (S001175) [D-CA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                         Offered By: Ms. Speier

       Amendment No. 18: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), add the following new section:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to award a noncompetitively bid contract to an Alaska 
     Native Corporation, Indian Tribe, or Native Hawaiian 
     Organization in an amount in excess of the competitive 
     bidding threshold.
```
