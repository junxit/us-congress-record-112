# Text of House Amendment 45

**Speaking:** McCollum, Betty (M001143) [D-MN]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
H.R. 2017

                        Offered By: Ms. McCollum

       Amendment No. 45: At the end of the bill (before any short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to enter into a contract, memorandum of 
     understanding, or cooperative agreement with or to make a 
     grant to any corporation for which any unpaid Federal tax 
     liability has been assessed, all judicial and administrative 
     remedies have been exhausted or have lapsed, and such 
     liability is not being paid in a timely manner pursuant to an 
     agreement with the authority responsible for collecting such 
     liability.
```
