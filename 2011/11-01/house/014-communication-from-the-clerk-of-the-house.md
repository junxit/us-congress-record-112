```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Campbell) laid before the House the 
following communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                 Washington, DC, November 1, 2011.
     Hon. John A. Boehner
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, the Clerk received the following message 
     from the Secretary of the Senate of November 1, 2011 at 9:44 
     a.m.:
       That the Senate passed with amendments H.R. 394.
       That the Senate passed without amendment H.R. 368.
       That the Senate passed S. 1637.
       With best wishes, I am
           Sincerely,
     Karen L. Haas.
```
