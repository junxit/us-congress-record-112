# UNITED STATES PAROLE COMMISSION EXTENSION ACT OF 2011

**Speaking:** Smith, Lamar (S000583) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2944

```
         UNITED STATES PAROLE COMMISSION EXTENSION ACT OF 2011

  Mr. SMITH of Texas. Madam Speaker, I ask unanimous consent to take 
from the Speaker's table the bill (H.R. 2944) to provide for the 
continued performance of the functions of the United States Parole 
Commission, and for other purposes, with a Senate amendment thereto, 
and concur in the Senate amendment.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Clerk will report the Senate amendment.
  The Clerk read as follows:

       Senate amendment:
       On page 2, line 12, strike `` `27 years' or `27-year 
     period' '' and insert `` `26 years' or `26-year period' ''.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Texas?
  There was no objection.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
