# H.R. 2017, CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2012

**Speaking:** Mulvaney, Mick (M001182) [R-SC]

**Measures:** H.R. 2017

```
             H.R. 2017, CONTINUING APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2012

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICK MULVANEY

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, October 12, 2011

  Mr. MULVANEY. Mr. Speaker, H.R. 2017 would fund the Federal 
Government from October 1, 2011 through October 4, 2011. It was 
considered by a unanimous consent request, which does not require a 
recorded vote. Had a recorded vote been required, I would have voted 
against this spending bill.
```
