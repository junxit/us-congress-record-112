# SCHEDULE

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Madam President, following leader remarks, the Senate will 
begin consideration of the free-trade agreements. There are three of 
them. There will be up to 12 hours of debate on these matters. The 
Senate will have its normal recess from 12:30 p.m. until 2:15 p.m. 
today for our caucus meetings. We expect to yield back some of the 
time--I certainly hope so--on the trade agreements, although people can 
speak as much as they want on these matters. But we are going to 
complete the action tonight. Whether it is at 4 o'clock or midnight, we 
are going to complete action on these bills today. The House is 
awaiting our action.

                          ____________________
```
