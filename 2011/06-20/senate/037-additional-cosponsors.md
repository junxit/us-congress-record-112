                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 277

  At the request of Mr. Burr, the name of the Senator from Georgia (Mr. 
Isakson) was added as a cosponsor of S. 277, a bill to amend title 38, 
United States Code, to furnish hospital care, medical services, and 
nursing home care to veterans who were stationed at Camp Lejeune, North 
Carolina, while the water was contaminated at Camp Lejeune, and for 
other purposes.

  At the request of Mr. Cardin, the names of the Senator from Utah (Mr.

Senator from North Dakota (Mr. Hoeven) were added as cosponsors of S.

direct Israeli-Palestinian negotiations, reaffirming opposition to the

efforts to gain recognition of a state outside direct negotiations 
demonstrates absence of a good faith commitment to peace negotiations, 
and will have implications for continued United States aid.
