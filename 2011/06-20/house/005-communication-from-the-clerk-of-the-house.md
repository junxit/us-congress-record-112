# COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

```
               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, June 17, 2011.
     Hon. John A. Boehner,
     The Speaker, The Capitol, House of Representatives, 
         Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to the permission granted in 
     Clause 2(h) of Rule II of the Rules of the U.S. House of 
     Representatives, I have the honor to transmit a sealed 
     envelope received from the White House on June 17, 2011, at 
     10:50 a.m., and said to contain a message from the President 
     whereby he notifies the Congress he has extended the national 
     emergency with respect to highly enriched uranium in the 
     territory of the Russian Federation.
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                                    Karen L. Haas,
     Clerk of the House.

                          ____________________
```
