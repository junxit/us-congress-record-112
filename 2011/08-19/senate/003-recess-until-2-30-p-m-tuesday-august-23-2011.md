# RECESS UNTIL 2:30 P.M. TUESDAY, AUGUST 23, 2011

```
            RECESS UNTIL 2:30 P.M. TUESDAY, AUGUST 23, 2011

  The ACTING PRESIDENT pro tempore. Under the previous order, the 
Senate stands in recess until 2:30 p.m., Tuesday, August 23, 2011.
  Thereupon, the Senate, at 10 and 2 seconds a.m., recessed until 
Tuesday, August 23, 2011, at 2:30 p.m.
```
