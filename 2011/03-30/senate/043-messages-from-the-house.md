# MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

```
                        MESSAGES FROM THE HOUSE

  At 11:28 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bill, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 839. An act to amend the Emergency Economic 
     Stabilization Act of 2008 to terminate the authority of the 
     Secretary of the Treasury to provide new assistance under the 
     Home Affordable Modification Program, while preserving 
     assistance to homeowners who were already extended an offer 
     to participate in the Program, either on a trial or permanent 
     basis.


                          Enrolled Bill Signed

  At 5:25 p.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the Speaker has 
signed the following enrolled bill:

       H.R. 1079. An act to amend the Internal Revenue Code of 
     1986 to extend the funding and expenditure authority of the 
     Airport and Airway Trust Fund, to amend title 49, United 
     States Code, to extend the airport improvement program, and 
     for other purposes.

  The enrolled bill was subsequently signed by the President pro 
tempore (Mr. Inouye).
```
