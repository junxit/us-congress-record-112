# ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 31, 2011

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                  ORDERS FOR THURSDAY, MARCH 31, 2011

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 9:30 a.m. on Thursday,

[[Page S2001]]

March 31; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day, and the Senate proceed to a period of morning business, with 
Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each, with the 
first hour equally divided and controlled between the two leaders or 
their designees, with the majority controlling the first 30 minutes and 
the Republicans controlling the second 30 minutes.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
