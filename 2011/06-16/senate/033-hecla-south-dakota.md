# HECLA, SOUTH DAKOTA

**Speaking:** Thune, John (T000250) [R-SD]

```
                          HECLA, SOUTH DAKOTA

● Mr. THUNE. Mr. President, today I recognize Hecla, SD. The 
town of Hecla will celebrate its 125th anniversary this year.
  Located in Brown County, Hecla was founded in 1886 and was named 
after a volcano in Iceland. Today Hecla is known for its excellent 
hunting, abundant bird watching opportunities, and friendly atmosphere.
  Hecla has been a successful and thriving community for the past 125 
years, and I am confident that it will continue to serve as an example 
of South Dakota values and traditions. I would like to offer my 
congratulations to the citizens of Hecla on this landmark occasion and 
wish them continued prosperity in the years to come.●
```
