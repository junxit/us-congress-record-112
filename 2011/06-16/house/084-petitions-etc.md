# PETITIONS, ETC.

**Measures:** H.R. 1231, H.R. 1555

```
                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, petitions and papers were laid on the 
Clerk's desk and referred as follows:

       9. The SPEAKER presented a petition of California State 
     Lands Commission, California, relative to a resolution 
     opposing the enactment of H.R. 1231; to the Committee on 
     Natural Resources.
       10. Also, a petition of Town of Cambria, New York, relative 
     to a resolution opposing H.R. 1555; to the Committee on 
     Homeland Security.
       11. Also, a petition of American Bar Association, Illinois, 
     relative to a resolution supporting the development and use 
     of evidence-based, clinical, or medical practice guidelines 
     or standards regarding patient care and safety; jointly to 
     the Committees on Energy and Commerce and the Judiciary.
```
