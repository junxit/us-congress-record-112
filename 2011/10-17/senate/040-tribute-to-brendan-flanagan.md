# TRIBUTE TO BRENDAN FLANAGAN

```
                      TRIBUTE TO BRENDAN FLANAGAN

● Mr. RUBIO. Mr. President, today I recognize Brendan Flanagan, 
a summer law extern in my Washington, DC office for all of the hard 
work he has done for me, my staff, and the people of the State of 
Florida.
  Brendan is a graduate of Florida State University in Tallahassee, FL, 
where he majored in political science. Currently, he is entering his 
second year at John Marshall Law School. He is a dedicated and diligent 
worker who has been devoted to getting the most out of his internship 
experience.
  I would like to extend my sincere thanks and appreciation to Brendan 
for all the fine work he has done and wish him continued success in the 
years to come.●

                          ____________________
```
