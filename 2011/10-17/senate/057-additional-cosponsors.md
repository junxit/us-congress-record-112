                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 165

  At the request of Mr. Vitter, the name of the Senator from Missouri 
(Mr. Blunt) was added as a cosponsor of S. 165, a bill to amend the 
Public Health Services Act to prohibit certain abortion-related 
discrimination in governmental activities.

(Mr. Moran) was added as a cosponsor of S. 504, a bill to preserve and 
protect the free choice of individual employees to form, join, or 
assist labor organizations, or to refrain from such activities.

other purposes.

with Alzheimer's disease and related dementias by improving detection,
