# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following leader remarks, there will be a 
period of morning business until 4:30 p.m., with Senators permitted to 
speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  At 4:30 p.m. the Senate will proceed to executive session to consider 
Calendar No. 10, the nomination of James Boasberg, of the District of 
Columbia, to be a U.S. district judge for the District of Columbia. 
There will be up to 1 hour of debate, equally divided, prior to a vote 
on that nomination.
  Senators should expect two rollcall votes at 5:30. They will be in 
relation to the confirmation of James Boasberg and cloture on the 
motion to proceed to S. 493, the Small Business Reauthorization Act of 
2011.
  The current continuing resolution expires this Friday. We expect the 
House to send us a 3-week CR on Tuesday or Wednesday. We hope we can 
work out an agreement to consider the bill before the end of this week.

                          ____________________
```
