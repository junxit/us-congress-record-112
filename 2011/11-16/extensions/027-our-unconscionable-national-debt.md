# OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

**Speaking:** Coffman, Mike (C001077) [R-CO]

```
                    OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, November 16, 2011

  Mr. COFFMAN of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, today for the first time our 
national debt has surpassed $15 trillion dollars. Currently, our 
national debt is $15,033,607,255,920.32.
  On January 6, 2009, the start of the 111th Congress, the national 
debt was $10,638,425,746,293.80.
  This means the national debt has increased by $4,395,181,509,626.52 
since then. This debt and its interest payments we are passing to our 
children and all future Americans.
```
