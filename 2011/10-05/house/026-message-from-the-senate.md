# MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

**Measures:** H.R. 771, H.R. 1632

```
                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Curtis, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment bills of the House of the 
following titles:

       H.R. 771. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 1081 Elbel Road in Schertz, 
     Texas, as the ``Schertz Veterans Post Office''.
       H.R. 1632. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 5014 Gary Avenue in Lubbock, 
     Texas, as the ``Sergeant Chris Davis Post Office''.

                          ____________________
```
