# A BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

```
                   A BILL PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Karen L. Haas, Clerk of the House reports that on February 23, 2011 
she presented to the President of the United States, for his approval, 
the following bill.

       H.R. 514. To extend expiring provisions of the USA PATRIOT 
     Improvement and Reauthorization Act of 2005 and Intelligence 
     Reform and Terrorism Prevention Act of 2004 relating to 
     access to business records, individual terrorists as agents 
     of foreign powers, and roving wiretaps until December 8, 
     2011.

                          ____________________
```
