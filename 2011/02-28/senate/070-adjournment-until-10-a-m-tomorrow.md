# ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

**Measures:** S.Res. 78

```
                   ADJOURNMENT UNTIL 10 A.M. TOMORROW

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, if there is no further business to come 
before the Senate, I ask unanimous consent that it adjourn under the 
previous order and under the provisions of S. Res. 78 as a further mark 
of respect to the late former Senator James McClure.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 7:02 p.m., adjourned until 
Tuesday, March 1, 2011, at 10 a.m.
```
