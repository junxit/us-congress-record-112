# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 1, 2011

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, MARCH 1, 2011

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 10 a.m. tomorrow, 
Tuesday, March 1; that following the prayer and the pledge, the Journal 
of proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks there be a period of morning 
business for 1 hour, with Senators permitted to speak therein for up to 
10 minutes each, with the time equally divided and controlled between 
the two leaders or their designees, with the majority controlling the 
first half and the Republicans controlling the second half; further 
that the Senate recess from 12:30 to 2:15 to allow for the weekly party 
caucus lunches; finally, following morning business, the Senate resume 
consideration of the Patent Reform Act of 2011.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
