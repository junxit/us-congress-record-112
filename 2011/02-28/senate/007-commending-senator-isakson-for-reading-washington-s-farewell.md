# COMMENDING SENATOR ISAKSON FOR READING WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

```
  COMMENDING SENATOR ISAKSON FOR READING WASHINGTON'S FAREWELL ADDRESS

  Mr. McCONNELL. First, let me congratulate the Senator from Georgia 
for his excellent presentation of George Washington's Farewell Address.

for his reading of that for all of us on this important occasion.
```
