# ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 28, 2011

**Speaking:** Reid, Harry (R000146) [D-NV]

```
                   ORDERS FOR TUESDAY, JUNE 28, 2011

  Mr. REID. I now ask unanimous consent that when the Senate completes 
its business today, it adjourn until tomorrow morning, Tuesday, June 
28, at 10 a.m.; that following the prayer and pledge, the Journal of 
proceedings be approved to date, the morning hour be deemed expired, 
and the time for the two leaders be reserved for their use later in the 
day; that following any leader remarks, the Senate proceed to executive 
session under the previous order; and that following disposition of the 
Cole, Seitz, and Monaco nominations, the Senate recess until 2:15 p.m. 
for the weekly caucus meetings; finally, that at 2:15 p.m., the Senate 
resume consideration of the Presidential Appointment Efficiency and 
Streamlining Act.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
