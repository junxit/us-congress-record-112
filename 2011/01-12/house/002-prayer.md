# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  With the psalmist, we pray for this Congress and the Nation:
  ``Come, let us bless the Lord at all times. Let praise always be on 
our lips. If the Lord is your boast, be humble, listen and be moved.
  Glorify the Lord with me. Together let us reverence the Holy Name. 
When in difficulty, we looked for the Lord. Then from all our terrors 
we were set free.
  When most vulnerable, we called out to the Lord, and we were rescued 
from all our difficulties.
  So reverence the Lord with all the saints, for those who revere the 
Lord will lack no blessing.''
  In the midst of everything, let us seek the Lord both now and 
forever. Amen.

```
