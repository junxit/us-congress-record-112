# Daily Digest/Senate Committee Meetings

```
Committee Meetings
(Committees not listed did not meet)
NOMINATIONS
Committee on Finance: Committee concluded a hearing to examine the 
nominations of Joseph H. Gale, of Virginia, to be a Judge of the United 
States Tax Court, Michael W. Punke, of Montana, to be a Deputy United 
States Trade Representative, with the Rank of Ambassador, and Islam A. 
Siddiqui, of Virginia, to be Chief Agricultural Negotiator, Office of 
the United States Trade Representative, with the rank of Ambassador, 
both of the Executive Office of the President, Paul Piquado, of the 
District of Columbia, to be Assistant Secretary of Commerce, and David 
S. Johanson, of Texas, to be a Member of the United States 
International Trade Commission, who was introduced by Senators Cornyn 
and Grassley, after the nominees testified and answered questions in 
their own behalf.
```
