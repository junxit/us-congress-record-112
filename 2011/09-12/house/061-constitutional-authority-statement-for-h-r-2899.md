# Constitutional Authority Statement for H.R. 2899

**Measures:** H.R. 2899

```
           By Mr. ROHRABACHER:
       H.R. 2899.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 4 of the US Constitution: ``To 
     establish an uniform Rule of Naturalization, and uniform Laws 
     on the subject of Bankruptcies throughout the United 
     States.''
```
