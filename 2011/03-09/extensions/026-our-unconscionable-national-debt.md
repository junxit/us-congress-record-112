# OUR UNCONSCIONABLE NATIONAL DEBT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE COFFMAN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 9, 2011

  Mr. COFFMAN of Colorado. Mr. Speaker, today our national debt is 
$14,193,176,753,471.62.
