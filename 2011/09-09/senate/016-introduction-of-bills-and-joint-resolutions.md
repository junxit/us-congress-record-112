# INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

```
              INTRODUCTION OF BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS

  The following bills and joint resolutions were introduced, read the 
first and second times by unanimous consent, and referred as indicated:

           By Mr. SCHUMER (for himself, Mr. Casey, and Mrs. 
             McCaskill):
       S. 1536. A bill to require disclosure of the physical 
     location of business agents engaging in customer service 
     communications, and for other purposes; to the Committee on 
     Commerce, Science, and Transportation.
           By Mr. INOUYE (for himself, Mr. Schumer, and Mrs. 
             Gillibrand):
       S. 1537. A bill to authorize the Secretary of the Interior 
     to accept from the Board of Directors of the National 
     September 11 Memorial and Museum at the World Trade Center 
     Foundation, Inc., the donation of title to The National 
     September 11 Memorial and Museum at the World Trade Center, 
     and for other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and 
     Natural Resources.
```
