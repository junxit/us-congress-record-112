# SCHEDULE

```
                                SCHEDULE

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, following any leader remarks, the Senate 
will be in morning business. During that period of time, Senators will 
be allowed to speak for up to 10 minutes each. There will be no votes 
today.
  I am working with the House to find out what they can work with us 
on. We know we have the FAA bill, which expires next Friday. The 
highway bill expires at the end of this month. Those are the two 
primary issues we are working on, in addition to the FEMA problems we 
have in Virginia and around the rest of the country. We are going to 
make a decision today on what we will move forward on, but I need to 
hear from the House first.
```
