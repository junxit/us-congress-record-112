# ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 7, 2011

```
                    ORDERS FOR MONDAY, MARCH 7, 2011

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that when the Senate 
completes its business today, it adjourn until 2 p.m. on Monday, March 
7; that following the prayer and pledge,

[[Page 3354]]

the morning hour be deemed expired; the time for the two leaders be 
reserved for use later in the day; that following any leader remarks 
there be a period of morning business until 4:30 p.m., with Senators 
permitted to speak therein for up to 10 minutes each; at 4:30 p.m., the 
Senate proceed to executive session as under the previous order; and, 
finally, the filing deadline for second-degree amendments to S. 23 be 5 
p.m. on Monday, March 7.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
