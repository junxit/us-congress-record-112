# MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

**Measures:** H.R. 362, H.R. 1423

```
                         MESSAGE FROM THE HOUSE

  At 10:04 a.m., a message from the House of Representatives, delivered 
by Mrs. Cole, one of its reading clerks, announced that the House has 
passed the following bills, in which it requests the concurrence of the 
Senate:

       H.R. 362. An act to redesignate the Federal building and 
     United States Courthouse located at 200 East Wall Street in 
     Midland, Texas, as the ``George H.W. Bush and George W. Bush 
     United States Courthouse and George Mahon Federal Building''.
       H.R. 1423. An act to designate the facility of the United 
     States Postal Service located at 115 4th Avenue Southwest in 
     Ardmore, Oklahoma, as the ``Specialist Micheal E. Phillips 
     Post Office''.

                          ____________________
```
