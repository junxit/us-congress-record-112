# COME TOGETHER

```
                             COME TOGETHER

  (Mr. McCOTTER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McCOTTER. Mr. Speaker, the administration and the Democratic 
Senate, in their posturing, show what America knows: We are being 
neither

[[Page 12532]]

led nor governed. Yet in their policy irresponsibility, they remain 
united in their party unity.
  On the right, take note: It is as unwarranted and injurious for a 
Republican to call a Tea Partier a hobbit as it is for a Tea Partier to 
call a Republican a RINO. We cannot unite America if we divide the 
movement. Consequently, the time has come for the Tea Party to grow up 
and the Republican Party to wake up and come together to serve and save 
this great Nation.

                          ____________________
```
