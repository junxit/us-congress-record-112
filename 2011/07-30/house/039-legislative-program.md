# LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

**Speaking:** Hoyer, Steny H. (H000874) [D-MD], Cantor, Eric (C001046) [R-VA]

```
                          LEGISLATIVE PROGRAM

  (Mr. HOYER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. HOYER. Madam Speaker, I yield to my friend, the majority leader, 
for the purpose of asking about the schedule for the rest of the 
weekend and the upcoming week.
  Mr. CANTOR. I thank the gentleman from Maryland.
  Madam Speaker, the House will now meet in pro forma session only 
tomorrow. Therefore, no votes are expected in the House on Sunday. 
Given the critical fiscal and economic situation, however, Members 
should be prepared to return to Washington quickly if needed. We may 
only be able to assure a few hours' notice, at most.
  Lastly, I would say to the gentleman that the House will be in 
legislative session on Monday, and first votes are expected as early as 
noon.
  Mr. HOYER. I thank the gentleman for his information, and I yield 
back the balance of my time.

                          ____________________
```
