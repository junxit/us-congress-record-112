# prayer

```
                                 prayer

  The Chaplain, Dr. Barry C. Black, offered the following prayer:
  Let us pray.
  Eternal God, great is Your power, and Your understanding is infinite. 
We need You on Capitol Hill. As we gather this Saturday, a nation looks 
to our government's legislative branch for responsible action. Deliver 
our lawmakers from the paralysis of analysis when constructive and 
prompt action is desperately needed. Faced with potentially disastrous 
consequences, give the Members of this body the wisdom to work while it 
is day, for the night comes, when no one can work.
  We pray in Your great Name. Amen.

                          ____________________
```
