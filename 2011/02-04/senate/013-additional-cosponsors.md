# ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS

```
                         ADDITIONAL COSPONSORS


                                 S. 228

  At the request of Mr. Barrasso, the names of the Senator from South 
Carolina (Mr. DeMint) and the Senator from Idaho (Mr. Risch) were added 
as cosponsors of S. 228, a bill to preempt regulation of, action 
relating to, or consideration of greenhouse gases under Federal and 
common law on enactment of a Federal policy to mitigate climate change.


                            AMENDMENT NO. 29

  At the request of Mr. Nelson of Nebraska, the names of the Senator 
from New Jersey (Mr. Menendez) and the Senator from Montana (Mr. 
Tester) were added as cosponsors of amendment No. 29 intended to be 
proposed to S. 223, a bill to modernize the air traffic control system, 
improve the safety, reliability, and availability of transportation by 
air in the United States, provide modernization of the air traffic 
control system, reauthorize the Federal Aviation Administration, and 
for other purposes.
```
