# PRAYER

```
                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Daniel P. Coughlin, offered the following 
prayer:
  Lord God, Your loving and sustaining presence breaks through certain 
moments of time. You enlighten Your people to take the next step and 
make the necessary decisions that will lead them through the maze of 
present needs.
  Guide the Members of the House of Representatives, that priorities 
will reflect the full promise of Your compassion for those most in 
need, build greater justice, and secure the path of peace in this 
fragile and complex world.
  Lord, be with us now and years to come.
  Amen.

                          ____________________
```
