# Text of House Amendment 24

**Speaking:** Farenthold, Blake (F000460) [R-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                       Offered By: Mr. Farenthold

       Amendment No. 24: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


  funding limitation related to speed limit reduction in padre island 
                           national seashore

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used issue a preliminary rule or a final rule, or to take 
     any other action to reduce the legal speed limit in Padre 
     Island National Seashore.
```
