# REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1938, NORTH AMERICAN-MADE ENERGY SECURITY ACT

**Measures:** H.Res. 370, H.R. 1938

```
 REPORT ON RESOLUTION PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1938, NORTH 
                   AMERICAN-MADE ENERGY SECURITY ACT

  Mr. WEBSTER, from the Committee on Rules, submitted a privileged 
report (Rept. No. 112-181) on the resolution (H. Res. 370) providing 
for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1938) to direct the President to 
expedite the consideration and approval of the construction and 
operation of the Keystone XL oil pipeline, and for other purposes, 
which was referred to the House Calendar and ordered to be printed.
```
