# Text of House Amendment 4

**Speaking:** Amash, Justin (A000367) [R-MI]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                         Offered By: Mr. Amash

       Amendment No. 4: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


  funding limitation for national capital area performing arts program

       Sec. __. None of the amounts made available in the Act may 
     be used for the National Capital Area Performing Arts 
     Program.
```
