# Text of House Amendment 20

**Speaking:** Gosar, Paul A. (G000565) [R-AZ]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                         Offered By: Mr. Gosar

       Amendment No. 20: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


         funding limitation related to border patrol activities

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available under this Act 
     may be used to enforce any regulation that would impede or 
     obstruct the United States Border Patrol from patrol 
     activities on Federal lands.
```
