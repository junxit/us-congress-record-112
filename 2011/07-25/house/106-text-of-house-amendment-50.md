# Text of House Amendment 50

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 50: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


                           funding limitation

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used in contravention of National Historic Preservation 
     Act (16 U.S.C. 470) or the Omnibus Parks and Public Lands 
     Management Act of 1996 (Public Law 104-333).
```
