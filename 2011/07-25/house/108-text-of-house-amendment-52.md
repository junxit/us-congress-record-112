# Text of House Amendment 52

**Speaking:** Jackson Lee, Sheila (J000032) [D-TX]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                  Offered By: Ms. Jackson Lee of Texas

       Amendment No. 52: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


                           funding limitation

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used in contravention of sections 405(b) or 410(b) of 
     Public Law 101-593.
```
