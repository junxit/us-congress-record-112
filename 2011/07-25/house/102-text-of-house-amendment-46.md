# Text of House Amendment 46

**Speaking:** Posey, Bill (P000599) [R-FL]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                         Offered By: Mr. Posey

       Amendment No. 46: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title) add the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used by the Department of the Interior for any new oil or 
     gas preleasing, leasing, drilling, or related activities 
     using facilities that are visible from shore for any area of 
     the Outer Continental Shelf located within 25 miles of the 
     State of Florida.
```
