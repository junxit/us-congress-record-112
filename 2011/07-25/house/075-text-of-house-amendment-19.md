# Text of House Amendment 19

**Speaking:** Landry, Jeffrey M. (L000574) [R-LA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                         Offered By: Mr. Landry

       Amendment No. 19: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available in this Act may 
     be used to pay the salary of individuals appointed to their 
     current position through, or otherwise carry out, paragraph 
     (1), (2), or (3) of section 5503(a) of title 5, United States 
     Code.
```
