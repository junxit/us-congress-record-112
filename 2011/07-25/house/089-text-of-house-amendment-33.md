# Text of House Amendment 33

**Speaking:** Scott, Austin (S001189) [R-GA]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                Offered By: Mr. Austin Scott of Georgia

       Amendment No. 33: At the end of the bill (before the short
```
