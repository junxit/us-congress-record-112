# Text of House Amendment 15

**Speaking:** Berg, Rick (B001272) [R-ND]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                          Offered By: Mr. Berg

       Amendment No. 15: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to promulgate, implement, administer, or enforce a 
     Federal implementation plan under the Clean Air Act (42 
     U.S.C. 7401 et seq.) that imposes any standard or requirement 
     to address regional haze pursuant to subpart P of part 51 of 
     title 40, Code of Federal Regulations (relating to protection 
     of visibility).
```
