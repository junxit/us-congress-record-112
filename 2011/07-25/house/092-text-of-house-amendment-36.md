# Text of House Amendment 36

**Speaking:** Huelskamp, Tim (H001057) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                       Offered By: Mr. Huelskamp

       Amendment No. 36: At the end of the bill, before the short 
     title, insert the following:


     funding limitation related to the heritage partnership program

       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used for the Heritage Partnership Program.
```
