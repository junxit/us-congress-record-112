# Text of House Amendment 40

**Speaking:** Pompeo, Mike (P000602) [R-KS]

**Measures:** H.R. 2584

```
H.R. 2584

                         Offered By: Mr. Pompeo

       Amendment No. 40: At the end of the bill (before the short 
     title), insert the following:
       Sec. __.  None of the funds made available by this Act may 
     be used to enforce the requirements of section 211(o) of the 
     Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. 7545(o)) against a refiner (as 
     defined in section 80.1142(a)(1) of title 40, Code of Federal 
     Regulations) for operations conducted in 2012 fiscal year.
```
