# RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

```
                RECESS SUBJECT TO THE CALL OF THE CHAIR

  Mr. REID. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the Senate now 
stand in recess subject to the call of the Chair.
  There being no objection, the Senate, at 6 p.m., recessed subject to 
the call of the Chair and reassembled at 7:21 p.m. when called to order 
by the Presiding Officer (Mr. Blumenthal).
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The majority leader is recognized.
  Mr. REID. Mr. President, thank you very much for your patience and 
also for being willing to be here when most are doing other things.

                          ____________________
```
