# URGING EUROPEAN UNION TO DESIGNATE HIZBALLAH AS A TERRORIST ORGANIZATION

```
      URGING EUROPEAN UNION TO DESIGNATE HIZBALLAH AS A TERRORIST 
                              ORGANIZATION

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution (H. Res. 834) 
urging the governments of Europe and the European Union to designate 
Hizballah as a terrorist organization and impose sanctions, and urging 
the President to provide information about Hizballah to the European 
allies of the United States and to support the Government of Bulgaria 
in investigating the July 18, 2012, terrorist attack in Burgas.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from Florida (Ms. Ros-Lehtinen) that the House suspend the 
rules and agree to the resolution.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
