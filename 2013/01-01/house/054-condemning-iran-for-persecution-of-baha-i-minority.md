# CONDEMNING IRAN FOR PERSECUTION OF BAHA'I MINORITY

```
           CONDEMNING IRAN FOR PERSECUTION OF BAHA'I MINORITY

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the question on 
suspending the rules and agreeing to the resolution (H. Res. 134) 
condemning the Government of Iran for its state-sponsored persecution 
of its Baha'i minority and its continued violation of the International 
Covenants on Human Rights, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from Florida (Ms. Ros-Lehtinen) that the House suspend the 
rules and agree to the resolution, as amended.
  The question was taken; and (two-thirds being in the affirmative) the 
rules were suspended and the resolution, as amended, was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.

                          ____________________
```
