# TRIBUTE TO TONY HANAGAN AND KEIRA HARRIS

```
                TRIBUTE TO TONY HANAGAN AND KEIRA HARRIS

  Mr. McCONNELL. Mr. President, Tony Hanagan and Keira Harris are

two former pages who returned to the Senate, graciously volunteering to 
sacrifice some of their Christmas vacation to help here on the Senate
```
