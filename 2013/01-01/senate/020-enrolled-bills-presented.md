# ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

```
                        ENROLLED BILLS PRESENTED

  The Secretary of the Senate reported that on today, January 1, 2013, 
she had presented to the President of the United States the following 
enrolled bills:

       S. 3202. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     ensure that deceased veterans with no known next of kin can 
     receive a dignified burial, and for other purposes.
       S. 3666. An act to amend the Animal Welfare Act to modify 
     the definition of ``exhibitor''.
```
