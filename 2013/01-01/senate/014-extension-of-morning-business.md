# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. MERKLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the period for 
morning business for debate only be extended until 6 p.m., with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  Mr. MERKLEY. Mr. President, I suggest the absence of a quorum.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. The clerk will call the roll.
  The legislative clerk proceeded to call the roll.
  Mr. MERKLEY. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that the order 
for the quorum call be rescinded.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
