# EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

```
                     EXTENSION OF MORNING BUSINESS

  Mr. CONRAD. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent the period for 
morning business be extended until 3 p.m. for debate only, with 
Senators permitted to speak for up to 10 minutes each.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.
  The Senator from Alabama is recognized.
  Mr. SESSIONS. Mr. President, I ask unanimous consent that I be 
allowed to use as much time as I may consume.
  The PRESIDING OFFICER. Without objection, it is so ordered.

                          ____________________
```
